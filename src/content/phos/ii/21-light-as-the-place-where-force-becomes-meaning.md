Force alone produces movement or change. Meaning arises when that force enters an interior field capable of sensation, memory, comparison, imagination, and interpretation.

The soul is therefore the place where force becomes meaning.

## The passage inward

A physical event reaches the senses. The etheric body registers its effect upon vitality. The astral body experiences attraction, pain, pleasure, fear, or desire. The psychic layers relate the event to memory and identity. Intelligence discovers patterns. Spirit may orient the experience toward a greater purpose.

Light is the thread making these transitions perceptible. It carries the event inward, reveals its relations, and allows the soul to form an image of what has occurred.

Meaning is not contained entirely within the incoming force or invented entirely by the receiver. It emerges between them.

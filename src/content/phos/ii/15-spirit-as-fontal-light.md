The word *fontal* means source-like or fountain-like. Spirit is fontal light because it participates nearest to the animating source from which life, intelligence, and orientation proceed.

Spirit does not merely possess brightness. It gives direction toward a greater order. Its luminosity is known through animation, conscience, intelligence, aspiration, vitality of purpose, and the capacity to exceed purely reactive existence.

## Pneuma and Nous

*Pneuma* can be understood as breath, spirit, or animating current. *Nous* names the higher capacity for intelligible apprehension. Neither should be reduced to ordinary thought or emotion.

Spirit is not the personality idealising itself. Fontal light does not belong to the ego as private property. It is received participation in something greater than the finite vessel.

Spirit illuminates by awakening the being toward its highest possible relation.

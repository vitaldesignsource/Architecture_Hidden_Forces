The native doctrine of light within The Architecture of Hidden Forces can now be stated.

Light is the activity of disclosure through which hidden force becomes distinguishable and form becomes capable of perception. It proceeds from a source without exhausting that source. It enters Root Ether as primordial receptivity and passes through the Fourfold Veil, where Warmth awakens, Light differentiates, Tone orders, and Life integrates.

Within Morphaithēr, luminous activity receives tattvic bias. Ākāśa opens, Vāyu moves, Tejas transforms, Apas coheres, and Pṛthivī stabilises. The gunas condition the transparency and motion of the field: sattva clarifies, rajas activates, and tamas condenses.

Spirit receives light fontally. Essence gives it distinctive orientation. Soul interprets it through sensation, image, desire, memory, and meaning. The etheric body organises it as living continuity. The astral body experiences it as felt and imaginal motion. The physical body gives it boundary and action. CHON provides the material alphabet through which biological form is written.

Ignisophia gathers light into transformative fire. The Psychic Flywheel accumulates directed luminous momentum. The Law of Right Relation governs whether transmission becomes nourishing, distorting, or destructive. Heka and Logos give luminous force articulate expression.

The Crypt of Primordial Memory retains the curvature produced by former illuminations. The Ossuary of Living Forms receives the remains of completed embodiments. Spiritualisation allows matter to become increasingly capable of carrying meaning without ceasing to be matter.

## The complete native sequence

> Source → Aphanic Light → Root Ether → Fourfold Veil → Morphaithēr → Tattvic Bias → Gunic Condition → Spirit → Soul → Etheric Formation → Astral Experience → Bodily Embodiment → Meaning → Memory → Return

## The governing propositions

- Form is frozen force. Force is liberated form.
- CHON is the material alphabet through which formative force writes biological form.
- Warmth awakens. Light differentiates. Tone orders. Life integrates.
- Spirit animates. Essence determines. Soul mediates. Body embodies.
- No force can be judged solely by its nature; it must also be judged by the relations through which it becomes active.

Light is not the whole of the architecture. It is the principle through which the architecture becomes visible to itself.

It reveals force, travels through mediation, awakens form, enters the soul as meaning, becomes embodied through matter, and leaves its trace within the memory of the world.

Rajas is the principle of motion, intensity, striving, expansion, friction, and change. It gives light urgency and gives force the momentum required to overcome inertia.

Without rajas, illumination might remain contemplative without becoming operative. Rajas directs the ray, intensifies the flame, activates desire, and drives the movement from possibility toward accomplishment.

## The moving field

Yet rajasic light is unstable. It flashes, pursues, stimulates, and multiplies. Within the psyche it can produce fascination, ambition, anxiety, visionary excitement, and rapid symbolic association. Its brightness may be mistaken for clarity because it generates powerful experience.

Agitated radiance does not necessarily reveal falsely, but it rarely reveals completely. The moving field disturbs its own image.

Rajas must therefore be given direction through will, proportion through Tone, and integration through Life. When rightly ordered, it becomes transformative momentum. When disordered, it becomes scattered fire.

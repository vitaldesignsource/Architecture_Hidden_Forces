Force in complete abstraction has not yet acquired a definite mode of expression. As it enters Morphaithēr, tattvic coloration biases it toward particular movements, relations, textures, and possible forms.

Ākāśic light opens space and establishes resonance. Vāyavic light moves, circulates, and communicates. Tejas-light differentiates, heats, clarifies, and transforms. Apasic light reflects, joins, nourishes, and adapts. Pṛthivī-light condenses, stabilises, defines, and gives endurance.

## Not a fixed chart

These descriptions should not be mistaken for a fixed chart in which every visible colour or event possesses one unavoidable tattvic meaning. A red object is not automatically Tejas, nor is every blue phenomenon automatically Apas. Tattvic coloration concerns the behaviour and formative tendency of the current, not merely its sensory appearance.

Complex forms arise through mixtures. A living organism may require Pṛthivī for boundary, Apas for circulation, Tejas for metabolism, Vāyu for exchange, and Ākāśa for systemic openness. What distinguishes one organism or event is not the presence of only one tattva but the proportion, rhythm, hierarchy, and relation among them.

Tattvic coloration is the beginning of differentiated character.

Ignisophia is one transformative fire appearing through six faces.

**Discernment** separates true light from glare, source from reflection, and necessity from impulse.

**Necessity** burns away what can no longer remain if transformation is to proceed.

**Truth** exposes the actual condition of the vessel without ornament or evasion.

**Initiation** crosses the threshold after which knowledge becomes lived responsibility.

**Silence** contains the fire so that it can deepen rather than dissipate through premature expression.

**Longing** provides the eros that draws the soul toward a form of being it has not yet embodied.

## One fire

These are not six different fires. They are six operations of the same fire of wisdom. Discernment directs it. Necessity intensifies it. Truth clarifies it. Initiation embodies it. Silence contains it. Longing renews its movement.

Together they prevent the Inner Sun from becoming either passive contemplation or uncontrolled conflagration.

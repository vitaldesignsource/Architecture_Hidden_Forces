The Law of Right Relation states:

> No force can be judged solely by its nature; it must also be judged by the relations through which it becomes active.

A light may be beneficial in one proportion and destructive in another. The same revelation may liberate a prepared receiver and overwhelm an unprepared one. A truthful disclosure can become harmful when delivered without timing, care, context, or legitimate purpose.

Right relation — *orthē schesis* — is the dynamic proportion that preserves distinction while enabling participation.

## Five terms

Every luminous act should therefore be examined through five terms:

> Source — Force — Intermediary — Vessel — Field

Is the source what it claims to be? What force is actually being transmitted? What intermediary is shaping the transmission? Can the vessel receive and integrate it? What larger field will be altered by the event?

Light becomes good, truthful, and transformative through right relation — not through brightness alone.

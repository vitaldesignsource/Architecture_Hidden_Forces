The relation among spirit, soul, and body is governed by the established sequence:

> Spirit animates. Essence determines. Soul mediates. Body embodies.

Spirit is participation in a greater life and intelligence. Essence gives a being its distinctive pattern and direction. Soul is the interior field through which force becomes sensation, image, desire, memory, identity, and meaning. Body is the concrete, situated form through which these relations become capable of action.

## One movement, three registers

Light appears differently within each. In spirit, light is fontal and animating. In soul, it is receptive, interpretive, imaginative, and reflective. In body, it becomes bounded, sensory, rhythmic, biochemical, and operative.

These are not separate lights sealed into compartments. They are registers of one luminous movement undergoing successive mediation.

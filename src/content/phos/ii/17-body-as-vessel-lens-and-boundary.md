A body is not an incidental container. It is an organised living whole and the concrete site at which forces become situated, limited, and capable of action.

As a vessel, the body receives and contains force. As a lens, it modifies what passes through it according to its structure and condition. As a boundary, it distinguishes an organism from its environment while still permitting exchange.

## Limitation makes expression possible

Every body reveals some possibilities and excludes others. Its boundaries make expression possible. Without limitation, force could not become this gesture, this voice, this life, or this act.

The body is therefore neither the enemy of light nor a prison from which light must simply escape. It is the architecture through which luminosity becomes bearable, relational, and effective.

Form is spirit made bearable through successive degrees of mediation.

The soul — *Psychē* — is the living intermediary of sensation, image, desire, memory, movement, perception, and inward organisation.

Soul receives light, but it never receives passively. It interprets according to its history, desires, wounds, expectations, symbols, and degree of development. It is simultaneously chamber, mirror, lens, prism, and translator.

This is why identical events can produce different meanings in different people. The incoming force is only one part of the event. The soul gives that force interior form.

## Conscious of its colouring

When ordered, the soul becomes a truthful mediator between spirit and body. When disordered, it may confuse its own coloration with the source. Psychic glamour, projection, inflation, and misinterpretation arise when the interpretive activity of the soul remains unrecognised.

The soul's task is not to become colourless. It is to become conscious of how it colours what it receives.

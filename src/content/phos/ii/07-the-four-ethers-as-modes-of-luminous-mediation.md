The Four Ethers describe four interdependent functions of formative mediation.

**Warmth Ether** awakens. It is the ether of ignition, animation, activation, and the threshold between potential and operative force. Luminous manifestation begins when possibility is quickened into activity.

**Light Ether** differentiates. It establishes orientation, contrast, direction, spatial articulation, and the conditions through which form becomes distinguishable. Light Ether should not be equated with photons. It names the formative function of differentiation for which visible light provides the clearest physical image.

**Tone Ether** orders. It establishes ratio, interval, resonance, chemical affinity, relational grammar, and proportion. Tone is the power through which differentiated elements enter meaningful arrangement rather than remaining scattered.

**Life Ether** integrates. It gathers differentiated and ordered relations into an enduring whole capable of continuity, repair, reproduction, and self-renewal.

Their condensed formula:

> Warmth awakens. Light differentiates. Tone orders. Life integrates.

## Maintained, not crossed once

These four functions can recur within a single process. A living form is continually reawakened, redifferentiated, reordered, and reintegrated. The Veil is not crossed once; it is maintained through rhythmic participation.

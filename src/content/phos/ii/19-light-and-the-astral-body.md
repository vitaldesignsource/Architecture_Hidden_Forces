The astral body is a field of felt vectors. It includes desire, sensation, emotional movement, attraction, repulsion, dream, image, and symbolic experience.

Astral light is mobile and image-bearing. It can intensify a memory, surround a desire with symbolic forms, transform bodily sensation into dream imagery, or translate an obscure psychic movement into an inward scene.

## Coloured by the gunas

Because it is highly responsive, astral luminosity is easily coloured by the gunas. Sattva makes the image transparent and coherent. Rajas makes it vivid, rapid, and unstable. Tamas makes it repetitive, heavy, obscured, or fixed.

Astral brightness should never be mistaken automatically for spiritual authority. An image can be brilliant because it carries tremendous desire or emotional charge.

The astral body is the theatre of luminous appearance, not the final judge of what appears.

The etheric body is the real vital-formative dimension of the embodied organism. It is the subtle body through which living form is generated, coordinated, repaired, and preserved across time. Physical anatomy supplies its material embodiment, while the etheric body supplies the organised vitality through which anatomy becomes and remains alive. It is the intermediary through which formative causality becomes rhythmic growth, repair, circulation, differentiation, and continuity.

Light within the etheric body is not necessarily visible. It appears as orientation, organisation, responsiveness, and the maintenance of living form through time.

## The Veil in the organism

The Fourfold Veil is especially relevant here. Warmth activates living processes. Light differentiates tissues, directions, and functions. Tone establishes proportion and relational order. Life integrates these activities into a self-maintaining whole.

The etheric body is therefore not a glowing duplicate of the physical body, nor a name for physiology. It is the real formative body through whose activity the organism continually becomes itself — ontologically prior to the physical processes that embody it, and not produced by them.

Its luminosity is organisational before it is visual.

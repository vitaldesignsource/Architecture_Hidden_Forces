The tattvas are not additional ethers. They are morphogenic biases within formative activity. They incline a current toward particular types of expression without absolutely determining its final form.

Within the system:

- **Ākāśa** inclines toward spaciousness, continuity, resonance, and receptive openness.
- **Vāyu** inclines toward movement, exchange, circulation, variation, and transmission.
- **Tejas** inclines toward ignition, radiance, distinction, metabolism, and transformation.
- **Apas** inclines toward cohesion, flow, reflection, adaptation, and continuity.
- **Pṛthivī** inclines toward stability, resistance, boundary, density, and enduring structure.

## Every ether, every bias

A given ether can carry different tattvic biases, and a tattvic quality can express itself through every ether. Tejas, for example, may appear as activating warmth, differentiating brilliance, ordering intensity, or transformative life activity.

Tattvas colour the manner in which light acts. They do not simply add visible colour to it. They give luminosity a formative preference.

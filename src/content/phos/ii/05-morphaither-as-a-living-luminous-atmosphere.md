Morphaithēr is called *living* because it is neither passive emptiness nor an indifferent channel. It is continually altered by what passes through it and by the forms that arise within it. Every organism, place, ritual, symbol, repeated behaviour, and historical event contributes to the atmosphere through which subsequent forces must move.

It is called *luminous* because it is a field of potential disclosure. Relations concealed at one level may become perceptible when the field is appropriately illuminated. Morphaithēr carries the possibility that force can become image, sensation, form, meaning, and memory.

It is called *formative* because the field biases manifestation toward some possibilities more readily than others. This does not mean that the future is mechanically predetermined. It means that manifestation has inherited conditions.

## A living weather of formation

Morphaithēr may therefore be imagined as a living weather of formation. Its currents are [[ii-8|tattvic]], its transparency is conditioned by the [[ii-10|gunas]], its transmissive organisation is articulated through the [[ii-6|Fourfold Veil]], and its local expressions are shaped by bodies, histories, and acts of participation.

Light reveals the atmosphere, but the atmosphere also determines how the light will appear.

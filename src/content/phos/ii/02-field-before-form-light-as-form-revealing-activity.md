The Field Before Form is the prior relational depth from which particular forms emerge. It is not a container filled with finished objects waiting to become visible. It is a field of potential orientation, resonance, resistance, proportion, memory, and becoming.

Light enters this field as a form-revealing activity. It does not necessarily create every relation it reveals, but it allows latent distinctions to become active and perceptible. Under illumination, boundaries emerge, directions become apparent, contrasts intensify, and possible configurations begin to separate from one another.

Light therefore participates in individuation. It helps transform an undifferentiated field into a structured environment in which one thing can appear in relation to another.

## Every illumination selects

Yet the Field Before Form always exceeds what any light reveals. Every illumination selects. A beam establishes a visible region while leaving another region obscure. A concept clarifies one relationship while temporarily excluding others. A symbol discloses one face of a mystery while preserving the rest in depth.

Light does not abolish the Field Before Form. It draws provisional architectures from it.

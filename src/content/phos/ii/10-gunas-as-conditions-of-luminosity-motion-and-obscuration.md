If tattvas describe the directional biases of formative activity, the gunas describe the condition under which that activity operates.

> Sattva clarifies and harmonises. Rajas activates and agitates. Tamas condenses and obstructs.

The gunas are not three moral categories dividing reality into good, bad, and worse. Every manifested process requires all three. Sattva makes relation transparent. Rajas supplies movement and transition. Tamas grants weight, rest, boundary, and persistence.

## Disproportion

Difficulty arises through disproportion. Excessive rajas scatters luminosity into agitation. Excessive tamas blocks or fixes what should continue moving. Sattva without sufficient embodiment may remain lucid but ineffective.

The gunas condition whether light becomes nourishing, distorted, unstable, or stagnant as it passes toward form.

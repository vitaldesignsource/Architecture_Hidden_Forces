Sattva is the condition in which a medium becomes sufficiently clear, coherent, and proportionate to transmit light with minimal distortion.

In the psyche, sattva appears as calm attention, emotional equilibrium, intellectual clarity, and receptivity free from compulsive projection. In ritual, it appears as order, cleanliness, proportion, ethical alignment, and concentration. In the body, it may be symbolised through balanced rhythms, responsiveness, and the absence of unnecessary obstruction.

Sattva does not generate the source. It makes participation in the source more faithful.

## Transparency without erasure

A sattvic vessel does not become invisible or lose all individuality. Its distinct form becomes transparent to the principle it serves. This is transparency without erasure.

The danger is treating sattva as passive purity. Genuine luminous transparency is active, attentive, and capable of embodiment. It does not retreat from the world merely to preserve its own clarity.

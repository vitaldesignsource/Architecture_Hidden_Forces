The Crypt of Primordial Memory is the retentive depth through which existence inherits the formative consequences of what has occurred.

It is not necessarily a celestial archive containing perfect recordings of every event. It is better understood as an altered topology of possibility. Past forms change the field. They make certain developments easier, others more resistant, and some no longer possible in precisely the same way.

## Residual intelligibility

Light within the Crypt is residual intelligibility. When a form disappears, the relations it established may continue to influence future formation. Its light remains not as a visible glow but as a trace that can be re-illuminated under appropriate conditions.

Memory is therefore inherited curvature. The past bends the paths available to the present.

The Crypt is luminous because nothing enters manifestation without leaving some consequence within the architecture through which future manifestation must proceed.

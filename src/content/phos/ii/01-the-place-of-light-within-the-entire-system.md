The Architecture of Hidden Forces begins from the proposition that visible things are not self-explanatory. Every formed thing is the temporary stabilisation of relations, forces, histories, boundaries, and mediating conditions that exceed its visible surface.

This is expressed through the governing formula:

> Form is frozen force. Force is liberated form.

Light occupies the interval between force and perceptibility. Force may operate invisibly, but light makes its action legible. It reveals the contours of what force has organised, exposes movement within the field, and allows a receiver to recognise that a relationship is present.

Light is therefore neither the sole origin nor the final product of manifestation. It is the activity of disclosure operating throughout the process. It accompanies the passage from hidden potential into differentiated relation, from relation into form, and from form into perception.

## Six primary terms

Within the complete system, light should be understood alongside five other primary terms:

- **Source** gives or originates.
- **Force** acts and produces change.
- **Ether** provides formability and transmission.
- **Light** differentiates and reveals.
- **Form** stabilises and embodies.
- **Consciousness** receives and interprets.

No single term replaces the others. Light without force would disclose without acting. Force without light could act without becoming intelligible. Ether without either would remain receptive potential. Form without continued relation would become inert abstraction. Consciousness without mediation would have nothing determinate to receive.

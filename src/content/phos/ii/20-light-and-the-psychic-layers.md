The psychic layers include memory, imagination, emotion, personal identity, inherited pattern, unconscious association, and the interior narratives through which experience becomes meaningful.

Light moves through these layers unevenly. Some memories are clearly available, while others remain obscured or fragmented. Some emotions illuminate genuine needs; others colour perception so strongly that the world becomes almost unrecognisable.

## Timing

Psychic illumination occurs when previously unconscious relations enter awareness without being immediately distorted or rejected. It may reveal why a pattern recurs, how an old experience continues to shape the present, or where desire has disguised itself as certainty.

The psychic layers do not merely hide truth. They also preserve material until consciousness becomes capable of encountering it.

The work of illumination must therefore respect timing. To expose is not always to heal. Light must arrive with sufficient warmth, proportion, and integration to be received.

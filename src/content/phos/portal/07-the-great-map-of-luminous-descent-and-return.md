The central map of the portal follows eight movements.

### Source

Source is light in its concentrated, fontal, or unmanifest condition. Within metaphysical traditions it represents the origin of intelligibility, life, consciousness, or divine presence. The source contains more than any single ray, image, colour, or manifestation can express.

### Procession

Procession is the movement from unity toward multiplicity. Light proceeds without the source necessarily being diminished. What is unified at its origin begins to acquire direction, relation, measure, and distinguishable operation.

### Mediation

No influence reaches manifestation without conditions of passage. Ethers, atmospheres, intermediary beings, symbols, words, organs, ritual structures, psychic faculties, natural media, and social forms may all operate as mediators within their appropriate domains. Mediation does not merely transport. It selects, translates, amplifies, attenuates, organises, or limits.

### Coloration

As light passes through a medium it acquires a particular signature. Within the system, [[ii-8|tattvas]] function as morphogenic biases that give direction and qualitative tendency to force. [[ii-10|Gunas]] condition luminosity through clarity, activity, or inertia. Psychic states add emotional and imaginal coloration. Cultural symbols determine how an illumination is understood and expressed.

Coloration is not necessarily corruption. Differentiation is required for manifestation. The danger begins when a conditioned colour mistakes itself for the total light.

### Embodiment

Light becomes established within form. It enters image, organism, symbol, doctrine, architecture, ritual, personality, work, relationship, or material structure. Embodiment gives light duration and operative presence, but it also limits and localises it.

A truthful form transmits more than it imprisons. A failing form retains the appearance of illumination after it has ceased to communicate the force that originally animated it.

### Perception

Every illumination is received through a capacity. Eye, body, imagination, memory, intellect, soul, and culture all participate in perception. What is received is therefore neither produced entirely by the observer nor encountered without mediation.

The same illumination may awaken one person, overwhelm another, and pass unnoticed by a third. This is the principle of [[xix-2|differential reception]].

### Transformation

True illumination changes the architecture of the receiver. It does more than provide information or emotional intensity. It reorganises perception, relation, conduct, meaning, or form. If nothing changes except the self-image of the observer, illumination may have been admired without being integrated.

### Return

Return is the reorientation of differentiated light toward its source and governing truth. It does not require the destruction of individuality. It requires that each form cease treating its partial illumination as self-originating.

Return may take the form of recollection, purification, integration, sacrifice, release, contemplation, death, or the transmutation of an exhausted form into a more truthful vessel. It is the luminous dimension of *epistrophē*: the turning back through which what has proceeded rediscovers its principle.

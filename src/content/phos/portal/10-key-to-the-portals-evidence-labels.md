Each article carries visible labels identifying the kind of knowledge it contains.

**Primary Text** indicates a direct passage from an ancient, medieval, religious, philosophical, scientific, ritual, or esoteric source. Translation and textual uncertainties are noted where relevant.

**Historical Doctrine** identifies an interpretation grounded in the documented teachings of a particular person, school, culture, or period.

**Scientific Evidence** marks information supported by established observation, experimentation, or current scientific scholarship.

**Phenomenological Report** identifies a description of lived or reported experience without claiming that one interpretation has been conclusively established.

**Architecture of Hidden Forces Doctrine** marks principles belonging specifically to this system, including its vocabulary, models, laws, and metaphysical synthesis.

**Comparative Parallel** identifies a meaningful resemblance between traditions while preserving their historical and doctrinal differences.

**Speculative Extension** marks a hypothesis or creative development that remains open to examination, refinement, rejection, or future support.

## Degree of confidence

Claims may also carry a degree-of-confidence marker:

- **Established** — strongly supported within the relevant field.
- **Probable** — supported by substantial evidence but not certain.
- **Contested** — subject to meaningful scholarly or interpretive disagreement.
- **Speculative** — proposed as a possibility rather than a demonstrated conclusion.
- **Symbolic** — intended as an analogical or contemplative formulation rather than a literal claim.

These labels are not interruptions of the mystery. They are part of the portal's ethical structure. They allow the reader to know when the ground is historical, scientific, experiential, metaphysical, comparative, or newly imagined.

:: On the page, the seven labels are set in gold above an entry's first line, and the confidence markers in bone beside them, so the kind of a claim and the confidence in it are never confused for each other.

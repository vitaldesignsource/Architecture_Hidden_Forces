The strength of this portal depends upon disciplined distinctions.

A **historical claim** asks what a particular text, civilisation, philosopher, or ritual community actually taught. It must be supported by primary sources, responsible translations, archaeological context, and relevant scholarship. A modern interpretation should not be placed into the mouth of an ancient writer.

A **scientific claim** concerns phenomena that can be observed, measured, modelled, tested, or subjected to repeatable investigation. Optics, electromagnetic radiation, colour perception, circadian biology, photosynthesis, neurological aura, and bioluminescence belong primarily to this domain.

A **phenomenological claim** describes what an experience is like without prematurely deciding what caused it. If a meditator reports a field of blue-white light, the report itself may be documented while its neurological, psychological, symbolic, or spiritual interpretation remains open.

A **metaphysical claim** proposes an account of reality, causation, participation, consciousness, or hidden order that may exceed the methods of empirical science. Such claims can still be evaluated for coherence, explanatory power, consistency, historical grounding, lived consequence, and relation to other parts of the system.

A **comparative claim** identifies a structural resemblance between traditions. Similarity does not automatically prove historical borrowing, identical experience, or metaphysical identity. The Egyptian solar rebirth, the Platonic intelligible sun, and the Upanishadic light of the Self may illuminate one another comparatively while retaining their differences.

A **speculative synthesis** extends the Architecture beyond what any source explicitly teaches. This is where the system may develop original principles such as [[xix-6|Tattvic Refraction]], [[xix-1|Luminous Mediation]], [[xix-11|Captive Light]], or [[xix-4|the Law of Coherent Radiance]]. These ideas should be presented openly as formulations original to the system rather than disguised as forgotten ancient doctrines. That their names are new does not make the realities they name inventions: the system affirms what it formulates, and marks the formulation as its own so that the reader knows whose testimony is being offered.

## The methodological covenant

The portal therefore maintains a firm covenant:

- Scientific findings will not be presented as automatic proof of metaphysical doctrines.
- Symbolic similarity will not be treated as proof of historical transmission.
- Visionary intensity will not be treated as proof of spiritual authority.
- Ancient language will not be used to conceal modern invention.
- Scepticism will not be used to erase meaningful experience before it has been understood.
- The absence of a physical measurement will not be treated as evidence that a reality upon another plane is absent.

This covenant allows wonder and rigour to inhabit the same architecture.

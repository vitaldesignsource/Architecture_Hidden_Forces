The Luminous Architecture can be entered through several routes.

**The Foundations Path** begins with the nature of light, its first principles, gradations, media, and relationship to darkness. This is the best route for understanding the internal metaphysics of the portal.

**The Qualities Path** begins with a particular appearance — golden, crystalline, diffuse, fiery, lunar, shadowless, pulsing, cold, turbid, or dark — and follows that quality through physical science, visionary accounts, symbols, rituals, and historical traditions.

**The Traditions Path** follows light through Egypt, Mesopotamia, Iran, Greece, Hermetism, Gnosticism, Judaism, Christianity, India, Buddhism, Daoism, Islam, alchemy, and modern esoteric thought.

**The Operations Path** studies what light does: reveal, form, animate, guide, purify, consecrate, heal, expose, awaken, dissolve, transfigure, or return.

**The Bodies and Worlds Path** follows light through cosmos, sun, moon, stars, elements, Morphaithēr, the Fourfold Veil, the etheric body, astral field, psychic life, soul, intellect, organism, and matter.

**The Primary Text Path** provides direct access to ancient and historical passages, accompanied by context, dating, translation notes, and links to corresponding ideas elsewhere in the portal.

**The Symbol Path** begins with an image — the solar disc, halo, flame, lamp, eye, star, lightning bolt, rainbow, mirror, crystal, mandorla, black sun, or solar barque — and unfolds its changing meanings.

## A portal, not a line

Because this is a portal rather than merely a linear book, each entry contains cross-connections. An entry on [[iv-55|crystalline light]] might connect to optics, the Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad, visionary phenomenology, crystal symbolism, mineral structure, ritual technology, and the system's concept of coherent transmission.

The reader may therefore move vertically through gradations, horizontally across traditions, historically through time, or inwardly through layers of experience.

:: In practice: the divisions are the Foundations, Bodies and Worlds, and Operations paths laid end to end; the facets on every entry carry the Qualities, Traditions, and Symbol paths across them; and the Primary Text path gathers in the reference library. Every entry has an address of the form /phos/division/slug and an id of the form division-number, so that a cross-reference is a promise the audit checks.

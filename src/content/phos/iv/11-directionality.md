Directionality is the degree to which light possesses a definite orientation.

A ray or beam is strongly directional. Diffuse light is weakly directional. A luminous field may surround the receiver from many directions simultaneously.

Directionality concentrates activity toward a particular destination. Within consciousness, attention gives light direction. Within ritual, intention aligns symbolic, emotional, and bodily forces toward an operation. Within speech, meaning directs sound toward understanding or action.

Directionality gives luminosity purpose, but excessive direction may become fixation. The light can become so narrowly aimed that it excludes necessary context.

Right direction preserves awareness of the larger field through which the ray travels.

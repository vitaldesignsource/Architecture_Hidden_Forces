A glimmer is a faint, uncertain, or distant appearance of light. It stands near the threshold of visibility.

Because it reveals little, the glimmer invites attention without providing certainty. It may announce a distant source, mark the first opening within darkness, or indicate that something hidden is beginning to become perceptible.

Psychically, a glimmer resembles intuition before articulation. A relation is sensed, but its form has not yet become stable enough for clear understanding.

The glimmer should therefore be approached as indication rather than confirmation. It says that something may be present, not that the nature of that presence has been fully established.

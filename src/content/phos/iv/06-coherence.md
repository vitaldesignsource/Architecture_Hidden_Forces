Coherence is the ordered relationship among the components of a luminous current. In physical optics, coherence has precise meanings concerning stable relationships among waves. Within the wider metaphysical system, it refers to coordination, internal consistency, and unified direction.

A coherent light carries pattern. Its parts reinforce rather than randomly disrupt one another. Thought, desire, emotion, imagination, and action become luminous when they participate in one intelligible orientation.

Coherence gives light penetrative and formative power. Scattered intensity may excite a field, but coherent intensity can reorganise it.

## Not sufficient

Coherence alone does not guarantee truth. A false doctrine can be internally consistent, and an unhealthy psychic pattern can be highly organised. Coherence must be joined to truthfulness, right relation, and beneficial consequence.

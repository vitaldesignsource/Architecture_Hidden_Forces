Veiled light is luminosity partially concealed or softened by a medium. Cloud, mist, smoke, fabric, glass, foliage, atmosphere, symbol, language, and psychic condition can all act as veils.

The veil reduces direct intensity while increasing atmosphere, subtlety, and mystery. It may protect the receiver from glare or prevent full recognition.

Veiled light demonstrates that concealment is not the opposite of revelation. A veil can make a light perceivable by reducing what would otherwise overwhelm the senses. It can also give form to the invisible by scattering light through a visible medium.

The veil becomes harmful only when necessary mediation hardens into permanent obstruction or deliberate deception.

An aureole is a surrounding field of sacred radiance, usually extending around the entire figure rather than only the head.

Where the halo emphasises the luminous intellect or sanctified identity, the aureole presents the whole person as immersed in or transmitting light. It indicates bodily participation in a greater radiance.

An aureole may be circular, flame-like, cloud-like, or geometrically structured. In art, it marks a field of transfiguration rather than a literal atmospheric effect.

Within the system, the aureole symbolises a vessel whose luminosity has become environmental. The transformed form does not merely hold light; it establishes a luminous atmosphere around itself.

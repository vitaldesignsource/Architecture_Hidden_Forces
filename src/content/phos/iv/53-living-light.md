Living light has a literal physical expression in bioluminescence: light produced by chemical processes within organisms. Fireflies, marine organisms, fungi, and other living forms generate illumination through specific biological mechanisms.

Within the metaphysical system, living light refers more broadly to luminosity joined to vitality, responsiveness, growth, adaptation, and self-renewal.

Living light does not merely shine upon an organism. It appears through the organism's active participation. Its pattern changes as life responds to environment, rhythm, need, and relation.

This does not mean that every living being emits a visible spiritual glow, since the light in question is organisational before it is visual. But the term is not a poetic description of physiology. It identifies a real formative quality upon the vital and etheric planes — light become inseparable from the processes of life — of which bioluminescence is one physical expression among many.

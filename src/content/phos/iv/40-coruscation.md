Coruscation is a brilliant, glittering, or rapidly flashing play of light. It is more dramatic than shimmer and often suggests multiple sharp reflections or energetic bursts.

The word belongs more to descriptive and poetic language than to a single scientific mechanism. Ice, metal, jewels, moving water, electrical activity, or countless reflective surfaces may coruscate.

Within the system, coruscation represents multiplicity under high luminous intensity. It can disclose extraordinary complexity, but it may also fragment attention through excessive brilliance.

Coruscating light is visually fertile but difficult to stabilise. It dazzles the receiver with many simultaneous points of disclosure.

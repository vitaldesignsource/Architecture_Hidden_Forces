Affect is the felt tone produced when light enters a living or psychic receiver. It includes attraction, awe, warmth, serenity, fear, exposure, longing, joy, grief, intimacy, and estrangement.

Affect does not belong entirely to the light itself. It arises through the relationship among the source, atmosphere, medium, bodily state, memory, expectation, and receiver.

The same light can comfort one person and disturb another. A dim room may feel peaceful because it shelters, or threatening because it conceals. Brilliant illumination may produce joy, scrutiny, liberation, or vulnerability.

Affect is therefore evidence of relation, not automatic evidence of the source's nature.

Discernment asks both what the light carries and what within the receiver is responding to it.

Shadowless light is illumination so evenly distributed that strong directional shadows nearly disappear. Perfectly shadowless physical illumination is difficult because form and occlusion continue to produce differences, but highly diffuse light can greatly reduce visible shadow.

Metaphysically, shadowless light represents a condition in which nothing stands outside the luminous field. It suggests omnipresence, total disclosure, or consciousness without a concealed exterior.

## The danger in the image

Yet the image carries danger. Without shadow, depth and distinction may become difficult to perceive. Shadows often reveal the geometry of form.

True illumination does not necessarily eliminate every shadow. It makes the relationship among source, object, boundary, and obscurity intelligible.

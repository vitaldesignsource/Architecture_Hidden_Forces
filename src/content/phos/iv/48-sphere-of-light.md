A sphere of light is luminosity appearing as a bounded, self-contained orb or globe. Physically, spherical appearances may result from luminous objects, plasma phenomena, defocused point sources, lens artefacts, atmospheric effects, or reflections.

Photographic orbs should not automatically be interpreted as spiritual entities. Dust, moisture, insects, and optical conditions produce many circular images.

Symbolically, the luminous sphere represents wholeness, centred radiance, self-containment, and equal extension in every direction. It is light organised around a centre without privileged orientation.

The sphere is the geometry of luminous totality. It becomes metaphysically significant when it expresses integration rather than merely circular appearance.

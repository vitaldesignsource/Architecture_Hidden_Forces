A corona is a crown-like luminosity surrounding a luminous or obscured source. The solar corona is the Sun's outer atmosphere, made dramatically visible during a total solar eclipse. Atmospheric coronas may also appear through diffraction by small droplets or particles.

The solar corona, the Sun's outer atmosphere of plasma at more than a million degrees, is described by the [University Corporation for Atmospheric Research](https://scied.ucar.edu/learning-zone/sun-space-weather/corona).

The corona differs from the halo through both mechanism and appearance, although the terms may overlap in ordinary speech. A corona tends to cling more closely to the source and may display delicate structure or spectral rings.

Symbolically, it represents radiance revealed at a boundary. When the central source is hidden, its surrounding power may become more visible.

The corona demonstrates that concealment can reveal dimensions of a source normally overwhelmed by its direct brilliance.

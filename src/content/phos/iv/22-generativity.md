Generativity is the power of light to bring forth new relations, capacities, images, organisms, meanings, or forms.

Generative light does not merely expose what already exists. It awakens latent possibilities. Sunlight participates in ecological growth. An insight generates further questions. A symbol gives rise to new interpretations. A truthful encounter reorganises the future possibilities of a life.

## The mark of a false light

Generativity distinguishes living illumination from sterile display. A false light often demands fascination while producing no new capacity in the receiver.

What genuine light generates should remain capable of relation, differentiation, and further development. Repetition alone is not generativity.

Generative light makes more life, understanding, and responsible participation possible.

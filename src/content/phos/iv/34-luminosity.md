Luminosity is the general condition or degree of being luminous. It is broader than glow, radiance, brilliance, or incandescence and can contain all of them.

In astronomy and physics, luminosity can have precise technical meanings concerning total emitted power. In artistic and phenomenological language, it describes the perceived presence, richness, or quality of light within an image or environment.

Within the system, luminosity is the capacity of a source, medium, form, or consciousness to participate in disclosure. Something may possess low visual brightness yet considerable symbolic or noetic luminosity.

Luminosity is therefore not one particular appearance. It is the general condition through which appearance becomes light-bearing.

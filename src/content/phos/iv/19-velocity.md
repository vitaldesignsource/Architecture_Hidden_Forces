Velocity concerns how rapidly light or luminous influence propagates.

Physical light travels through vacuum at the invariant speed *c*, while its phase and group behaviour within media depends upon the optical conditions involved. These physical meanings should not be confused with metaphysical claims about higher lights travelling faster than ordinary light.

## Through the levels

Within the wider system, luminous velocity describes how quickly an influence moves through levels of mediation. An insight may arise instantly but require years to enter emotion, habit, bodily action, and material consequence.

Rapid transmission is not always desirable. Speed can outrun integration. Slow illumination may penetrate more completely because each layer is given time to respond.

The true measure of luminous velocity is not haste but appropriate movement through the architecture of reception.

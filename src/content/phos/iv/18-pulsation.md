Pulsation is the recurrent intensification and relaxation of light. It is a particular form of rhythm organised around contraction and expansion, presence and recession, or concentration and release.

A pulse establishes a centre. It gathers energy, releases it into the field, and returns toward renewed concentration. Heartbeat, breath, neural firing, ritual drumming, and periodic flashes provide physical or symbolic analogies.

## Healthy and disordered

Psychic pulsation appears when an image, desire, or memory repeatedly enters awareness, withdraws, and returns. Such repetition may indicate unresolved force, developing meaning, or a pattern seeking embodiment.

Healthy pulsation renews a field. Disordered pulsation produces instability, compulsion, or exhaustion.

The pulse is the simplest architecture through which light begins to breathe.

Stability is the ability of a light to maintain its character despite interference, fluctuation, or environmental change.

A stable light remains sufficiently constant for sustained observation. A stable insight continues to organise understanding after the emotion of discovery has faded. A stable spiritual practice carries orientation through changing moods and circumstances.

## Dynamic, not immobile

Stability is not immobility. A flame can be stable while continually moving. A living luminous system maintains itself through adjustment rather than absolute stillness.

The most valuable stability is dynamic. It preserves identity while permitting adaptation.

Light that cannot tolerate any variation has become rigid. Light that changes with every disturbance cannot become architecturally formative.

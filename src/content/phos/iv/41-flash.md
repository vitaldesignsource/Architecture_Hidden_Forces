A flash is a sudden, brief, and intense appearance of light. Its power lies partly in the contrast between its arrival and the darkness or lower illumination surrounding it.

A flash may expose an entire field but provide too little time for examination. It reveals by interruption.

Psychically, the flash corresponds to sudden insight, recovered memory, shock, recognition, or visionary eruption. It may be accurate, distorted, or incomplete. Its intensity does not establish its truth.

The flash initiates knowledge but rarely completes it. What it reveals must later be recollected under steadier illumination.

Vitality is the degree to which light supports animation, renewal, responsiveness, growth, and self-maintaining organisation.

Physical light participates in many biological processes, including photosynthesis, circadian regulation, vision, and environmental signalling. This does not prove the existence of a generic invisible life-energy. The metaphysical category of vital light concerns the wider formative pattern expressed through living organisation.

## Recognised by what it sustains

Vital luminosity is recognised through what it sustains. It increases responsiveness without producing chaos, strengthens organisation without creating rigidity, and supports renewal without dissolving identity.

The etheric body is luminous in this formative sense. Its light appears as living continuity rather than visible brightness.

Vital light does not merely make the organism shine. It helps the organism continue becoming itself.

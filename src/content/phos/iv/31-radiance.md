Radiance is the expansive presence of light proceeding from or surrounding a source. It is broader than a ray and less bounded than a beam.

Radiance makes a source environmentally present. It reaches beyond the source's visible edge and establishes a surrounding field of illumination, warmth, or attention.

Metaphysically, radiance expresses communicative abundance. A source becomes participable without ceasing to remain itself. Its light proceeds into relation while the originating centre remains.

## Borrowed brightness

Radiance must be distinguished from borrowed brightness. A reflective object can appear radiant while depending entirely upon another source. Discernment asks whether the radiance is original, transmitted, reflected, or symbolically attributed.

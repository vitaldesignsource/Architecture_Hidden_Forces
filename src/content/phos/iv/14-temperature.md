Temperature enters luminous analysis through several distinct meanings that must not be confused.

Physical light can transfer energy and contribute to heating. **Colour temperature**, however, describes chromatic appearance and does not always indicate how much thermal heat a light delivers to a particular object. A visually cool blue light may arise from a source with a higher colour temperature than a visually warm amber light.

## Warmth and coolness

Metaphysically, warmth signifies activation, intimacy, animation, and participation. Coolness signifies distance, distinction, stillness, and intellectual clarity. Neither is inherently superior.

Warm light draws the receiver into relation. Cool light may create the distance necessary for accurate perception.

A complete illumination often requires both: warmth sufficient to awaken participation and coolness sufficient to preserve discernment.

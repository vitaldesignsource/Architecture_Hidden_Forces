Colour is one of the principal ways light becomes qualitatively differentiated. Physically, it emerges through relations among spectral composition, surfaces, media, sensory systems, and perception.

Colour does not belong exclusively to the source or object. It is an event arising among them.

## Tattvic coloration

Within Morphaithēr, [[ii-9|tattvic coloration]] refers to formative bias rather than literal visible hue. Ākāśa, Vāyu, Tejas, Apas, and Pṛthivī may be represented through colours, but their deeper significance lies in spaciousness, movement, transformation, cohesion, and stability.

Symbolic colour can organise meaning, evoke affect, and direct attention. Its interpretation must remain contextual. No colour possesses one universal significance in every culture, organism, ritual, or state of consciousness.

Colour is light made qualitative through relation.

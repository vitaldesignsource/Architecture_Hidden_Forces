Splendor is brilliance joined to magnitude, order, dignity, and meaningful form. It is not merely bright; it appears worthy of sustained attention.

A sunrise may be brilliant, but it becomes splendid through scale, atmosphere, colour, proportion, and the way it reorganises the entire landscape. Splendor is therefore relational and architectural.

In sacred and royal symbolism, splendor indicates a luminosity through which invisible rank, order, beauty, or excellence becomes outwardly manifest.

## Manufactured splendour

Yet splendor can be manufactured. Power often surrounds itself with luminous display. Authentic splendor deepens under examination because its appearance corresponds to an interior order. False splendor depends upon spectacle alone.

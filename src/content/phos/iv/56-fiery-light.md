Fiery light is luminosity joined to heat, combustion, excitation, transformation, and consumption. It belongs most directly to flame, incandescent matter, lightning, and the radiant consequences of intense energy release.

Metaphysically, fiery light corresponds strongly to Tejas and to [[ii-22|Ignisophia]]. It differentiates, purifies, activates, and changes whatever enters its field.

## What fire does not leave intact

Fire reveals through transformation. It does not leave every form intact. It may liberate what a form contains or destroy the vessel before its contents can be integrated.

Fiery light therefore requires wisdom, direction, and proportion. Ignisophia is not fire alone but fire governed by [[ii-23|discernment, truth, necessity, initiation, silence, and longing]].

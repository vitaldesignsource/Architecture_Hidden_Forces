Intensity is the local strength with which light becomes present. Physically, it concerns how much luminous or radiant power reaches a given region. Phenomenologically, it is experienced as brightness, vividness, pressure, or force of appearance.

Intensity determines how insistently a light enters the field of attention. A low-intensity light may remain near the threshold of perception, while an intense light can dominate the entire visual or psychic field.

## Not effectiveness

Intensity is not identical with effectiveness. Too little may fail to disclose. Too much may produce glare, saturation, pain, or the collapse of distinction. A highly intense psychic experience may overwhelm the soul without conveying clear meaning.

The correct intensity is relational. It depends upon the purpose of the light and the capacity of the receiver.

A mandorla is an almond-shaped or vesica-like aureole enclosing the whole body of a sacred figure. It commonly marks moments of revelation, transfiguration, enthronement, cosmic sovereignty, or passage between worlds.

## Its geometry

Its geometry is significant. The mandorla resembles the intersecting region created by two overlapping circles. It therefore symbolises the threshold where two orders meet: divine and human, hidden and manifest, eternal and temporal.

The figure within the mandorla occupies neither realm exclusively. It appears within the opening produced by their relation.

Within the luminous architecture, the mandorla is a geometry of mediation. It is light shaped as a threshold.

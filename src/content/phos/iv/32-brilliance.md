Brilliance is luminosity distinguished by exceptional intensity, clarity, vividness, or optical force. It commands perception and may temporarily subordinate everything around it.

Brilliance can reveal fine detail when proportionate, but excessive brilliance produces glare. At high intensity, the receiver loses distinctions and encounters a nearly undifferentiated field.

Intellectual brilliance similarly combines intensity with rapid differentiation, but it does not automatically guarantee wisdom or truth. Psychic brilliance may arise from powerful emotion, imagination, or excitation.

Brilliance describes the force of appearance. Its value depends upon what that force reveals and whether the receiver can integrate it.

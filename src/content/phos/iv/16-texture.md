Texture is the perceived surface or internal grain of light. A luminosity may appear smooth, granular, shimmering, velvety, crystalline, hazy, sharp, liquid, or fractured.

Physical texture can arise from surfaces, particles, atmospheric variation, interference, scattering, or changes in intensity. Psychic texture arises when emotion and imagination give an inward light a distinctive felt character.

## What texture reveals

Texture reveals the condition of the medium. Smooth illumination suggests continuity. Grain may indicate particles, fragmentation, or fine differentiation. Shimmering suggests rapid variation. Hard-edged light emphasises boundary; soft light emphasises atmosphere.

Texture gives illumination tactility. It allows light to be experienced almost as though it possessed surface, substance, or touch.

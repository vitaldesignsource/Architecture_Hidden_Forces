Rhythm is the patterned organisation of luminous change through time. It includes recurrence, interval, alternation, acceleration, deceleration, emergence, and withdrawal.

Day and night, lunar phases, seasons, waking and sleep, inspiration and integration all belong to luminous rhythm. The temporary disappearance of light may be part of its order rather than evidence of failure.

## Exposure and assimilation

Rhythm allows a receiver to alternate between exposure and assimilation. Continuous intensity can exhaust attention. Darkness and rest give the organism time to incorporate what illumination has awakened.

Tone Ether governs rhythm through relation, ratio, and interval. Life Ether integrates the repeated cycle into continuity.

Luminous rhythm is time becoming formative.

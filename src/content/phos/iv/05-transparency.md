Transparency is the capacity of a medium to permit light to pass through it. It belongs primarily to the intermediary rather than the source.

No medium is absolutely neutral. Even transparent air, glass, or water modifies transmission through absorption, refraction, scattering, polarisation, or delay. Transparency therefore means relative openness, not the absence of mediation.

A transparent soul permits experience to be received without immediately burying it beneath projection, defensiveness, or compulsive interpretation. A transparent symbol allows meaning to shine through its form while remaining distinctly embodied.

Transparency does not require the medium to disappear. The finest vessel remains present while faithfully serving what it carries.

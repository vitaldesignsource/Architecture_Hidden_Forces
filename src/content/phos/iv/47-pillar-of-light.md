A pillar of light is a vertical luminous formation connecting lower and higher regions. Physical light pillars may arise when light reflects from plate-shaped ice crystals suspended in the atmosphere. Beams made visible by mist, smoke, or dust can also appear pillar-like.

Symbolically, the pillar establishes an axis between heaven and earth, source and vessel, descent and return. It is radiance given vertical direction and architectural stability.

A pillar of light may represent revelation descending, aspiration rising, or the formation of a central channel through which both movements become possible.

Within the human vessel, it can symbolise the aligned axis around which psychic and etheric forces become organised. It should remain a symbolic geometry unless a physical mechanism is specifically being described.

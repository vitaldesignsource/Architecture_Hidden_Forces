Luminous intelligence is the capacity of light to reveal or carry meaningful order. It should not be assumed that physical light is conscious merely because it behaves according to lawful patterns.

Intelligence enters the luminous event when relationships become organised, selected, interpreted, or directed in ways that preserve intelligibility.

## The chain of articulation

Noetic light reveals architecture. Logos gives that architecture proportion and articulation. Nous recognises the disclosed order. The soul translates it into image and meaning. The body renders it capable of action.

A luminous current may therefore be called intelligent when it carries a coherent formative ratio or participates in an intelligence greater than the receiving personality. This remains distinct from claiming that every ray possesses an individual mind.

Intelligent light does not merely illuminate objects. It reveals why and how things belong together.

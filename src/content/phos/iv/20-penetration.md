Penetration is the capacity of light to pass beyond surfaces and reach deeper regions of a medium, form, organism, or consciousness.

Physical penetration depends upon wavelength, material, absorption, scattering, thickness, and energy. Metaphysical penetration depends upon intensity, coherence, correspondence, receptivity, timing, and the organisation of the vessel.

A teaching may be understood intellectually without penetrating emotional life. An emotional realisation may remain unable to alter bodily habit. Transformative illumination occurs when light reaches the level at which a pattern is continually being reproduced.

## Governed by right relation

Penetration must be governed by right relation. Depth reached without preparation can become violation or fragmentation.

The deepest light is not the light that forces entry. It is the light capable of reaching the formative root while preserving the integrity of the vessel.

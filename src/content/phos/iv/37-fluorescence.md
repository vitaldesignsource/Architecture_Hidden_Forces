Fluorescence occurs when a substance absorbs radiation and rapidly re-emits part of that energy as light, typically at a longer wavelength. The visible emission generally ends almost immediately when excitation ceases.

The mechanism, absorption at one wavelength and near-instantaneous re-emission at a longer one, is described in [Florida State University's primer on fluorescence excitation and emission](https://micro.magnet.fsu.edu/primer/lightandcolor/fluoroexcitation.html).

Unlike phosphorescence, fluorescence is closely dependent upon the continuing presence of an activating source.

Metaphysically, fluorescence represents immediate responsive luminosity. A receiver encounters a current and translates it into another visible form. The response may be vivid while contact continues but leave little independent persistence afterward.

Fluorescence therefore raises a question central to spiritual and psychic discernment: does the vessel retain and integrate the illumination, or does it shine only while externally stimulated?

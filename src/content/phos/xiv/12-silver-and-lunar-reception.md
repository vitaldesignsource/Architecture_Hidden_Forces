Silver belongs to the lunar register because its brilliance is cool, receptive, reflective, and responsive to surrounding conditions. Where gold suggests a light radiating from a centred source, silver suggests the faithful reception and redistribution of light.

Ritually, silver can signify the soul, imagination, dream, cyclical time, fluidity, memory, and the mediating power of the moon. A silver surface changes as the observer and environment move. It does not present the same stable solar quality as gold; its appearance is relational.

## Tarnish and restoration

Silver's tendency to tarnish adds depth to its symbolism. Reception requires maintenance. The reflective soul can become obscured by accumulated contact, yet its luminosity has not been destroyed. Purification removes the surface condition and restores the capacity to receive and reflect.

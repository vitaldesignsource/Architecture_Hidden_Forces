Consecration is the foundation of luminous ritual technology because it establishes who or what a vessel serves, what force it is meant to receive, how that force may act, and toward what purpose the relation is directed.

A consecrated object becomes part of a vertical chain of mediation. Its material, form, name, history, ritual treatment, and use are brought into correspondence with a governing principle. Repetition deepens the relation, allowing the vessel to accumulate a stable luminous identity.

## Not possession

Right relation prevents consecration from becoming possession. The practitioner does not simply seize a power and assign it a function. The source, intermediary, vessel, operator, recipient, and intended consequence must be placed into ethical and ontological alignment. Consecration is truthful when every part of the architecture is allowed to remain what it is while participating in a coherent whole.

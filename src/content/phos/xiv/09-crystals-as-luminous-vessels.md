Crystals join material density with transparency, internal order, refraction, reflection, and the separation of light into colours. Their patterned structure makes them natural images of force stabilised without becoming entirely opaque.

Within ritual, a crystal may function as a focal point, lens, repository, marker of clarity, or vessel of consecrated relation. Its effectiveness does not require the claim that every crystal possesses the same power or that mineral species mechanically produce fixed spiritual results. The specific stone, physical properties, cultural history, preparation, placement, and ritual purpose all matter.

## The responsibility of mediation

A crystal can concentrate attention and make subtle changes in light visible. Yet it can also dazzle, distort, or multiply appearances. It symbolises not only purity but the responsibility of mediation. A crystal does not simply "contain light"; it alters the manner in which light becomes present.

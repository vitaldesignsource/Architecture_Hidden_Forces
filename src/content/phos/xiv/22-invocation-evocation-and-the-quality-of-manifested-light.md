Invocation ordinarily describes calling a power inward or into participation with the ritual consciousness. Evocation ordinarily describes calling a presence into a defined external or intermediate field. Historical traditions use these terms differently, so the distinction should be treated as functional rather than absolute.

Invocation emphasises reception, indwelling, inspiration, and identification. It requires a vessel capable of bearing the invoked quality without confusing participation with personal ownership. Evocation emphasises manifestation, boundary, communication, and discernible relation. It requires a field stable enough to support contact without collapsing the difference between practitioner and presence.

## Quality over intensity

In both cases, the quality of manifested light matters more than its intensity. Coherent manifestations produce ordered consequences and retain continuity of identity. Distorted manifestations may be brilliant yet unstable, flattering, contradictory, coercive, or dependent upon increasing psychic agitation. Brilliance alone does not identify the order of a being.

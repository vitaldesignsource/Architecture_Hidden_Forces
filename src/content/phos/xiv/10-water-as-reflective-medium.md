Water receives, reflects, refracts, transmits, and distorts light. When still, it becomes a mirror; when moving, it breaks a stable image into living fragments. When deep, it conceals what it contains. Water is therefore simultaneously a medium of revelation and mystery.

Ritually, water may purify, consecrate, receive offerings, preserve an image, carry a blessing, or serve as a surface for contemplation. Its capacity to assume the shape of a vessel associates it with receptivity, soul, memory, gestation, and [[xii-18|the Divine Feminine]]. Its movement connects it with circulation, transition, dissolution, and return.

## A complete small architecture

A bowl of water placed in relation to a flame, moon, image, or spoken name forms a small but complete ritual architecture. The light descends, the water receives it, and the surface makes the relation visible. Disturbing the water then demonstrates how psychic agitation alters reception without necessarily changing the source.

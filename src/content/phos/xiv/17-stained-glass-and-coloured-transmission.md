Stained glass transforms undifferentiated external light into an interior field of coloured meaning. The window does not generate the light; it receives, differentiates, and distributes it throughout sacred space. Architecture becomes a prism.

The imagery within stained glass is activated by illumination. Without light, the figures remain dark arrangements of material. When the sun enters, the images project colour across walls, floors, bodies, incense, and air. The sacred narrative moves beyond the window and participates in the whole chamber.

## A model of revelation

This provides a precise model of revelation. Higher light enters through a historically and culturally formed medium. It emerges coloured by symbol and material, yet can still communicate the source from which it proceeds. The goal is not to eliminate mediation but to make mediation truthful.

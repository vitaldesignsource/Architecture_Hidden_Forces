Theurgy is sacred action ordered toward participation in divine realities. In the Iamblichean understanding, theurgy does not succeed because the human intellect compels the gods. Divine symbols, rites, materials, names, and sacred correspondences are effective because the gods have already placed their signatures throughout the cosmos.

The theurgist assembles an architecture capable of receiving divine activity. Stones, plants, fragrances, colours, hymns, gestures, images, times, and places may act as *synthēmata* — tokens or signatures through which a particular divine order becomes present. Their power does not arise solely from human interpretation.

## Descent according to capacity

Divine illumination in theurgy descends according to the capacity of the receiver while remaining greater than the forms through which it appears. The goal is not spectacle, control, or the accumulation of unusual experiences. It is the reorientation and elevation of the whole person through participation in a divine order.

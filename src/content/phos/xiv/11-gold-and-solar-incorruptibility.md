Gold has long been associated with the sun because of its colour, brilliance, rarity, malleability, and resistance to ordinary corrosion. It can endure without losing its recognisable luminous quality, making it an image of constancy within change.

In ritual architecture, gold does not merely signify wealth. Gold leaf, vessels, ornaments, solar discs, and gilded images can create surfaces upon which light appears to rest without being absorbed. The material gives visible form to radiance and can make an image appear illuminated from within.

## Fidelity, not possession

Within the system, gold represents matter brought into sustained agreement with a solar principle. Its incorruptibility is symbolic rather than absolute: physical gold can still be alloyed, scratched, dispersed, and misused. Its highest meaning is not possession but fidelity — the capacity of a material vessel to preserve and transmit a luminous quality without rapid degradation.

A luminous phenomenology journal is a disciplined record of encounters with light in all their forms: physical, atmospheric, bodily, etheric, imaginal, dreamlike, meditative, ritual, and visionary. Its purpose is not merely to collect unusual events. It develops the capacity to perceive subtle distinctions that memory would otherwise blur.

## The conditions

Each entry should begin with ordinary facts: date, time, location, duration, weather, direction faced, sleep, food, emotional condition, physical health, substances or medications, and the practice being performed. These details do not diminish spiritual interpretation. They establish the complete field in which the event occurred. Spirit works through conditions, even when it is not reducible to them.

## The description

The experience itself should first be recorded without interpretation. Describe colour, intensity, motion, geometry, location, texture, temperature, rhythm, apparent distance, emotional tone, and whether the light appeared with the eyes open or closed. Note whether it seemed to occupy physical space, the subtle atmosphere, the surface of the body, an interior field, a dream environment, or a nonlocal-seeming domain. Record whether it responded to attention, breath, prayer, posture, speech, or movement.

## The interpretation and the fruit

Interpretation should follow in a separate section. Possible physical, neurological, psychological, etheric, astral, symbolic, and spiritual meanings may all be considered without prematurely choosing one. A final section should record consequences over the following hours and days. Often the meaning of an experience becomes clearer through what it produces than through what it initially resembles - the test set out in [[xi-27|the protocol for recording luminous experiences]].

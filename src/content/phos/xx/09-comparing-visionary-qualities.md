Visions should be compared by qualities rather than ranked by spectacle. A quiet inner light may carry greater intelligence than an overwhelming display. A small symbol may reorganise an entire life, while an ocean of colour may leave no durable meaning.

Useful comparative qualities include clarity, coherence, stability, autonomy, symbolic density, emotional tone, apparent intelligence, responsiveness, ethical orientation, and lasting consequence. Also note whether the vision demanded submission, flattered the personality, generated fear, respected questioning, or remained truthful when examined later.

## Signatures

Different orders of vision possess different signatures. Etheric perception may emphasise vitality, currents, formative movement, bodily extension, and living rhythm. [[xi-8|Astral vision]] often possesses image, emotion, desire, dramatic colour, mutable scenery, and symbolic beings. Noetic illumination may arrive as direct intelligibility in which meaning is grasped before it is expressed in images. Theophanic encounter may unite presence, intelligence, authority, beauty, and transformative force.

These are working distinctions, not rigid barriers. A single experience may descend through several layers, acquiring images and sensations as it approaches ordinary consciousness. The question is not only what realm the vision came from, but how faithfully each layer translated it.

## Concluding doctrine: the discipline that makes the rest usable

Doctrine that is never tested against observation becomes a vocabulary for describing what one already believed. This division exists so that the other twenty-one can be checked by the person using them.

Its method is unglamorous on purpose. Record the ordinary conditions first, and record them even when they seem irrelevant, because the pattern that eventually emerges will be visible only across many entries. Describe before interpreting. Hold several interpretations open rather than closing on the most flattering one. Then wait, and let consequence do the work that a single striking impression cannot.

Three cautions run through every practice here, and they are the division's real contribution. Intensity is not evidence of source: a brilliant experience and a true one are different claims, and [[xx-10|the distinction between radiance and excitement]] is the first thing a practitioner learns to make. Rank is not conferred by experience: [[xx-14|inflation]] is the commonest way a genuine opening turns into an obstacle. And no spiritual reading of an experience relieves anyone of ordinary care for themselves or another. Where an experience passes beyond the capacity to integrate it, [[xx-15|practice stops and appropriate professional support is sought]] — an act of right relation, not a repudiation of what was seen.

The practices end in return rather than in attainment, because the measure of the whole discipline is what the practitioner is like afterwards, among other people, on an ordinary day.

> The test of a light is not what it looked like, but what it left behind in the life that received it.

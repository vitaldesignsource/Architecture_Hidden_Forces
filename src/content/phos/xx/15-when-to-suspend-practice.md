Practice should be suspended when it persistently produces destabilisation beyond the practitioner's capacity to integrate. Warning signs include prolonged sleeplessness, panic, compulsive ritual repetition, escalating paranoia, dangerous commands, severe dissociation, inability to work or care for oneself, uncontrolled psychic opening, or a growing conviction that ordinary ethical limits no longer apply.

Suspension is not failure. It is an intelligent closing of the aperture. A lens sometimes needs to be covered; a vessel sometimes needs to be removed from the fire. The practitioner may return to ordinary meals, sleep, physical work, nature, conversation, and familiar routines. These acts strengthen embodiment and help redistribute force through the whole person.

## Practices requiring special caution

Any practice involving prolonged fasting, breath retention, sleep deprivation, intoxicants, extreme heat, fire, sensory overload, or dangerous isolation requires special caution. Intensity does not guarantee depth. A practice that repeatedly damages the receiver violates the Law of Corresponding Capacity and [[ii-25|the Law of Right Relation]].

If the disturbance becomes severe, frightening, or dangerous, seek help from an appropriate healthcare professional while also drawing upon trustworthy spiritual guidance if available. The two forms of support need not be treated as enemies. A truthful architecture accepts assistance at every level where assistance is required.

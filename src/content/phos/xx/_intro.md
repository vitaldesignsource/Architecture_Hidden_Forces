The study of light becomes complete only when doctrine is joined to sustained observation. Yet practice must not be reduced to the pursuit of dramatic visions. Its deeper purpose is to refine perception, enlarge the capacity of the receiver, establish right relation among the levels of the person, and learn how different orders of luminosity announce themselves.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, physical, etheric, astral, psychic, symbolic, and spiritual lights are all approached as meaningful realities. They must not, however, be carelessly collapsed into one category. A retinal afterimage is real as a sensory event. A dream-image is real within the dream and psychic field. Etheric perception concerns vital-formative activity. A theophanic encounter may carry an intelligence and authority exceeding the ordinary personality. One phenomenon may also contain several levels at once.

Discernment therefore does not exist to explain away experience. It exists to understand experience more exactly. The practitioner learns to observe not only what appeared, but where it appeared, how it behaved, what faculties received it, what conditions preceded it, and what kind of life followed from it.

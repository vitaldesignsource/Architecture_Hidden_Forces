Inflation occurs when the ordinary personality identifies itself with a force, being, insight, or archetypal magnitude that it has encountered. The practitioner does not merely receive solar light but begins to imagine that they are the sun around which everyone else must revolve.

Visions of crowns, divine names, royal identities, initiatory titles, angelic kinship, or past lives require particular restraint. Such images may carry profound symbolic truth. They may indicate vocation, an inner function, an ancestral pattern, or contact with a spiritual current. But symbolic participation is not automatically personal rank.

## The test of response

The proper response to an exalted experience is increased responsibility. If the vision concerns wisdom, does the person become more teachable? If it concerns healing, do they become more careful about harm? If it concerns authority, can they carry responsibility without demanding submission? If it concerns divine favour, do they become more generous or more entitled?

Authentic spiritual stature seldom needs continual proclamation. The light is known through the architecture it creates around itself. Rank claimed through brilliance alone remains unproven.

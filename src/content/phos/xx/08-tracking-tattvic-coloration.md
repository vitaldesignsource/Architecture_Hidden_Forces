Tattvic tracking studies how the living atmosphere becomes coloured by particular morphogenic biases. The practitioner is not merely looking for five predetermined colours. The inquiry concerns the quality of space, movement, heat, cohesion, density, rhythm, imagination, emotion, and formative tendency.

## The five biases

Ākāśic coloration may be perceived as spaciousness, resonance, depth, silence, or an opening in which other forms can arise. Vāyavic coloration may appear as mobility, scattering, acceleration, nervous movement, communication, or invisible pressure. Taijasic coloration may produce heat, brilliance, distinction, courage, conflict, purification, or sharpness. Āpasic coloration may appear as fluidity, reflection, dream, emotional joining, memory, or absorption. Pārthivic coloration may manifest as weight, boundary, structure, persistence, embodiment, or resistance.

An environment or experience usually contains several tattvic tendencies. One may predominate while others support, moderate, or oppose it. The practitioner should therefore record combinations: fiery-air movement, watery-earth containment, spacious-fire revelation, or air-water emotional volatility.

Over time, the journal can show how sleep, weather, season, location, ritual, planetary configurations, relationships, and personal condition correspond with changes in [[viii-26|the Morphaithēr]]. Correspondence should be assessed through repetition rather than assumed from a single striking event.

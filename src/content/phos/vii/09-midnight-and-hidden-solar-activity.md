At midnight, the Sun is absent from the visible sky yet remains causally present. The night exists not because the Sun has died but because the observer occupies a position turned away from its direct radiation.

Midnight therefore represents concealed continuity. The source continues operating beyond the horizon of ordinary perception.

Within the soul, midnight occurs when familiar consciousness loses access to its organising centre. Dreams, memories, fears, and hidden psychic processes become more active. In alchemical language, this may correspond to [[vi-22|nigredo]] and [[vi-21|the Black Sun]]: solar power withdrawn from surface awareness.

Midnight is not the annihilation of light. It is light operating beyond the current face of the world.

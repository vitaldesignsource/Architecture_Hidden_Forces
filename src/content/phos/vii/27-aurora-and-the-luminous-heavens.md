Auroras occur when charged particles associated with solar activity interact with Earth's magnetic field and upper atmosphere, exciting atmospheric gases and producing shifting curtains of light — see [NASA's explanation of auroras](https://science.nasa.gov/skywatching/).

Auroral light makes normally invisible relations among Sun, magnetosphere, atmosphere, and planet dramatically visible. It is a true architecture of hidden forces revealed through luminosity.

Symbolically, the aurora resembles celestial veils, flowing banners, luminous serpents, or the breathing of the heavens. Its movement suggests a living atmosphere without requiring the phenomenon itself to be treated as supernatural.

Aurora is cosmic force becoming colour through planetary mediation.

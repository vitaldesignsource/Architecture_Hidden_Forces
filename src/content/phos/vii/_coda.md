## The doctrine of celestial illumination

Cosmic light is physical radiation, environmental power, biological signal, temporal measure, symbolic language, and metaphysical image. These functions overlap without becoming identical.

The Sun centres and vitalises. The Moon receives, reflects, and measures change. The stars reveal multiplicity, distance, and historical depth. Planets organise traditional fields of correspondence. Mansions and decans articulate celestial time. Seasons, solstices, and equinoxes translate cosmic geometry into terrestrial rhythm.

Eclipses reveal through interruption. Comets and meteors disrupt familiar expectation. Lightning transmits through sudden intensity. The rainbow discloses multiplicity within unity. Aurora makes hidden solar, magnetic, and atmospheric relations visible. The Solar Barque carries radiance through death and regeneration.

> Celestial light becomes sacred not by ceasing to be physical, but by entering the full architecture of life, time, consciousness, culture, death, and renewed manifestation.

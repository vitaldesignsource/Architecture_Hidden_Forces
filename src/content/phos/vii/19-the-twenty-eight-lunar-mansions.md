Lunar mansions divide the Moon's path among groups of stars or celestial regions used for timekeeping, calendrical reckoning, ritual timing, and divination.

Indian systems commonly employ twenty-seven or twenty-eight *nakṣatras*, while Chinese and other East Asian systems developed twenty-eight mansions. These traditions interacted historically but should not be treated as identical lists with interchangeable meanings. A scholarly study published by [Duke University Press](https://read.dukeupress.edu/sungkyun-journal-of-east-asian-studies/article/22/1/1/299045/Zoomorphizing-the-Asterisms-Indigenous) emphasises both their antiquity and the distinctive development of the Chinese twenty-eight-mansion system.

Within the system, the mansions represent fine lunar articulation: the changing soul passing through differentiated regions of the stellar field.

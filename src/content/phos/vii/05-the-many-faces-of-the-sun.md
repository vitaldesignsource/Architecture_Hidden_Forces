The Sun appears differently according to time, atmosphere, season, latitude, culture, and symbolic relation. It may be creator, ruler, eye, judge, healer, destroyer, charioteer, child, falcon, lion, disk, wheel, boat, or hidden seed.

Ra, Helios, Sūrya, Shamash, Amaterasu, Sol, and other solar figures should not be reduced to interchangeable names for one generic sun god. Each arises from a distinct language, ritual world, cosmology, and historical development.

## Where comparison becomes valuable

Their comparison becomes valuable when differences are preserved. Some emphasise solar kingship, others justice, vision, cyclical renewal, fertility, sacred order, or the perilous passage through night.

The many faces of the Sun reveal how one physical luminary can become a vessel for numerous metaphysical and cultural participations.

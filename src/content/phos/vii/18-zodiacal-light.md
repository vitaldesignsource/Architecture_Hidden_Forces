Physical zodiacal light is a faint triangular or band-like glow visible under dark conditions near dawn or dusk. It is produced by sunlight scattered by interplanetary dust distributed through the plane of the solar system — see [NASA's scientific proceedings on zodiacal light](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19680018003/downloads/19680018003.pdf).

It is called zodiacal because it appears along the ecliptic — the apparent path of the Sun and the region through which the Moon and planets also appear to move.

Symbolically, Zodiacal Light is the diffuse luminous field underlying the twelvefold cycle. It is not one ray from one sign but the common solar medium through which the zodiacal sequence becomes visible.

The signs differentiate the field; zodiacal light reveals their shared plane of relation.

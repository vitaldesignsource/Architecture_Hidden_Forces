Many cultures have associated the dead with stars, constellations, the Milky Way, or particular regions of the night sky. These traditions should be studied individually rather than treated as one universal doctrine.

The star provides a compelling image of the dead because it remains visible yet unreachable, distant yet present, ancient yet continually renewed in perception. Starlight arrives after a long passage, much as memory carries the influence of those no longer physically present.

Within the system, the Stars of the Dead symbolise successful luminous retention. A completed life leaves traces capable of orientation, remembrance, and participation in the larger field.

This does not require every star to be interpreted literally as a deceased human being. The correspondence concerns enduring radiance after bodily withdrawal.

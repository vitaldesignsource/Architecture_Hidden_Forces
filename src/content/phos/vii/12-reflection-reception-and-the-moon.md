The Moon receives solar light and returns a portion of it toward Earth. Its luminosity depends upon surface, angle, orbit, phase, and the position of the observer.

Within The Architecture of Hidden Forces, this makes the Moon the great celestial image of the soul. [[ii-15|Spirit is fontal light]]; [[ii-16|soul is receptive and interpretive light]]. The soul receives, modifies, and presents what it cannot originate entirely by itself.

Lunar phases illustrate the changing relationship between wholeness and appearance. The Moon is not physically diminished at the crescent. Only its visible illumination changes.

The receiver may therefore remain whole while passing through changing degrees of disclosure. Absence of visible radiance does not necessarily mean absence of underlying form.

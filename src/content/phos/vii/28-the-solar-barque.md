The Solar Barque is the vessel carrying the solar deity through the ordered cycle of day, night, death, and regeneration. It is especially prominent in ancient Egyptian solar theology, where the king or justified dead could join the divine entourage associated with Ra's celestial and netherworld journey — see [Smarthistory on Egyptian deities](https://smarthistory.org/egyptian-deities/).

The barque gives movement a vessel. Solar power does not cross the cosmos as disembodied abstraction; it becomes organised through a structure capable of navigation, protection, continuity, and passage.

Within the system, the Solar Barque is a cosmic image of [[ii-22|Ignisophia]]. The Sun provides the central fire, the vessel contains it, the crew coordinates its powers, and the journey gives its radiance direction.

The Outer Sun is the astronomical body around which Earth travels and from which terrestrial daylight primarily proceeds. The Inner Sun is the real psycho-spiritual centre around which consciousness, value, intelligence, and transformative purpose become organised.

The Inner Sun should not be treated as a miniature physical star hidden inside the body. It is a real architecture of centredness upon the psychic and spiritual planes, and the Outer Sun is its physical correspondent rather than its mere image. Within [[ii-22|Ignisophia]], Tiphereth functions as the solar axis around which understanding and desire may become harmonised.

The Outer Sun provides the image because it gathers a world around its radiance. The Inner Sun performs an analogous task by giving psychic and spiritual life a coherent centre.

Health requires correspondence without confusion. The physical Sun remains astronomical; the Inner Sun remains an interior and metaphysical structure of orientation.

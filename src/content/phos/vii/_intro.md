Celestial light reaches humanity through several different orders at once. It is physical radiation produced, reflected, scattered, or absorbed by astronomical bodies. It is biological information regulating organisms and ecosystems. It is temporal light, dividing days, months, seasons, and years. It is also symbolic light through which cultures have interpreted creation, divine order, death, regeneration, kingship, destiny, and the structure of the cosmos.

These orders must remain distinct even when they correspond. Astronomy describes the measurable behaviour of celestial bodies. Myth gives cosmic processes narrative and symbolic form. Astrology interprets celestial cycles through systems of terrestrial correspondence. The Architecture of Hidden Forces studies how these levels meet without pretending that one automatically proves the others.

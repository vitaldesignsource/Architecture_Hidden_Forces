The biblical creation narrative famously places the command for light before the appointment of the Sun, Moon, and stars as celestial luminaries. This sequence has generated a long history of interpretation because it separates primordial light from its later astronomical bearers — see [BioLogos on Genesis and ancient interpreters](https://biologos.org/articles/genesis-creation-and-ancient-interpreters-the-beginning).

Within the system, Light Before the Sun means that the principle of disclosure precedes the localised body through which disclosure later becomes measurable and periodic. The Sun is a luminary: an embodied centre of cosmic illumination. It is not the origin of every metaphysical meaning carried by light.

## An ontological distinction

This is an ontological distinction rather than a competing astronomical theory. Physical sunlight requires a physical Sun. Primordial light names the possibility of manifestation before that possibility has become concentrated into a particular celestial source.

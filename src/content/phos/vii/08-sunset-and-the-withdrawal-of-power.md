Sunset is the visible withdrawal of solar illumination from the local field. The Sun has not ceased to radiate; Earth's rotation has carried the observer beyond direct exposure.

This distinction gives sunset its metaphysical power. A source may remain active even when the receiver no longer stands within its direct light.

Sunset symbolises decline, completion, ageing, death, relinquishment, recollection, and the entrance into hidden activity. It is not simply defeat. What has been made visible during the day now passes into memory, dream, digestion, and subterranean transformation.

The setting Sun teaches that withdrawal can be a mode of procession. Light leaves the visible world so that another phase of its work may begin.

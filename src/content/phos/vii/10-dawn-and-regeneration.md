Dawn is the gradual return of distinguishability. It begins before the solar disk appears because the atmosphere scatters sunlight into the observer's field.

Symbolically, dawn is regeneration accomplished through stages. Darkness softens into tonal difference; outlines emerge; colours return; orientation is restored; and the source finally becomes visible.

Dawn differs from sudden revelation. It gives the receiver time to awaken and adjust. Its light is mediated, diffuse, and developmental.

Within the system, dawn is the model of sustainable transformation. The new form does not erupt fully completed from darkness. It becomes perceptible through a sequence of thresholds.

Dawn is darkness learning to disclose what it has gestated.

Sensible Light is light available to sensory perception. Optical Light is light considered through the physical structures governing emission, propagation, reflection, refraction, diffraction, polarisation, interference, and detection.

This is the most directly measurable order of luminosity. It provides the empirical foundation from which many higher analogies are drawn.

Sensible Light is not philosophically trivial because it is physical. Its behaviour reveals a profound relational architecture: appearance depends upon source, medium, surface, sensory organ, nervous system, and interpretation.

Physical optics does not prove metaphysical doctrines, but it offers disciplined models for understanding mediation and disclosure.

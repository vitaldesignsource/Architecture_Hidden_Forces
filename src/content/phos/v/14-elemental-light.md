Elemental Light is luminosity qualified by elementary patterns of manifestation. These may be interpreted through classical elements, tattvas, or the observable behaviours of natural processes.

Ākāśic light opens space and resonance. Vāyavic light moves and communicates. Tejas-light ignites and transforms. Apasic light coheres and reflects. Pṛthivī-light stabilises and embodies.

Elemental lights are not necessarily separate glowing substances or personified beings. They are qualitative modes through which luminosity acquires a specific formative tendency.

Firelight, reflected water-light, atmospheric light, and mineral luminosity may become physical symbols of these modes, but the visible phenomenon should not be confused with the entire metaphysical principle.

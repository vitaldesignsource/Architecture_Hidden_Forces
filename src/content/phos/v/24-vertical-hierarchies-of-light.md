Vertical hierarchies describe differences in causal depth and scope. They trace light from superessential mystery and fontal source through noetic, archetypal, formative, psychic, vital, and sensible orders.

Hierarchy in this context means sacred or causal order, not domination. A higher principle is more universal and causally encompassing; a lower manifestation is more particular and embodied.

## The lower is not worthless

The lower is not worthless. It makes what is higher locally present. Without embodiment, higher light remains unavailable to concrete participation.

A healthy hierarchy protects difference while maintaining communication. A distorted hierarchy turns metaphysical order into social superiority or treats embodied life as contemptible.

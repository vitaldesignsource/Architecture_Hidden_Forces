One luminous current may produce different effects according to the level at which it is received.

The body receives intensity, warmth, and sensory stimulation. The etheric organisation responds through rhythm and vitality. The astral field receives mood, attraction, and image. The psychic layers relate the event to memory and identity. Nous recognises intelligible structure. Spirit may receive it as orientation toward a greater source.

These are not necessarily six different lights. They may be six transductions of one encounter.

The final appearance always belongs partly to the receiving architecture. Light reveals the receiver even as it reveals the source.

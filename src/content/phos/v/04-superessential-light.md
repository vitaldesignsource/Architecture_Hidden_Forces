Superessential Light names that which exceeds being as finite consciousness can conceive it. It is not one more luminous layer located above the others. It exceeds the entire ladder by exceeding every category used to construct it.

To call it *light* is already analogical. It signifies inexhaustible disclosure, goodness, presence, and intelligibility while acknowledging that their source cannot be reduced to any appearance.

## Dazzling darkness

Superessential Light may therefore be encountered as [[vi-19|dazzling darkness]]. The receiver does not find an object of extraordinary brightness but reaches the limit of its own capacity for distinction.

This is the apophatic summit of luminous language: the point at which light reveals the inadequacy of every image of light.

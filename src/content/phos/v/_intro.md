A gradation of light describes a difference in causal depth, degree of manifestation, or completeness of disclosure. An order of light describes the register within which luminous activity operates. A condition of light describes what has happened to it through transmission, reception, confinement, reflection, or distortion.

These distinctions prevent every kind of luminosity from being forced onto one simplistic ladder. Noetic light is not merely brighter than biological light. Dream light is not simply weaker than angelic light. Reflected light is not necessarily lower in value than direct light. Each belongs to a particular relationship among source, medium, function, form, and receiver.

:: Within this division, *higher* refers to greater causal depth, universality, or integrative power — not visual intensity, moral superiority, social rank, or personal importance.

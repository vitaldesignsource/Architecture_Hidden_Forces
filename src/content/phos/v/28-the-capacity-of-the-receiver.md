A receiver can admit only what its structure, preparation, condition, and openness allow it to integrate.

Capacity includes sensitivity, stability, differentiation, memory, symbolic literacy, bodily regulation, intellectual discipline, ethical formation, and the ability to withstand uncertainty.

A limited capacity does not imply inferiority. Every vessel has appropriate boundaries. A seed receives sunlight differently from an eye, and an eye differently from an intellect.

Development enlarges capacity not by eliminating limits but by refining aperture, lens, boundary, and integration. The mature receiver can receive greater complexity without fragmentation and greater intensity without inflation.

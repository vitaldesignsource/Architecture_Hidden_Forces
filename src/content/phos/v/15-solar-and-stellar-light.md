Solar and stellar light is physically produced through processes occurring within stars and carried across space as electromagnetic radiation. It provides energy, visibility, heat, temporal orientation, and the conditions supporting terrestrial life.

Symbolically, solar light signifies centred intelligence, sovereignty, vitality, revelation, and the unifying power of an inner axis. Stellar light signifies plurality, distance, orientation, destiny, cosmic order, and the persistence of radiance across immense intervals.

## Two modes of knowing

Traditional astrological systems interpret celestial lights through symbolic and temporal correspondences. These interpretations belong to a different mode of knowing from astronomical measurement and should be identified accordingly — but distinct does not mean lesser. Measurement clarifies quantity and repeatable physical relation. Symbolic perception discloses quality, meaning, participation, and formative pattern. Neither possesses exclusive authority over every plane of reality, and the influence of the luminaries upon terrestrial life is real upon more planes than the physical.

The Sun and stars are physical luminaries that also become inexhaustible symbolic vessels.

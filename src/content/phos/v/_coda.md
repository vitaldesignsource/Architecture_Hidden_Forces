## The complete order of luminous gradation

The vertical movement of the system can be summarised as:

> Superessential Mystery → Fontal Light → Noetic Light → Archetypal Light → Formative Light → Intermediary Light → Psychic and Imaginal Light → Etheric and Vital Light → Elemental and Biological Light → Sensible and Optical Light

Reflected, residual, captive, and counterfeit lights are not independent rungs. They are conditions that may occur at several levels. Any order may be reflected. Any embodied illumination may leave residue. Light may become captive within form. Any symbolic or psychic field may produce counterfeit luminosity.

The hierarchy is therefore neither a straight staircase nor a collection of unrelated lights. It is a living architecture of descent, participation, transduction, embodiment, recollection, and return.

> Light descends by differentiation, becomes present through mediation, and returns through the restoration of coherence.

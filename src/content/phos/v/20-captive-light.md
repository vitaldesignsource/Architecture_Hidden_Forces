Captive Light is luminosity bound, enclosed, latent, or prevented from free expression within a form.

Physically, energy may be stored in chemical bonds, excited states, thermal conditions, or constrained electromagnetic systems, although these should not all be described literally as imprisoned visible light.

Metaphysically, captive light is potential held within matter, memory, symbol, body, or institution. A capacity exists but cannot yet radiate because the necessary relation, opening, or transformation has not occurred.

## Discerning liberation

Captivity may protect light during gestation, or it may result from distortion and suppression. Liberation must therefore be discerning. Not every enclosure should be broken before the contained power can be integrated.

Counterfeit Light produces the appearance or affect of illumination without maintaining truthful relation to its claimed source.

It may appear as glamour, spectacle, borrowed authority, intellectual display, emotional intoxication, spiritual inflation, manufactured charisma, or coherence arranged around false premises.

## Not ugliness but misrepresentation

Counterfeit light often relies upon genuine luminous qualities. It may be brilliant, beautiful, emotionally powerful, symbolically sophisticated, and internally coherent. What makes it counterfeit is not ugliness but misrepresentation.

It conceals mediation, claims an authority it does not possess, or directs the receiver toward dependency rather than greater capacity.

Counterfeit light is identified through source, fidelity, ethical consequence, and what it enables the receiver to become.

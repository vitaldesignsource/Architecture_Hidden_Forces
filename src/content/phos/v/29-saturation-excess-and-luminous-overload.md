Saturation occurs when a receiver can no longer register meaningful increases in illumination. Distinctions collapse, the field becomes overwhelmed, and additional light ceases to reveal.

Physical overload produces glare, pain, bleaching, or damage. Psychic overload may produce anxiety, dissociation, compulsive interpretation, sleeplessness, emotional flooding, or visionary confusion. Noetic overload may create abstraction without integration.

## What protects the receiver

Higher intensity is therefore not always a gift. Veils, intervals, embodiment, sleep, silence, and gradual transmission protect the receiver.

Sometimes excessive light appears as darkness because the receiving faculty has reached its limit. The remedy is not always more exposure but greater capacity and better mediation.

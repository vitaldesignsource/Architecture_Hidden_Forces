Theurgic Light is luminosity encountered through ritual participation in a divine or transpersonal order. It is not simply light imagined by the practitioner, nor is it power manufactured by personal will.

In classical theurgic understanding, appropriate symbols, names, materials, times, gestures, and invocations form a vessel through which a higher order may become present according to its own nature.

## Correspondence and right relation

Theurgic Light therefore depends upon correspondence and right relation. Ritual structure functions as aperture, lens, mirror, and vessel. The practitioner prepares the conditions of participation but does not create the originating source.

Within the system, theurgic illumination is judged by its capacity to establish greater order, ethical responsibility, integration, and truthful relation — not by spectacle.

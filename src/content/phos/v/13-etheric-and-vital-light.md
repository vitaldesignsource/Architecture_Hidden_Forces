Etheric Light belongs to the vital-formative field through which living organisms awaken, differentiate, organise, repair, grow, and maintain continuity.

Its characteristic appearance is not necessarily visual. Etheric luminosity is expressed through rhythm, vitality, coordination, regenerative capacity, and the sustained organisation of living form.

The Four Ethers articulate its operations. Warmth activates. Light differentiates. Tone establishes relation and proportion. Life integrates and renews.

Vital Light is the life-supporting expression of this architecture. It is not a substitute for physiology or an undefined energy added to biology. It names the formative coherence through which biological processes participate in an organised living whole.

Etheric Light belongs to the etheric body: the real vital-formative dimension of the embodied organism, through which living form is generated, coordinated, repaired, and preserved across time. Physical anatomy supplies its material embodiment; the etheric body supplies the organised vitality through which anatomy becomes and remains alive.

Its characteristic appearance is not necessarily visual. Etheric luminosity is expressed through rhythm, vitality, coordination, regenerative capacity, and the sustained organisation of living form.

The Four Ethers articulate its operations. Warmth activates. Light differentiates. Tone establishes relation and proportion. Life integrates and renews.

Vital Light is the life-supporting expression of this architecture. It is not a substitute for physiology, nor an undefined energy added to biology from outside, nor a name for physiological coherence. It is the real formative luminosity through which biological processes are gathered into an organised living whole — a luminosity that physiology embodies and expresses but does not produce.

The **Luminous Ladder** describes vertical procession. Light descends from universality toward particularity, passing from source into intelligibility, archetype, formation, psychic experience, vitality, and sensible appearance.

The **Luminous Web** describes horizontal relation. Lights operating within the same general level interact through correspondence, resonance, reflection, interference, memory, and exchange.

The ladder explains derivation. The web explains ecology.

## Neither alone

Neither image is sufficient alone. A ladder without a web becomes rigid and falsely hierarchical. A web without a ladder cannot explain differences of causal depth or why some forms depend upon principles more universal than themselves.

The full architecture is a ladder woven into a web: vertically derived, horizontally related, and recursively active.

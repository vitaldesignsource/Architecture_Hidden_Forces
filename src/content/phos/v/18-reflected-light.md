Reflected Light does not originate in the surface from which it appears to proceed. It is received and returned.

Reflection may be faithful, selective, diffuse, distorted, intensified by geometry, or altered by the reflective material. The condition of the mirror determines the image.

Spiritually and intellectually, reflected light describes knowledge, authority, or radiance received from another source. Such light can be entirely legitimate. Most education and tradition depend upon reflection and transmission.

Error begins when the reflector claims to be the source or when the reflected image is treated as exhaustive. Reflected light is derivative, but derivative does not mean false.

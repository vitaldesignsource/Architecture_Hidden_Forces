Dream Light is the illumination within which dream environments, figures, colours, movements, and narratives become perceptible despite reduced dependence upon ordinary sensory input.

Dream luminosity may resemble daylight, twilight, moonlight, firelight, or impossible forms of self-luminous space. Its source is frequently not represented within the dream.

Dream Light reveals the image-producing capacity of the psyche. Memory, emotion, bodily sensation, unconscious conflict, archetypal pattern, and spiritual interpretation may all contribute to the dream field.

Because these sources can intermingle, dream brightness is not proof of spiritual origin. The dream must be interpreted through context, repetition, affect, symbolic structure, and its consequences within waking life.

Among the orders of light, dream light is the illumination of a field that lights itself. Waking sensible light has a source that can be located and a medium that can be crossed. Dream light has neither in the ordinary sense. Environments, figures, colours, and movements become perceptible within it, yet its source is frequently not represented anywhere in the dream, and what it illuminates is assembled rather than encountered.

## Where it stands

Dream light stands between [[v-10|psychic and imaginal light]], from which it draws its images, and [[v-12|astral light]], whose currents it may open onto. It shares with the imaginal its dependence on the image-producing capacity of the psyche. It shares with the astral the possibility of admitting what the psyche did not produce. This double kinship is what makes it an order of its own rather than a variety of either neighbour.

Its luminosity may resemble daylight, twilight, moonlight, firelight, or impossible forms of self-luminous space. None of these resemblances fixes its rank. A dream lit like noon is not thereby a higher dream than one lit like a lamp.

## Why brightness does not rank it

Because memory, emotion, bodily sensation, unconscious conflict, archetypal pattern, and spiritual encounter can all contribute to the same dream field, dream brightness is not proof of spiritual origin. The gradation of a dream light is read from what it discloses, how stably it holds, and what follows from it in waking life, exactly as [[v-27|the same light received at different levels]] is read elsewhere in this division.

The phenomenology of dreaming, the dreamer's altered relation among soul, astral body, and etheric body, and the interpretation of what appears are treated in [[x-15|the entry on dream light among the lights of the soul]]. This entry places dream light among the orders; that one describes living within it.

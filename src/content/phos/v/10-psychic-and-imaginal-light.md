Psychic Light arises within the soul as image, affect, memory, desire, inward perception, and symbolic meaning. Imaginal Light is the luminosity through which these forms become present within interior experience.

The imaginal should not be dismissed as merely unreal. Images can mediate genuine psychological, archetypal, cultural, and spiritual relations. Yet imaginal reality should not automatically be treated as literal external fact.

Psychic light is strongly affected by temperament, expectation, memory, longing, fear, and the gunas. It may clarify or distort. It may reveal a hidden pattern or project an existing pattern onto the unknown.

Its proper study requires phenomenological seriousness joined to disciplined interpretation.

Luminous return is the restoration of meaningful relation among differentiated parts. It does not erase the distinctions acquired through manifestation.

A fragmented psyche returns through the integration of memory, desire, understanding, body, and purpose. A fragmented system returns when its doctrines recover their shared architecture. A ritual returns its participants toward source by gathering symbol, action, speech, and attention into coherent participation.

Return is therefore not escape from multiplicity but its transfiguration. The many recognise how they belong together without being dissolved into undifferentiated sameness.

Coherence is multiplicity remembering its source.

Archetypal Light is noetic luminosity differentiated into formative patterns, logoi, or intelligible possibilities. It stands between universal intelligence and particular manifestation.

An archetype is not merely a recurring image. It is a governing pattern capable of producing families of related images, behaviours, structures, symbols, and forms.

Archetypal Light reveals what a thing may become before that possibility has been embodied in one final arrangement. It carries the grammar of form rather than the finished object.

Within the soul, archetypal light frequently appears through powerful symbolic images. These images are genuine expressions of pattern, but no single image exhausts the archetype from which it proceeds.

Fontal Light is luminosity still gathered within its source. It is the capacity for disclosure before that disclosure has become differentiated into rays, colours, forms, images, or fields.

It is called *fontal* because it is fountain-like: the originating abundance from which participation becomes possible. Yet it should not be imagined as a reservoir of invisible photons waiting above the world.

Fontal Light is not visually dark because it lacks luminosity. It is unmanifest because no distinction has yet arisen between illumination, illuminated form, and receiver.

Within the [[ii-2|Field Before Form]], Fontal Light is the first possibility that hidden being may become present without exhausting itself in appearance.

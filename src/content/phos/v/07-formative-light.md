Formative Light is luminosity operating as organisation. It differentiates direction, proportion, boundary, rhythm, and possible structure before or during embodiment.

Within the [[ii-6|Fourfold Veil]], Light Ether performs this differentiating function. Warmth awakens activity; Light distinguishes; Tone orders relations; Life integrates the result.

Formative Light does not merely shine upon a completed organism. It belongs to the process through which an organism acquires and maintains coherent form.

Within Morphaithēr, formative light receives [[ii-8|tattvic bias]] and [[ii-10|gunic condition]]. It becomes expansive or contractive, mobile or stable, radiant or cohesive, clear or obstructed.

Its luminosity is seen most clearly in organisation rather than brightness.

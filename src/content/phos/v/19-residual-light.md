Residual Light is luminosity persisting after direct contact with its source has ended. Its physical analogues include afterimages, delayed luminescence, retained heat, and traces registered by photosensitive material.

Psychically, residual light remains as memory, altered perception, symbolic imprint, habit, or renewed capacity. Culturally, it appears in texts, rituals, institutions, art, and inherited forms whose originating event has passed.

Within [[ii-27|the Crypt of Primordial Memory]], residual luminosity survives as altered possibility rather than necessarily as a perfect recording.

Residual Light is what illumination leaves behind in the structures it has touched.

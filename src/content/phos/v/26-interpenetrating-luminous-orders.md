Luminous orders are distinguishable but not sealed into isolated layers. A single event may operate simultaneously across physical, biological, etheric, psychic, symbolic, and noetic registers.

Sunrise is optical radiation, environmental transition, biological signal, emotional atmosphere, symbolic image, temporal marker, and possible occasion of philosophical insight.

These dimensions interpenetrate without becoming identical. Physical light does not turn into archetypal light merely because a person interprets it symbolically. Rather, one event becomes capable of participation across several organised levels.

Interpenetration allows unity without category confusion.

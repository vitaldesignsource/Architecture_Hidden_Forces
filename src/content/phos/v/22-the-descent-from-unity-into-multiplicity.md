Luminous descent is the movement through which unified possibility becomes differentiated participation.

Fontal Light does not descend by physically travelling downward. It descends through increasing determination. Noetic unity becomes archetypal pattern. Archetypal pattern becomes formative relation. Formative relation enters psychic image, etheric organisation, elemental quality, biological life, and sensible appearance.

Each stage adds specificity while narrowing possibility. This is not necessarily a corruption. Without differentiation, unity could not become communicable within finite forms.

Descent becomes fragmentation only when the resulting parts lose all conscious relation to one another and to their source.

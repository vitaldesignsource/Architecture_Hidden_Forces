Horizontal varieties are differences among lights operating within the same general order.

Two psychic lights may differ through affect, clarity, symbolism, or gunic condition. Two biological lights may differ through species, mechanism, wavelength, function, or ecological setting. Two noetic illuminations may disclose different principles without one being simply higher than the other.

Horizontal diversity creates luminous ecology. Lights interact, reinforce, complement, reflect, obstruct, and translate one another.

The web is therefore as important as the ladder. Not every difference should be interpreted vertically.

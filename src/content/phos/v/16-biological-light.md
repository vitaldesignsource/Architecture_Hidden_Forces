Biological Light includes literal bioluminescence as well as the many relationships between organisms and illumination. Living beings produce, detect, absorb, reflect, and respond to light.

Plants transduce light into chemical activity. Eyes convert light into neural signals. Circadian systems respond to cycles of illumination and darkness. Bioluminescent organisms generate visible light through biochemical reactions.

Within the metaphysical architecture, Biological Light also names luminosity embodied through living process. It is light entering [[ii-3|CHON-based organisation]] and becoming metabolism, orientation, signalling, behaviour, and ecological relation.

The symbolic category should remain grounded in biology rather than being used to replace it.

Death appears as the withdrawal of organised vitality from a body. Warmth decreases, responsiveness ceases, metabolic coordination collapses, and the physical form begins returning to wider material cycles.

Many traditions describe a corresponding release of soul, spirit, breath, radiance, double, or subtle body. Egyptian mortuary theology seeks the transformation of the dead into an effective [[xv-10|akh]]. Christian traditions speak of entering divine light. Tibetan traditions describe [[xv-69|clear light]] associated with death. Manichaean cosmology treats death within the larger liberation of captive light.

These teachings should be understood according to their own systems before being combined. Yet they share the intuition that death is not simply the arrival of darkness. It is a separation of previously joined principles.

## What separates

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, death releases the organised relation among physical, etheric, astral, psychic, and spiritual components. The body's dissolution does not necessarily imply the immediate extinction of every higher vehicle or [[ix-24|retained imprint]].

Light and death therefore meet in withdrawal, exposure, and transition. Death reveals which qualities belonged to the temporary architecture, which remain in the field as consequences, and which continue with the soul.

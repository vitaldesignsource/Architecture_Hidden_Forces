The comparative mysteries ask what occurs when light enters relation with another primordial principle. Light alone reveals; fire transforms; aether transmits; breath animates; sound orders; word names; number measures; geometry gives boundary; memory retains; and life embodies. Each pairing produces a distinct operation.

Comparison must preserve difference. Two traditions may employ similar images without teaching the same doctrine, and two forces may correspond without being identical. The goal is to discover real structural kinships while resisting false equivalence.

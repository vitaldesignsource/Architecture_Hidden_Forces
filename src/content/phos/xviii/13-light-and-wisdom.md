Knowledge receives and organises information. Wisdom discerns order, proportion, purpose, consequence, and right relation. Light becomes wisdom when illumination is integrated deeply enough to guide action.

Sophia is not merely the possession of luminous concepts. She is the intelligence that knows how force should enter form, when revelation should be spoken, what a receiver can bear, and how a part belongs within the whole.

Solar wisdom reveals the architecture of things. [[vii-12|Lunar wisdom]] receives, reflects, gestates, and interprets. Their conjunction produces an intelligence capable of both seeing and embodying.

## Receptivity as power

[[xii-18|The divine feminine as radiant wisdom]] should not be confined to passivity. The mirror actively receives; the womb actively forms; the soul translates spiritual light into living meaning. Receptivity is a power of transformation.

Within [[ii-22|Ignisophia]], fire and Sophia unite. Fire supplies the power to transform; wisdom governs its proportion and aim. Without Sophia, fire becomes indiscriminate. Without fire, wisdom may remain unactualised.

A wise light is recognised by timing, proportion, and consequence. It reveals what can be integrated, conceals what requires protection, and never mistakes maximum exposure for maximum truth.

Immortality is not merely endless duration. A process can continue indefinitely while remaining fragmented, unconscious, or bound to repetition. True immortality implies participation in an order not exhausted by temporal change.

[[vii-14|Stars]] became symbols of immortality because they appeared imperishable compared with earthly life. Solar cycles represented renewal. [[xvii-27|Gold]] represented incorruptibility. Luminous garments and celestial bodies represented forms capable of bearing life beyond ordinary decay.

Traditions distinguish several kinds of survival: bodily resurrection, continued soul existence, ancestral presence, reincarnation, astral persistence, deification, union with God, or transformation into an immortal luminous body. These should never be treated as synonyms.

## What actually persists

Within the system, immortality depends upon coherent participation in higher being. A psychic image may persist without containing the full person. An astral shell may preserve habits without spiritual intelligence. [[ix-24|Residual light]] may remain after a form's dissolution without constituting conscious survival.

The immortal element is not whatever lasts longest but what participates most deeply in the source that does not perish. Luminous immortality therefore requires integration, purification, and a form capable of remaining truthful beyond the conditions that produced the temporary personality.

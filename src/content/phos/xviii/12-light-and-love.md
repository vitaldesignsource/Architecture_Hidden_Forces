Love is light entering relation without ceasing to recognise the other. Light reveals; love affirms the value of what is revealed. At its highest, love does not possess the illuminated being but helps it become more fully itself.

Many traditions connect divine love with radiance, warmth, beauty, attraction, and overflowing goodness. [[xv-28|Neoplatonic procession]] arises from superabundance and returns through desire for the source. Christian light becomes sacrificial love. [[xv-82|Sufi illumination]] becomes longing, intimacy, annihilation of possessive ego, and abiding in God.

Love may be compared to gravitational attraction or resonance, but it should not be reduced to either. Physical attraction is not moral care, and resonant systems do not automatically love one another. The correspondence lies in relation, orientation, and the capacity of separated beings to enter unity without complete erasure.

## Right relation experienced inwardly

Within the system, love is [[ii-25|the Law of Right Relation]] experienced inwardly. It prevents illumination from becoming cold exposure and power from becoming domination.

A being may possess brilliance, knowledge, or visionary intensity without love. Such luminosity remains incomplete and may become dangerous. True higher light increases the capacity to perceive another being without reducing it to an instrument of one's own desire.

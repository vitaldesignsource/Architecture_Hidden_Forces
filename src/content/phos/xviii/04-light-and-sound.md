Light and sound are both wave-bearing phenomena in physical science, but they are not the same kind of wave. Light is electromagnetic and can cross a vacuum. Ordinary sound is mechanical and requires a material medium. Their physical velocities, modes of transmission, and interactions differ profoundly.

Metaphysically, however, they express complementary operations. Light reveals simultaneously across a field; sound unfolds sequentially through time. Light gives form to space; sound articulates relation through rhythm. Light shows; sound calls.

Creation traditions frequently begin with voice, vibration, command, song, or word. The world is not merely exposed by light but ordered through utterance. Light makes the formed world visible; sound represents the process through which its relations are measured, distinguished, and harmonised.

## Tone ether

[[ii-7|Tone ether]] occupies the bridge between these mysteries. It should not be identified with ordinary acoustic vibration. It is the formative power of interval, ratio, affinity, separation, and recombination, of which audible harmony is one sensible expression.

When light and sound are ritually joined, image and invocation cooperate. The visible symbol provides spatial form; [[xiv-18|the spoken name]] awakens and directs its current. A silent diagram may contain potential order, while the correctly related word sets that order into motion.

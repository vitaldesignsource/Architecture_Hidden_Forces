Resurrection is light returning to or recreating form after death. It differs from simple survival. Survival means that something continues; resurrection means that life reconstitutes embodiment or appears in a transformed body.

Solar resurrection follows the pattern of descent, nocturnal regeneration, and dawn. Agricultural resurrection follows seed, burial, dissolution, germination, and renewed growth. Christian resurrection concerns Christ's victory over death and the promised transformation of embodied life. Egyptian mortuary religion concerns reassembly, animation, justification, and transfiguration into an akh.

These patterns resemble one another, but resemblance does not prove direct identity. Solar cycles repeat naturally; Christian resurrection is a particular historical and theological claim; Egyptian transfiguration belongs to its own ritual and divine order.

## A supreme coagula

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, resurrection is a supreme form of [[xiii-23|coagula]]. Force released from a failed architecture does not merely return to its previous condition. It receives a renewed form capable of embodying a higher degree of coherence.

The test of resurrection is transformation. If the exact disorder simply reappears, there has been repetition rather than rebirth. True resurrection preserves identity while freeing it from the form that could no longer bear its light.

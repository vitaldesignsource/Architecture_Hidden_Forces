Illumination reveals the present form; transformation alters its organisation. The two can occur separately. A person may see clearly and remain unchanged, or undergo violent change without understanding what is happening.

True transformation unites revelation with reformation. Light identifies what must be released, fire separates it, darkness protects the period of dissolution, and formative power gives the liberated force a new architecture.

This is *solve et coagula*. [[xiii-22|Solve]] releases light from a form that has become restrictive, exhausted, or false. [[xiii-23|Coagula]] does not imprison that light again in the same structure. It creates a more adequate vessel.

## Two depths of change

*Metamorphōsis* changes the pattern by which a being appears. [[xiii-25|Metastoicheiōsis]], trans-elementation, changes the elemental conditions through which the being is constituted. The second is therefore more radical than alteration of surface appearance.

Within the system, transformation is truthful only when the new form embodies a genuinely higher relation. Increased intensity, complexity, psychic power, or visionary brilliance does not by itself constitute advancement.

The fruit of luminous transformation is greater coherence among being, meaning, action, and relation.

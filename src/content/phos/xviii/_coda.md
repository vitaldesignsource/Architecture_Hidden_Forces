## Concluding doctrine: what comparison can and cannot establish

Twenty-one pairings, from fire to return, and one closing warning. The division's method has been consistent: place light beside another great term, find where the two genuinely illuminate each other, and mark exactly where the resemblance stops.

The pairings that hold do so for a reason that can be stated. Light and breath converge because both cross freely between world and organism and both are received rather than seized. Light and word converge because both make something present that was not present before, and both can be false. Light and death converge because both concern what a form can and cannot carry. In each case the convergence is structural, not verbal.

The pairings that fail, fail in the same way each time. Two traditions use a bright word; an English translation renders both by the same term; and a correspondence is announced that neither tradition would recognise. The word did the work that the argument should have done.

This division therefore carries a lower confidence than most of the encyclopaedia, and says so. A structural resemblance well described is a real finding and a genuine instrument of understanding. It is not an identity, and it does not become one by being repeated.

> Two things that behave alike in one respect have shown you that respect, and nothing further.

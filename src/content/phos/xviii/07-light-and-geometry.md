Geometry gives light direction, proportion, containment, and relation. [[xvii-1|The point]] concentrates; the line transmits; the circle radiates; [[xvii-5|the triangle]] stabilises; the square establishes a field; the spiral transforms recurrence into development; and the sphere gathers extension around a centre.

Physical optics confirms that geometry governs illumination. Aperture, angle, curvature, distance, surface, and refractive index determine how rays form images. Sacred geometry extends this insight symbolically and ritually: a structure's proportions determine how spiritual meaning and embodied attention are organised.

Yet geometry is not automatically sacred. A precise figure can be spiritually empty, while an imperfect hand-drawn symbol can become powerful through living participation. Sacred geometry arises when measure, material, orientation, intention, and higher correspondence enter right relation.

## Frozen relation

Within the system, geometry is frozen relation waiting for activation. Light enters and reveals the architecture's capacities. A circle becomes [[xiv-23|a boundary]]; a triangle directs force; a cross establishes axes; [[xvii-6|a vesica]] opens mediation; a mandorla forms a vehicle of transfiguration.

Geometry is therefore one of the principal ways force becomes truthful form. When the geometry contradicts the operation, force is distorted. When geometry and force correspond, the figure becomes a stable luminous interface.

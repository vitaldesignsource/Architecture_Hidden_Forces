Life receives, converts, stores, emits, and organises light. [[xvi-20|Photosynthesis]] transforms solar radiation into chemical potential. Circadian systems translate environmental light into biological time. Vision transforms photons into meaningful perception. Some organisms become luminous sources through [[xvi-21|bioluminescence]].

Metaphysically, life is more than illuminated matter. It is matter organised by an inwardly coherent formative activity. A living organism maintains identity through continuous material exchange, repairs damage, develops according to inherited tendencies, and responds adaptively to its environment.

Light supplies energy and information, but the organism determines how they are received. The seed does not simply absorb sunlight; it unfolds a latent architecture through its relation with light, warmth, water, gravity, soil, and surrounding life.

## Coherence, not visible glow

Within the system, [[ix-18|the etheric body]] is the real vital-formative field through which living unity is sustained. Physical light participates in its earthly conditions but should not be equated with the whole etheric organisation.

[[ix-20|Health as luminous coherence]] means that physical, etheric, psychic, and spiritual forces maintain workable relations. It does not mean that healthy people must visibly glow or that illness always proves weak spiritual light. Illness may arise at any level or through interaction among several levels.

Initiation conducts a person from one mode of perception and participation into another. Darkness, blindfolding, enclosure, symbolic death, instruction, testing, unveiling, and the sudden appearance of light recur in many initiatory traditions.

The candidate does not merely learn secret information. Ideally, the candidate's [[v-28|capacity to receive and embody force]] is altered. The secret matters because premature disclosure can reduce a living operation to curiosity or expose the unprepared person to powers they cannot integrate.

Light at the conclusion of initiation represents recognition: the world appears again, but the initiate's place within it has changed. The same objects may now bear different meaning because the receiver has been reorganised.

## The discipline of comparison

Historical mystery traditions varied enormously, and secrecy limits what can be reconstructed confidently. Comparative study must avoid inventing one universal initiation behind Eleusinian, Mithraic, Hermetic, Masonic, African, Indigenous, yogic, and occult rites. [Disciplined comparative religion](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/religion-comparative/) requires attention to differences as well as similarities.

Within the system, initiation strengthens the vessel. Its aim is not to produce an intense moment alone but to establish a lasting new relation among spirit, soul, etheric body, psyche, and embodied life.

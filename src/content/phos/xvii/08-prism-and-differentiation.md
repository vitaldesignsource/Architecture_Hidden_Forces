The prism symbolises the disclosure of multiplicity within apparently unified light. White light enters; differentiated colours emerge. The prism does not simply invent these distinctions, but neither is it irrelevant to their appearance. Its material and geometry provide the conditions under which they become visible.

Metaphysically, the prism is a structure of mediation. A higher unity enters a lower architecture and appears as a spectrum of differentiated qualities. Divine unity becomes logoi, archetypes, rays, planetary powers, elements, colours, faculties, and formed beings.

The prism also warns against confusing the differentiated ray with the entirety of the source. A single colour can be authentic without being complete. Error begins when a particular ray claims to contain all luminosity.

## Prismatic levels

Within the Fourfold Veil, each level may function prismatically. Spirit is refracted through soul; soul through the etheric organisation; etheric activity through bodily matter. Each transition reveals possibilities while introducing limitations.

The prism should therefore become one of the principal symbols of [[ii-8|tattvic differentiation]]. Tattvas are not separate substances added to an otherwise colourless force. They are morphogenic biases through which the same proceeding power assumes different directions, qualities, densities, and forms.

The torch is portable fire. It differs from the stationary lamp because it carries illumination from one place to another. It symbolises initiation, succession, teaching, pilgrimage, and the movement of living knowledge through history.

One torch can ignite another without diminishing its own flame. This makes it an especially clear image of radiance without depletion. Genuine transmission multiplies participation rather than dividing a finite possession.

Yet the torch also exposes the danger of succession without understanding. A flame can be passed while its purpose is forgotten. Initiatic lineage is not secured solely by possessing names, titles, documents, or ritual implements. The recipient must actually receive and embody the current.

## Handle and flame

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, the torch represents a transmitted current joined to a bearer. Its handle is the lineage's formed structure; the flame is the living force. When either is absent, the transmission is incomplete.

The torch should therefore signify both continuity and responsibility. The bearer does not own the flame. The bearer protects it, carries it through difficult terrain, and offers it to those capable of receiving it.

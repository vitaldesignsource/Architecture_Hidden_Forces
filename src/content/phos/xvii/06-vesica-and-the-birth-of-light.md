The vesica piscis is produced when two circles of equal radius overlap so that the centre of each lies upon the circumference of the other. The almond-shaped intersection is neither circle considered separately. It is a third region born through their relation.

Symbolically, the vesica represents manifestation through meeting: heaven and earth, spirit and matter, source and receiver, invisible and visible, solar and lunar, or divine and human. Light appears within the interval where two complete fields become mutually present.

The vesica can therefore be interpreted as an aperture or [[vi-10|womb]]. It is open enough to permit passage but bounded enough to give the emerging force a determinate form. Its vertical form became especially important in Christian [[xvii-17|mandorlas]] surrounding Christ or Mary, although not every historical use of the shape carried the same geometrical interpretation.

## Where force becomes form

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, the vesica is a primary symbol of [[ii-5|Morphaithēr]]. It shows how a living intermediate atmosphere arises through the overlap of orders. The subtle world is not necessarily an independent layer inserted between spirit and matter; it may be the real relational field generated where they interpenetrate.

The centre of the vesica is the place where force becomes form without ceasing to be force. It is conception, mediation, and luminous birth.

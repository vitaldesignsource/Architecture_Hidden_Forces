The mirror receives an image without permanently possessing it. It reveals what stands before it while remaining distinct from what it reveals. It therefore symbolises consciousness, self-knowledge, imagination, purity, divination, and reflective wisdom.

A clean mirror returns a clear image. A warped mirror distorts. A broken mirror fragments. A stained mirror obscures. These variations make it an exact image of the soul's receptive condition.

Mystical traditions frequently describe the heart or intellect as a mirror polished through discipline. The purpose is not to invent divine light but to remove what prevents its truthful reflection.

## The danger of the surface

Yet a mirror can reverse and seduce. It can encourage fixation upon appearance or mistake reflection for source. Narcissus does not fail because reflection is unreal; he fails because he cannot establish the correct relation to it.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, [[x-13|imagination]] is a living mirror between higher intelligence and bodily consciousness. It can receive authentic symbolic forms, reproduce memories, or generate projections. Discernment asks what stands before the mirror, what condition the mirror is in, and whether the observer recognises the image as mediation.

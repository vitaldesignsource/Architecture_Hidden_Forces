The point is light before extension. Geometrically it possesses position without measurable length, width, or depth. Metaphysically it signifies concentrated potential: the first determinacy emerging from an immeasurable source.

A point of light appears small only from the perspective of manifested space. Symbolically it may contain an entire order before that order has unfolded. The first luminous point is therefore not a fragment broken from the source. It is the source's first concentrated appearance - the moment at which the unbounded becomes locatable without yet becoming divided.

In Kabbalistic symbolism, Kether can be understood as such a primordial concentration beneath the veils of limitless light. In Neoplatonic language, it resembles the first intelligible determination proceeding from unity. In cosmological imagery, it is the germinal star within the darkness before the architecture of worlds develops.

## Seed of geometry

Within the present system, the point corresponds to [[iii-1|fontal light]] entering the first threshold of manifestation. It is the seed of geometry, number, orientation, and form. Because every line begins from a point, every extended luminous operation carries something of this first concentration within it.

The point is also a symbol of [[x-8|attention]]. Dispersed awareness becomes operative when gathered into a centre. Yet concentration must eventually proceed. A point that refuses extension becomes sterile contraction; a point that radiates without losing its centre becomes a living source.

Flame is visible transformation. Fuel, oxygen, heat, motion, colour, and emitted light are united in one rapidly changing form. It appears stable while continually consuming and renewing its material conditions.

Spiritually, flame symbolises purification, sacrifice, intelligence, desire, vitality, destruction, and consecration. These meanings are not identical. Fire [[xiii-11|purifies]] by separating, transforms by altering composition, and [[xiii-12|consecrates]] when its operation establishes a thing within sacred relation.

The flame's vertical motion makes it an image of aspiration. Its roots remain attached to fuel while its body reaches upward. It exists at the threshold between matter and radiance.

## Correct fire

Within Ignisophia, flame is the dynamic wisdom that knows how to transform without merely annihilating. Unregulated fire consumes its vessel. Insufficient fire leaves the substance unchanged. Correct fire reveals, separates, matures, and renews.

Its colours can also indicate zones of reaction, temperature, and material composition. Symbolically, this teaches that one flame may contain multiple operations at once: hidden heat at the base, intense transformation in the body, and released radiance at the summit.

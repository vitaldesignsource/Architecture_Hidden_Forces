A luminous sphere extends the solar circle into three dimensions. It represents a source whose presence is not confined to one frontal direction but surrounds itself with a complete field.

The sphere is both centre and atmosphere. Its innermost point contains maximum concentration; its surface marks the current limit of transmission. Between them lie gradations of intensity, density, and participation.

Visionary accounts frequently describe [[xi-15|spheres, globes, pearls, eggs, suns, and self-contained fields of light]]. Such forms may symbolise psychic integration, celestial beings, concentrated powers, subtle vehicles, or intelligences capable of sustaining a complete luminous environment around themselves.

## A centred wholeness

A genuine sphere of radiance should not be understood simply as a bright ball floating in physical space. It may represent an organised field whose centre is conscious and whose circumference is functional. Its boundary distinguishes it from its surroundings without making it absolutely separate.

Within Morphaithēr, the sphere is the natural figure of a coherent being. The physical body, [[ix-18|etheric body]], astral atmosphere, and spiritual presence may produce interpenetrating spheres with different boundaries. These are not necessarily smooth or static; "sphere" names their centred wholeness.

The symbol also provides a test. A coherent sphere radiates from a stable centre. A distorted field may contain tears, projections, excessive density, asymmetry, or currents that no longer return harmoniously to their source.

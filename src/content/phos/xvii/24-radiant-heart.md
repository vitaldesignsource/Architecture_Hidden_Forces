The radiant heart places the source of illumination within the centre of affect, relation, courage, and interior life. Unlike [[xvii-15|the radiant crown]], which emphasises authority and intelligibility, the heart emphasises love, participation, sacrifice, and living connection.

Christian images of the Sacred Heart depict Christ's heart aflame, radiant, wounded, crowned with thorns, and surmounted by a cross. These elements unite suffering, love, fire, and inexhaustible giving. [[xv-82|Sufi traditions]] treat the *qalb* as a subtle organ capable of remembrance, unveiling, and divine reflection. Indian traditions place luminous or lotus-like imagery around the heart centre. These systems should be compared without being declared identical.

The heart radiates through circulation. It receives and distributes. Biologically, it preserves life by rhythmic exchange; symbolically, it distributes psychic and spiritual vitality through the whole person.

## The central chamber

Within the system, the radiant heart is the central chamber in which force becomes meaning and power becomes relation. It prevents solar intelligence from becoming cold domination and emotional life from becoming uncentred turbulence.

The heart's light is tested by what it nourishes. Genuine radiance increases the capacity for truth, courage, compassion, and faithful relation.

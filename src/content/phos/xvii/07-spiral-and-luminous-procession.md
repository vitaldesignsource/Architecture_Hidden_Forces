The spiral joins recurrence with movement. A circle returns to the same path; a spiral returns in altered relation. It therefore expresses emanation, evolution, recollection, and transformation through cycles.

An outward-moving spiral symbolises [[v-22|luminous procession]]: unity expanding into increasing multiplicity. An inward spiral symbolises [[v-23|return]]: scattered experience gathered back toward its centre. A double spiral can show both operations as simultaneous currents.

The spiral also appears throughout nature - in galaxies, storms, shells, whirlpools, plant growth, and patterns of biological organisation. These forms do not prove a universal hidden doctrine, but they demonstrate that rotation combined with translation is a fundamental way in which matter and energy organise movement.

## Momentum and compulsion

Within the Psychic Flywheel, spiral motion is especially important. Psychic force rarely proceeds only in straight lines. Repetition creates momentum; memory returns; desires circle their objects; attention may tighten toward concentration or widen into dissipation.

A healthy luminous spiral maintains communication with its centre while expanding. A pathological spiral repeats without transformation, drawing energy into compulsion. The distinction between a spiral and a closed circle is therefore the possibility of genuine change.

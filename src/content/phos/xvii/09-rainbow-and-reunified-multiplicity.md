The rainbow is differentiated unity made visible within a natural atmosphere. Sunlight, water droplets, observer, angle, reflection, refraction, and dispersion must enter a precise relationship before the bow appears.

It therefore belongs neither exclusively to the sun nor to the rain. It is an event generated through their meeting and completed from the observer's position. Each observer sees a slightly different rainbow because the relevant light reaches each eye through different droplets.

Historically, the rainbow has signified covenant, divine bridge, celestial weapon, road between worlds, differentiated glory, and restored harmony. These meanings arise in distinct cultures and should not be treated as if they descended from one original doctrine.

## The arc that holds the spectrum

Within this system, the rainbow is the spectrum reintegrated into an arc. Unlike [[xvii-8|the prism]], which emphasises differentiation, the rainbow shows differentiated qualities held within one continuous order. No colour loses its distinction, yet no colour exists independently of the bow.

The rainbow is therefore an image of successful [[xiii-23|coagula]]. What was separated becomes a higher unity without being homogenised. It represents plurality brought into right relation.

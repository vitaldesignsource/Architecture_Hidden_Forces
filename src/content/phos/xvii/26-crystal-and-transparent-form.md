Crystal joins matter with ordered transparency. Its internal lattice gives it definite structure, while its optical properties permit transmission, refraction, reflection, polarisation, or spectral differentiation.

Symbolically, crystal is matter that has become truthful to light. It does not cease to be formed, but its form allows illumination to pass through and reveal hidden geometries. Transparency is therefore not formlessness. It is highly ordered embodiment.

Different crystals affect light differently. Some split rays, rotate [[xvi-11|polarisation]], produce interference colours, fluoresce, or guide transmission. Their symbolic power arises partly from these real physical behaviours and partly from their durability, rarity, geometry, and traditional [[xiv-9|ritual uses]].

## Selective transparency

Within this system, the crystal is an ideal symbol of the spiritualised body. Matter is not discarded; it is clarified and ordered until it can bear a higher intensity without opacity or collapse.

The crystal also teaches that transparency is selective. A material may transmit one wavelength and absorb another. Likewise, a developed soul may be open to certain powers yet remain opaque to others. True transparency requires more than brightness - it requires structural correspondence.

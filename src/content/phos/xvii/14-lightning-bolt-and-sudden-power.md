Lightning is light appearing as violent passage. It joins heaven and earth through a path formed almost instantaneously. It therefore symbolises divine command, judgment, inspiration, revelation, sovereignty, and the sudden release of accumulated force.

Across cultures, storm gods wield lightning as a sign of authority. Zeus, Jupiter, Indra, Baal, and other figures possess thunder or lightning weapons, although their mythologies must not be treated as interchangeable.

Lightning differs from steady solar illumination. The sun reveals through continuity; lightning reveals through interruption. For a moment, an entire landscape becomes visible, then darkness returns. [[vii-25|Sudden revelation]] may therefore disclose more than the mind can immediately integrate.

## What follows the flash

Within the system, lightning represents force exceeding the prepared pathways of gradual transmission. It can initiate, awaken, or shatter. Its spiritual value depends upon what follows. A momentary vision becomes transformative only when the receiver builds an architecture capable of retaining and embodying its disclosure.

Lightning is therefore revelation without guarantee of integration. It shows why intensity is not identical with attainment.

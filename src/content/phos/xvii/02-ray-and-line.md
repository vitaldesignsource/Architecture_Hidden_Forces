A line is a point placed into motion. A luminous ray adds direction, linking a source with something capable of receiving its influence. It is therefore the most elementary geometry of relation.

The ray expresses [[iii-2|procession]]. It shows that light does not merely exist in self-contained brilliance but proceeds outward, crosses an interval, and establishes contact. Unlike an isolated line, however, a ray retains reference to an origin. Its direction communicates where the illumination comes from and toward what it moves.

In sacred art, rays frequently extend from suns, stars, divine figures, eyes, hands, wounds, names, or heavenly openings. The ray may bestow life, authority, knowledge, judgment, healing, or consecration. [[xv-4|Aten's rays]] terminate in hands because solar radiance is represented as an active power that reaches, gives, and sustains.

## Straight, broken, converging

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, a ray is a directed current passing through Morphaithēr. Its original quality may be spiritual, planetary, psychic, etheric, or elemental. As it crosses the Fourfold Veil, it can be [[viii-16|refracted]], polarised, absorbed, or clothed in symbolic form.

A straight ray signifies undivided intention. A broken ray signifies interrupted transmission. Converging rays concentrate power; diverging rays distribute it. Crossing rays establish an event of relationship. The luminous line is therefore not merely distance - it is the active bond between source and manifestation.

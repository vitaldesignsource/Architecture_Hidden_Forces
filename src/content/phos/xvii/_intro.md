Light becomes symbolically intelligible when it enters geometry, image, material, and architecture. A point concentrates it; a ray directs it; a circle contains it; a prism differentiates it; a mirror returns it; a lamp houses it; and a temple choreographs its movement through space and time.

These forms are not interchangeable decorations. Each expresses a different luminous operation. Historical meanings must also be preserved: the Egyptian solar barque, Jewish menorah, Christian mandorla, Indian lotus, and alchemical black sun arose within distinct traditions. The Architecture of Hidden Forces can place them in correspondence without erasing their native identities.

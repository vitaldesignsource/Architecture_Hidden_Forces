The black sun is a paradox: the source of light represented as dark. It may signify eclipsed power, the sun at midnight, solar descent into the underworld, hidden fire, the alchemical *sol niger*, or an illumination so interior or transcendent that it appears as darkness to ordinary sight.

In alchemical symbolism, the black sun is frequently associated with nigredo, putrefaction, death, and the withdrawal of visible vitality. Yet the hidden solar principle has not necessarily been destroyed. It operates within decomposition, releasing what had been imprisoned in an exhausted form.

The black sun should not be equated automatically with evil. It can represent latent or apophatic luminosity. But it may also become destructive when used to glorify annihilation, domination, or nihilism.

A modern emblem also called the "Black Sun" or Sonnenrad has been appropriated by Nazi and neo-Nazi movements. That political symbol must be distinguished explicitly from older alchemical, mystical, astronomical, and underworld images of the hidden sun.

## Ignisophia concealed

Within this system, the black sun is Ignisophia during concealment. It is the inner source operating when outer identity, certainty, and form have failed. Its test is regeneration: genuine hidden illumination prepares a more truthful dawn; counterfeit darkness only consumes.

Shadow arises because a formed being cannot express every possibility simultaneously. To become one thing is to leave other possibilities unmanifest. A particular ray illuminates one direction more strongly than another. A structure that protects also limits. A symbol that reveals one relation may conceal others.

## Generative darkness

Shadow is therefore not automatically evil. It can be the necessary consequence of embodiment, distinction, privacy, incubation, rest, and protection. Generative darkness allows unformed life to develop without premature exposure. The womb, cave, crypt, night, and hidden seed are not enemies of light. They are chambers in which light prepares another mode of appearance — the reading [[vi-10|darkness as womb]] and [[vi-9|darkness as latency]] give in full.

## When shadow becomes pathological

Necessary shadow becomes pathological when it is denied, projected, exploited, or rendered permanently unconscious. An architecture that declares itself wholly luminous becomes incapable of recognising the darkness produced by its own boundaries. It begins to locate all obscurity outside itself and may persecute others as embodiments of the shadow it refuses to acknowledge.

The mature path does not abolish shadow. It brings shadow into truthful relation with the whole so that concealed forces no longer govern through denial.

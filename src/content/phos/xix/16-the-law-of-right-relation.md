Right relation is the central law of the entire system. A force is not judged solely by its magnitude, origin, or appearance. Its truth depends upon how it relates. Fire can warm a home or consume it. Authority can protect a community or subordinate it. Vision can guide the soul or inflate the visionary. Even a genuinely spiritual force may become destructive when invoked without proportion, preparation, ethical orientation, or concern for the receiver — the principle [[ii-25|light and the Law of Right Relation]] first states within the system.

## Not passivity, not perpetual harmony

Right relation does not mean passivity or perpetual harmony. It may require boundary, judgment, confrontation, withdrawal, protection, or the dissolution of a corrupted form. Harmony without truth is only suppressed conflict. The aim is not the elimination of tension but its placement within an order capable of generating greater life and consciousness.

## The questions every operation must answer

Every operation of light can be evaluated through this law. One must ask what it joins, what it separates, what it nourishes, what it consumes, what it reveals, what it conceals, and what it requires from those who participate in it.

To ask whether a practice works is insufficient. One must ask what it costs, whom it serves, what kind of consciousness it cultivates, and whether its effects remain truthful over time.

Coherence does not mean uniformity. A living organism contains many organs, rhythms, fluids, structures, and functions. A soul contains thought, desire, memory, imagination, will, and affect. Coherence occurs when differentiated parts participate in an intelligible whole without losing their proper character — the quality [[iv-6|the entry on coherence]] defines.

## When the levels agree

Luminous coherence arises when spirit, soul, astral life, etheric organisation, and bodily action begin to agree. What is known inwardly becomes increasingly expressible ethically, relationally, and materially. The person does not speak of compassion while acting cruelly, claim spiritual freedom while producing dependency, or celebrate truth while refusing correction. Light becomes coherent when its radiance reaches conduct.

## Disturbance is not disproof

A powerful experience may initially disrupt ordinary organisation. Illumination can expose contradictions, release buried material, and dissolve structures that previously held the personality together. Temporary disturbance does not automatically invalidate the event.

The deeper test is developmental: does the experience eventually generate greater integration, honesty, freedom, and capacity for relationship, or does it produce escalating fragmentation, grandiosity, deception, and isolation? This is the test [[xx-11|testing luminous experiences by their consequences]] turns into practice.

Coherent radiance does not demand that every level express light in the same way. It requires that each level contribute truthfully to a greater living order.

Captive light may exist within traumatic memory, compulsive desire, a dead institution, an inherited psychic structure, an exhausted symbol, or matter organised through violence. The light has not vanished. Its power remains enclosed, fragmented, appropriated, or forced to sustain an architecture opposed to its original purpose — the condition [[v-20|the gradation named captive light]] locates among the orders of light.

## The Ossuary and the Crypt

Within the Ossuary of Living Forms, abandoned structures retain residues of former animation. Some are empty shells. Others contain luminous impressions, ancestral patterns, unfinished intentions, or forces awaiting release. The Crypt of Primordial Memory likewise preserves potentials that may be recovered when the proper key, relation, word, or act of recollection is found. [[ii-28|Residual light in the Ossuary]] and [[ii-27|light in the Crypt]] treat the two chambers in turn.

## Liberation is not demolition

Liberation does not mean simply destroying the container. A form may need to be opened, purified, mourned, translated, fulfilled, or consciously dissolved. Solve releases the bound force; coagula provides it with a more truthful embodiment. Liberation without reformation may scatter what has been recovered. Reformation without liberation merely gives captivity a new appearance.

Captive light is freed when force can once again participate in living circulation without being compelled to preserve the structure of its imprisonment.

A teacher illuminates a student, but the student's questions reveal dimensions of the teaching that might otherwise remain hidden. Consciousness illuminates an object, yet the object gives consciousness something through which to recognise its own powers. The sun reveals the world, while the colours, forms, and lives of the world reveal what solar radiance can generate.

## Reciprocity without equality of function

Reciprocity does not mean equality of function. The source and receiver may occupy distinct orders. Yet manifestation is relational: the receiver gives the ray a place to become particular. Through response, reflection, resistance, transformation, and embodiment, it reveals possibilities implicit within the illuminating power — the movement [[x-22|self-knowledge as the turning of light upon its source]] describes at the level of the soul.

## Even resistance illuminates

Even resistance may illuminate. A boundary reveals the direction and pressure of the force that encounters it. A failed form may disclose an inadequacy in the way light was mediated. A misunderstanding may reveal where the communication between levels broke down.

## The ethical consequence

This law has ethical consequences. No being should be treated as passive matter for another's spiritual project. Even guidance must remain attentive to what the guided being reveals. Illumination that refuses reciprocity becomes domination. Authentic radiance awakens the other's own centre rather than demanding permanent dependence upon an external source.

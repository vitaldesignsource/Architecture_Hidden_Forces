## The complete cycle

Taken together, the Laws of Luminous Architecture describe a complete cycle.

Light proceeds without depletion. It enters mediation. It is refracted through subtle conditions and received according to capacity. It forms, reveals, vivifies, and leaves memory. It encounters shadow, limitation, and the possibility of captivity. Through consecration and right relation, it becomes coherently embodied. When embodiment ceases to be truthful, the light withdraws, is liberated, and returns — carrying the fulfilled meaning of its passage through form.

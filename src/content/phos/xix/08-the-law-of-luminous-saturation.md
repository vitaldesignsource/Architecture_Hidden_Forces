More light is not always better. Eyes adapted to darkness can be blinded by sudden brilliance. Inwardly, an intensity exceeding the capacity of the nervous system, soul, astral body, etheric organisation, or symbolic understanding may produce dazzlement, exhaustion, dissociation, confusion, compulsive interpretation, or psychic disorganisation — the condition [[v-29|saturation, excess, and luminous overload]] describes among the gradations.

## Disproportion, not disproof

Luminous saturation does not necessarily prove that the encountered light was false. It may indicate a disproportion between intensity and capacity. The receiver may require gradual preparation, bodily restoration, ethical integration, interpretive restraint, or time for the experience to distribute itself through the entire architecture.

## Why initiation enlarges before it intensifies

The purpose of initiation is partly to enlarge capacity without violently breaking the vessel. Training establishes channels through which greater force can circulate without becoming trapped in one faculty or centre. A revelation that remains concentrated in the imagination, for example, may generate obsession. When it descends into thought, feeling, conduct, body, and relationship, it can become integrated knowledge.

## The teacher's responsibility

This law places responsibility upon teachers and ritual practitioners. It is dangerous to force intensity upon an unprepared person and call the resulting destabilisation purification. Genuine theurgy respects measure, sequence, consent, timing, and integration. Light should be given in the form and degree through which it can become life — and where it has already exceeded that degree, [[xx-15|when to suspend practice]] governs.

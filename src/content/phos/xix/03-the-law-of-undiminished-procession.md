A material flame diminishes as its fuel is consumed, but higher light does not necessarily operate through depletion. Knowledge can be communicated without leaving its knower empty. Love can radiate without ceasing to exist at its centre. A spiritual source may generate innumerable participations without being divided into fragments.

## Radiance without depletion

This is the law of radiance without depletion, set out as a first principle in [[i-11|the entry of that name]]. The source remains while its light proceeds. Its manifestations are neither entirely separate from it nor numerically identical with it. They participate according to their capacities. The one light can therefore become present within many beings without ceasing to be one at its origin.

## Procession against extraction

This law also distinguishes procession from extraction. A false architecture drains its members, environments, or subordinate beings so that a central power may appear radiant. Such a centre is not demonstrating undiminished procession; it is consuming concealed fuel. Its brilliance is purchased by the exhaustion of others.

Authentic spiritual radiance gives life without secretly impoverishing what receives it. Where apparent illumination requires the continual sacrifice of the vulnerable, one is not encountering a spiritual sun but predation disguised as solar authority — the counterfeit [[xix-19|the nineteenth law]] is written to expose.

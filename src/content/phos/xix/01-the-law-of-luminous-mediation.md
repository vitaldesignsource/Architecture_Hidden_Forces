Fontal light may be undivided in its source, but its appearance within a world requires a medium, relation, boundary, or receptive field. Between the unmanifest source and a particular visible form stand numerous mediations: noetic orders, spiritual beings, psychic structures, the astral field, the etheric body, the Fourfold Veil, tattvic atmospheres, biological organs, cultural symbols, and material conditions.

## Mediation is not corruption

Mediation is not necessarily corruption. It is the means by which the unbounded becomes communicable without ceasing to transcend its communication. A higher light does not become false merely because it assumes an image, word, gesture, body, or ritual form. Such forms allow the receiver to enter relationship with realities that could not otherwise be consciously endured or understood.

## Through the Morphaithēr and the Four Ethers

Within the CHON Architecture, light passes through Morphaithēr, the living atmosphere in which formative forces acquire direction, rhythm, and character. The Four Ethers mediate different aspects of this activity: warmth awakens and mobilises; light ether differentiates and spatially organises; chemical or tone ether establishes proportion and relational resonance; life ether maintains generative continuity. These are not pieces into which the source is mechanically divided. They are modes through which a deeper activity becomes operative in living form, as [[ii-7|the entry on the Four Ethers as modes of luminous mediation]] describes.

## The channel is part of the evidence

No mediator transmits with perfect neutrality. Every soul, symbol, ritual, institution, body, and world colours what it conveys. The study of light must therefore include the study of its channels. One cannot judge a revelation solely by the brilliance of its claimed origin. One must also examine the medium through which it descended and the form it ultimately produced.

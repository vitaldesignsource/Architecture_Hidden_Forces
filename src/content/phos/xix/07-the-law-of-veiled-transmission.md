A veil is not merely an obstruction. Some realities can enter a lower or denser order only by clothing themselves in forms that reduce their intensity. Symbol, myth, ritual, dream, body, language, and sacred image may all function as protective veils. They make contact possible without requiring the receiver to endure the source in its unmediated fullness.

## The veils of the person

The Fourfold Veil translates spiritual activity into warmth, luminous organisation, resonance, and living continuity, as [[ii-6|the entry on the Fourfold Veil as a structure of transmission]] sets out. The astral body veils intelligible force in image, emotion, sensation, and desire. The etheric body veils archetypal form in growth, rhythm, vitality, and development. The physical body veils living activity in boundary, density, resistance, and location. Each veil conceals one dimension while revealing another.

## When a veil claims to be the source

A veil becomes deceptive when it conceals its own mediating nature and claims to be the source itself. A sacred image becomes an idol in the destructive sense when the form demands the worship that belongs to the power it was created to transmit. A doctrine becomes opaque when it preserves the vocabulary of illumination while preventing living perception.

Discernment asks whether a veil remains transparent to what it was created to communicate. A traditional form may remain outwardly beautiful after its living relation has departed. Conversely, an unfamiliar or imperfect symbol may still transmit genuine light. The truth of a veil is determined by its relation to its source and by the life it makes possible.

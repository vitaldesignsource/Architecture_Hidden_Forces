One radiance may produce clarity in one being, terror in another, inflation in a third, and quiet transformation in a fourth. The difference does not necessarily reside in the source. It may arise from the structure through which the light is received. A transparent medium transmits; a turbulent medium scatters; a wounded medium may distort; an unprepared medium may be overwhelmed.

## Each level receives through its own faculty

Different levels of a person also receive light according to their proper faculties. The intellectual soul receives light as intelligibility. The imagination receives it as image and symbol. The ethical will receives it as obligation or direction. The astral body may receive it through colour, desire, movement, and visionary form. The etheric body may receive it as vitality, warmth, rhythm, expansion, or formative reorganisation. The physical body may register the encounter through tears, trembling, changes in breathing, exhaustion, wakefulness, or renewed strength.

## Different, but not equally accurate

Differential reception helps explain why traditions and individuals describe related spiritual encounters in radically different ways — the pattern [[v-27|the same light received at different levels]] traces through the gradations. Yet it does not mean that all interpretations are equally accurate. A reception can be partial, mixed, distorted, or counterfeit.

The proper question is not simply whether a light appeared. It is: what received it, through which level, under what conditions, and with what consequences?

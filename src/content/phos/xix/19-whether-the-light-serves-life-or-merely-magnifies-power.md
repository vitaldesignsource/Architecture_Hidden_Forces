Illumination can magnify intelligence without producing wisdom. It can intensify charisma without creating love. It can increase psychic perception while leaving desire disordered. It can strengthen an institution's influence while deepening its corruption. Power exposed to light may become more effective before it becomes more ethical.

## The test of life

The essential test is therefore whether light serves life. Does it increase the capacity for truthful relationship, responsible freedom, creativity, healing, understanding, and protection of what is vulnerable? Does it permit differentiation without fragmentation? Does it deepen reverence for living beings, or reduce them to instruments of an exalted will?

## Power is not the enemy

Power is not inherently opposed to light. Life requires power: the power to grow, resist destruction, establish boundaries, speak truth, create form, protect the innocent, and transform inherited conditions. The distinction lies between power as the servant of living order and power that demands life as its fuel — the extraction [[xix-3|the Law of Undiminished Procession]] names.

## Solar authority and its counterfeit

Solar authority nourishes what exists within its field. Counterfeit solarity requires everything else to orbit its self-image. One generates other centres of life; the other permits only reflections of itself. [[xii-16|The Solar King]] and [[xviii-14|light and authority]] treat the figure and the comparison respectively.

The question is therefore not whether the light is strong. It is whether its strength makes life more capable of becoming itself.

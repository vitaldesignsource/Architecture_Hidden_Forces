The soul is an interior field rather than a single faculty. Perception, memory, imagination, emotion, desire, attention, judgement, conscience, identity, and spiritual receptivity arise within it as differentiated but interacting powers.

"Interior" does not mean unreal or sealed inside the skull. The soul's interiority is a mode of presence. It includes relations to bodies, persons, places, symbols, ancestors, spiritual beings, and collective atmospheres. The soul possesses inward depth while continually participating in realities beyond its personal boundary.

## Regions of the field

Its field has regions of clarity and obscurity, centres and margins, thresholds and chambers, recurrent paths and forgotten depths. Some contents are immediately accessible; others remain latent until memory, dream, symbol, ritual, suffering, or revelation draws them forward.

The soul is also capable of carrying more than the waking personality recognises. Psychic life extends beneath deliberate thought, while spiritual receptivity extends above ordinary cognition. Consciousness occupies only part of the soul's total field at any moment.

The interior world is therefore not merely a private theatre of subjective inventions. It is a real field of encounter in which bodily, psychic, symbolic, ancestral, astral, and spiritual realities may become present.

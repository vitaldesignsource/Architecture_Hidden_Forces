Light becomes meaning when disclosure is received, interpreted, valued, and incorporated into a living centre. Physical light may reveal an object, but the soul recognises beauty, danger, memory, kinship, sacredness, loss, or possibility within what has been revealed.

Meaning is therefore neither entirely contained in the object nor arbitrarily invented by the observer. It arises through participation. Object, history, symbol, consciousness, condition, and field enter a relation through which significance becomes present.

## The continual transformation

The soul performs this transformation continually. Sensation becomes experience. Experience becomes memory. Memory becomes identity. Desire becomes direction. Suffering becomes knowledge or fragmentation. Symbol becomes revelation or glamour. Spiritual force becomes vocation, responsibility, and embodied action.

This is why the same event can produce different meanings without all interpretations being equally true. The capacity of the receiver, the condition of the field, the source of the illumination, and the structure of the soul influence what can be received.

The soul is thus the place where light ceases to be mere exposure and becomes inwardly consequential. It is:

> The place where force becomes meaning and where light becomes a life.

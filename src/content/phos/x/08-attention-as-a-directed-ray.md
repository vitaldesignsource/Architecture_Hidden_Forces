Attention is the directed ray of consciousness. It concentrates illumination upon a selected portion of the experiential field, strengthening some contents while allowing others to recede. What attention repeatedly illuminates becomes easier to perceive, remember, desire, and enact.

The ray of attention is real psychic directionality. It is not composed of photons, but neither is it merely a decorative comparison. Attention carries the formative power of consciousness toward an object. It can nourish a thought, intensify an emotion, activate a memory, strengthen a symbol, deepen perception, or sustain contact with a spiritual reality.

## Momentum and its ambiguity

Attention also participates in the formation of [[ii-24|the Psychic Flywheel]]. Repeated attention deposits momentum. A thought repeatedly attended becomes a path; a path becomes a tendency; a tendency becomes character; character shapes the forms through which future force can move.

Yet concentration is not automatically wisdom. Attention may illuminate truth, feed obsession, magnify fear, sustain prayer, strengthen fantasy, or intensify astral glamour. Its value depends upon Source, Force, Intermediary, Vessel, and Field.

The disciplined soul learns not only how to concentrate attention but when to withdraw it, broaden it, redirect it, and return it to its proper centre.

Physical light reveals bodies by making them available to sight. Consciousness performs a deeper act: it makes experience present. Sensations, images, memories, desires, thoughts, emotions, symbols, and spiritual encounters enter an illuminated field in which they can be apprehended, interpreted, remembered, and given meaning.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, this resemblance is not merely poetic. Consciousness is genuinely luminous, although its light is not electromagnetic radiation. It is an ontological light: the power of disclosure through which something becomes present to a knowing being. The brain and nervous system participate in embodied consciousness, but they do not exhaust the spiritual and psychic realities of awareness.

Spirit is prior to embodied consciousness as its animating and illuminating source. Soul receives and mediates that illumination. The astral body supplies a mobile theatre of images, desires, sensations, and emotions. The etheric body sustains the living conditions through which consciousness remains connected to the organism. The physical body gives consciousness location, resistance, action, and participation in the sensible world.

> Spirit illuminates and animates. Essence determines. Soul mediates. The astral body images and moves. The etheric body vitalises and organises. The physical body embodies.

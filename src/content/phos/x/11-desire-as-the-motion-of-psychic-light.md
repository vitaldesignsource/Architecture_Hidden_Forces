Desire is psychic light in motion toward an object, condition, experience, person, image, or possibility. It establishes attraction and gives direction to psychic energy. Without desire, the soul would possess awareness but lack much of the movement required for participation.

Desire is not inherently corrupt. It can move toward nourishment, intimacy, beauty, knowledge, justice, creation, union, or the divine. It becomes distorted when it mistakes a fragment for the whole, converts participation into possession, or continues moving after its object has ceased to serve life.

## Currents in the astral body

In [[ii-19|the astral body]], desire creates currents, intensities, repetitions, attractions, and aversions. These movements can colour perception before reflective consciousness recognises them. A person may believe that thought produced a conclusion when desire had already directed attention toward the evidence it preferred.

Netzach is the independent wheel of desire within [[ii-22|Ignisophia]]. Its power is indispensable, but it must remain in right relation to Hod, the wheel of understanding, and to Tiphereth, the Inner Sun. Desire supplies motion; wisdom supplies orientation.

Purification does not require the extinction of desire. It requires its liberation from fragmentation, so that longing may recover its deepest object.

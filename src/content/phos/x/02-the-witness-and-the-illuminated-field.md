Within every experience there is both what appears and that to which it appears. Sensations change, thoughts pass, emotions rise and dissolve, and identities are revised. Yet awareness contains a witnessing dimension capable of recognising that these changes have occurred.

The Witness is not the personality observing itself from a distance, nor is it emotional dissociation disguised as spiritual detachment. It is the stable luminous presence within consciousness through which the contents of experience become knowable. It does not need to speak continuously or construct an argument. Its first activity is presence.

## The field

The illuminated field is everything presently disclosed: bodily sensation, external perception, inner speech, memory, imagination, desire, emotional atmosphere, symbolic impression, and subtle influence. Most of this field remains peripheral, while attention selects particular contents and brings them into greater clarity.

The Witness and the field must be distinguished without being severed. A witness without any field would have nothing differentiated to know. A field without a witness would not yet be experience. Consciousness occurs through their living relation.

At its deepest level, the Witness is the presence of spiritual light within the soul. It remains capable of observing psychic motion because it is not identical to every motion passing through the psychic field.

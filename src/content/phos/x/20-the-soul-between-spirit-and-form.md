The soul stands between spirit and form, but this middle position is not one of weakness or indecision. It is the place of mediation through which spirit becomes embodied and form becomes capable of returning toward intelligibility.

In descent, spiritual illumination enters the soul as meaning, value, vocation, image, knowledge, and intention. These become astral motion, etheric organisation, bodily action, relationship, and material consequence.

## Return

In return, physical experience becomes sensation; sensation becomes image; image becomes reflection; reflection becomes understanding; and understanding may be recollected into spiritual knowledge. This is the movement of *epistrophē*, return toward source.

The soul can turn downward without corruption when it embodies spirit truthfully. It can turn upward without rejecting the body when it gathers embodied experience into wisdom. Its failure lies not in mediation itself, but in losing right relation — becoming trapped in form, inflated by image, dispersed through desire, or detached from embodiment.

The soul's task is not escape from the architecture, but conscious participation in it.

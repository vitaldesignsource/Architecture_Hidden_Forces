Hypnagogic light appears at the threshold between waking and sleep. It may arise as flashes, colours, moving forms, geometric patterns, faces, landscapes, sounds, words, presences, or sudden scenes. These appearances can be brief and unstable, yet at times they possess extraordinary clarity.

The hypnagogic state loosens the ordinary organisation of external attention. Sensory engagement diminishes, deliberate thought fragments, and imaginal activity becomes more autonomous. This allows subconscious material, neural phenomena, astral impressions, psychic formations, and spiritual influences to become perceptible through the same transitional field.

## Neither credulity nor dismissal

It is mistaken to classify all hypnagogic light as revelation. It is equally mistaken to assume that every such experience is meaningless neurological residue. The threshold is real precisely because several orders may approach one another there.

Discernment depends upon recurrence, coherence, context, verifiable content when applicable, spiritual quality, and the effects produced within the waking life. The threshold should be entered with openness, but not credulity.

Hypnagogic light demonstrates that consciousness does not end abruptly at the boundary of waking perception. It changes its relation to the body, senses, images, and surrounding fields.

A thought is a form arising within mental light. It possesses structure, emphasis, relation, duration, and direction. Some thoughts appear briefly and dissolve; others recur until they become stable psychic formations capable of influencing perception, desire, memory, and action.

Thought is not identical to language. It may arise as concept, image, relation, pattern, intuition, inner sound, or silent apprehension. Language gives thought articulation, but thought can precede and exceed verbal expression.

## Thought-forms

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, sustained thoughts can acquire real formative presence. A thought-form is not necessarily an independent being, but it is more than an inconsequential abstraction. Repetition, emotion, imagination, attention, and collective reinforcement may strengthen it within the psychic and astral fields.

[[viii-7|Tone Ether]] participates when thought becomes ordered relation. Logos participates when thought acquires intelligible articulation. [[ii-29|Heka]] becomes possible when thought is coordinated with name, breath, gesture, image, material, will, and field.

Mental light can therefore disclose or fabricate. A thought may reveal a true relation, impose a false pattern, receive a higher idea, or reorganise fragments into illusion. Discernment examines not only the brilliance of a thought but its source, structure, consequences, and relationship to reality.

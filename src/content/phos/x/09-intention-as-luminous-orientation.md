If attention is the directed ray, intention is the orientation that gives the ray purpose. Attention determines where consciousness is concentrated; intention determines toward what end that concentration moves.

Intention begins the organisation of possible action before the action becomes externally visible. It selects a future, forms an inward vector, gathers relevant images and desires, recruits etheric vitality, and prepares bodily expression. It is one of the principal means by which spiritual and psychic causation enter form.

An intention need not be verbally stated to become active. It may exist as a persistent orientation beneath conscious speech. Conversely, a declared intention may possess little power when it conflicts with deeper desire, habit, fear, or identity.

## Luminous intention

Luminous intention is intention clarified by truth and aligned with a higher centre. It does not merely force reality according to personal appetite. It brings will, knowledge, desire, image, vitality, and circumstance into right relation.

Within [[ii-22|Ignisophia]], the Charioteer gives direction to the movement generated by the independent wheels of Hod and Netzach. Understanding alone does not move the chariot, and desire alone does not know its proper road. Intention unites their motion around the Inner Sun.

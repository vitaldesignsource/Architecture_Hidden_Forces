Emotion is the coloration of the psychic field. It changes how the world appears, what becomes salient, which memories are activated, how the body organises itself, and what forms of action seem possible.

Fear contracts the illuminated field around threat. Grief lowers the horizon of possibility and deepens the presence of what has been lost. Anger intensifies contrast and prepares force for confrontation. Love reveals value, kinship, beauty, and participation. Wonder enlarges the field by allowing the unknown to appear without immediate reduction.

Emotions are not merely thoughts, nor are they only chemical events. They involve physical, etheric, astral, psychic, relational, and sometimes spiritual dimensions. Bodily chemistry participates in emotion, but it does not exhaust the reality or meaning of what is experienced.

## Tattva and guna

[[ii-9|The tattvas]] colour emotional movement. Tejas may intensify anger, courage, passion, or revelation. Apas may deepen attachment, grief, tenderness, or adaptation. Vāyu may create excitement, anxiety, changeability, or imaginative movement. Pṛthivī may stabilise emotion or fix it into heaviness. Ākāśa may open emotion toward spaciousness, resonance, or apparent emptiness.

[[ii-10|The gunas]] further condition luminosity. Sattva clarifies, rajas agitates, and tamas obscures or fixes. None is evil in itself; distortion arises when their proportions no longer serve the whole.

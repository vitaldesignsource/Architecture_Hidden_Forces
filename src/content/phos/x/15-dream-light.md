Dream light is the illumination within which dream images become visible despite the absence of ordinary waking perception. A dream may contain sunlight, darkness, colour, fire, stars, architecture, faces, and landscapes while the physical eyes remain closed.

Some dream imagery arises through memory, emotion, bodily condition, neural activity, and the reorganisation of recent experience. Other dreams may open into genuine psychic, astral, ancestral, symbolic, prophetic, or spiritual encounters. These possibilities are not mutually exclusive. A dream may contain several levels at once.

## The dreamer's mobility

The dreamer often participates through a more mobile relation between soul, astral body, etheric body, and physical organism. Ordinary sensory constraints are reduced, allowing images to assemble according to correspondences, emotional affinities, symbolic laws, and subtle environments.

Dream light is not uniform. Some dreams are dim, unstable, and fragmentary. Others possess a radiance, coherence, or reality exceeding ordinary imagination. Such intensity may accompany higher revelation, but intensity alone does not establish the level of the source.

Dream interpretation must therefore consider atmosphere, structure, recurrence, consequence, symbolic depth, and the condition of the dreamer. A dream becomes meaningful not merely because it is strange, but because its light reveals a relation previously hidden.

Consciousness is illumination because it grants presence. What is wholly outside consciousness may act upon a being, but it has not yet become experience. When a sensation, image, thought, memory, or spiritual influence enters awareness, it passes from hidden activity into inward manifestation.

Consciousness is not merely a neutral container into which completed objects are deposited. It is an active field of disclosure. It differentiates figure from background, directs attention, establishes relevance, joins present perception to memory, and permits experience to acquire meaning.

## Two orders, one principle

The luminosity of consciousness does not imply that awareness consists of physical photons. It means that consciousness performs upon the interior plane what light performs upon the visible plane: it reveals, differentiates, relates, and makes apprehension possible. Physical light and conscious light are distinct orders participating in a common principle of manifestation.

Consciousness can be dim, fragmented, concentrated, expanded, agitated, lucid, veiled, or overwhelmed. These gradations are not simply differences in intellectual ability. They describe changes in the relation among spirit, soul, subtle bodies, organism, object, and field.

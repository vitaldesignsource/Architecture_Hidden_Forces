The astral body is the real subtle vehicle and field of desire, image, sensation, emotion, dream, and psychic motion. It is not merely a metaphor for the imagination or an antiquated name for neurological activity.

It functions as a theatre of luminous images because psychic forces acquire appearance and movement within it. Desires become attractions and trajectories. Emotions become atmospheres and colours. Memories become scenes. Symbols become living presences. Dreams become environments through which consciousness moves.

## The theatre is real

The theatre analogy must not imply unreality. A theatre is a place where powers assume form, interact, and become perceptible. The astral body provides such a field upon a subtler plane. Its images can be personally generated, collectively inherited, environmentally received, produced by other beings, or transmitted from higher and lower orders.

The astral body is not the whole soul. The soul contains reflective judgement, conscience, spiritual receptivity, deliberate will, and the capacity to discern what appears within the astral field. Without this distinction, every powerful image could falsely claim authority.

Astral projection is possible because the astral body possesses mobility beyond the ordinary limits of physical sensation. Yet projection, dream, imagination, and spiritual ascent must be carefully distinguished. They may overlap without being identical.

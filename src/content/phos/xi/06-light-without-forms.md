Light without forms is luminosity without distinguishable objects, figures, symbols, or boundaries. It may appear as uniform brightness, transparent radiance, self-luminous space, or an immeasurable field in which nothing particular stands apart.

Such experience may indicate the withdrawal of differentiated imagery while illumination remains. Consciousness encounters not a luminous object but luminosity as a condition of manifestation. The field reveals itself before any particular form emerges within it.

## Profound, not final

At higher levels, formless light may approach noetic or spiritual illumination: intelligence without discursive thought, presence without image, disclosure without representational form. It may also arise through neurological or perceptual conditions that flood or simplify the visual field. Phenomenology alone records the absence of form; discernment determines the probable source.

Formless light can be profound, but it is not necessarily final. Beyond even the most immeasurable luminosity may lie the apophatic source that exceeds both light and darkness as consciousness ordinarily understands them.

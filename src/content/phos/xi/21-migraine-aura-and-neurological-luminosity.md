Migraine aura can produce scintillating lines, zigzags, geometric fortifications, luminous arcs, blind regions, flickering patterns, distortions, and changes in visual scale. These phenomena commonly develop gradually and may occur before, during, or even without headache. They arise through neurological processes rather than external light. [Clinical literature](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9857878/) describes the characteristic visual phenomena and their differential diagnosis.

Neurological luminosity is real as experience. The fact that its proximate cause lies in the nervous system does not make the appearance fictitious. Nor does it mean that every geometric or flashing vision is migraine aura.

## Meaning and mechanism

A neurological event may also acquire symbolic meaning or coincide with psychological and spiritual transformation. Meaning and mechanism belong to different questions. Discovering a neurological mechanism does not exhaust the experience's significance, just as spiritual interpretation should not prevent medical assessment.

First-time aura, abrupt changes in an established pattern, persistent symptoms, severe sudden headache, weakness, confusion, speech difficulty, loss of consciousness, or vision changes confined to one eye warrant prompt medical attention, because other serious conditions can resemble migraine.

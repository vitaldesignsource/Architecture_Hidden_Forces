A pillar of light expresses vertical connection. It may join above and below, descend into a person or place, rise from a centre, or establish an axis through which different planes communicate. The pillar is a form of transmission, alignment, and stabilised passage.

A sphere of light expresses centred totality. Its circumference surrounds a unified interior, while every point on its surface relates to one centre. Spheres may appear around beings, objects, places, or concentrated spiritual presences.

An ocean of light abolishes ordinary boundaries. Rather than observing a luminous object, the experiencer becomes immersed in radiance extending beyond measurement. Individual identity may remain as a centre of awareness or temporarily dissolve into the encompassing field.

## Shape and source

These forms possess genuine metaphysical significance, but their geometry does not guarantee their source. A pillar may be an etheric current, spiritual descent, imaginal organisation, or neurological appearance. A sphere may be a protective field, concentrated aura, symbolic form, or psychic construction. An ocean may be noetic participation or perceptual boundary-loss.

Geometry describes how force has become available to perception. Discernment identifies the order to which that force belongs.

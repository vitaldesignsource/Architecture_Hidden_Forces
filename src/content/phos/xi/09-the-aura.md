The aura is the real subtle field surrounding and interpenetrating a living being. It is not one uniform shell or a decorative coloured outline. It is the perceptible expression of several interacting levels: etheric vitality, astral movement, psychic condition, thought, emotion, spiritual orientation, bodily health, and environmental relation.

The etheric aura is generally closest to physical embodiment and reflects formative vitality, rhythm, boundary, and energetic distribution. The astral aura is more mobile and coloured by desire, emotion, imagination, attraction, and aversion. Psychic and mental dimensions may carry recurring forms, symbols, structures, and directed currents. Spiritual illumination may penetrate these lower fields without being reducible to them.

## Perceiving the field

Aura perception may occur through subtle sight, bodily sensitivity, symbolic impression, spontaneous knowing, or a combined mode of perception. Physical optical effects, expectation, contrast, and afterimages can sometimes imitate or mingle with auric perception. This does not make the aura unreal; it requires the perceiver to distinguish the subtle field from effects produced within ordinary vision.

The aura changes, but it is not infinitely arbitrary. Temporary emotions may colour it, established habits may structure it, illness may disturb it, and spiritual practice may clarify or strengthen its relations. Its interpretation should be based upon repeated observation rather than rigid colour dictionaries.

Visionary consciousness may enter complete luminous environments: temples, cities, mountains, gardens, deserts, heavens, chambers, seas, libraries, or terrains illuminated without an identifiable physical source.

Such landscapes may arise in dream, trance, meditation, ritual, near-death experience, astral projection, or spontaneous vision. Some are generated from memory and imagination. Others may be genuine environments belonging to astral, psychic, ancestral, or spiritual planes. Still others are mixed constructions in which a real field is clothed in forms supplied partly by the receiver.

## Qualitative space

A subtle landscape need not obey physical geometry. Distance may correspond to affinity, ascent to refinement, descent to interiority, doors to changes of state, and centres to concentrations of power. Space becomes qualitative rather than merely metric.

The reality of such landscapes should not be judged solely by whether they exist physically. A real astral place is real according to the conditions of the astral plane. Its apparent architecture may be stable, collective, inhabited, historically conditioned, or transformed by the beings participating within it.

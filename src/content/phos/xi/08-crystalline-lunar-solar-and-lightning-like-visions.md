Crystalline light is transparent, precise, refractive, ordered, and often experienced as clarifying. It may indicate differentiation without fragmentation: many facets transmitting one luminosity. It can accompany experiences of intellectual lucidity, subtle structure, purified perception, or the disclosure of hidden order.

Lunar light is receptive, reflective, imaginal, rhythmic, and frequently associated with dream, memory, soul, ancestry, and psychic mediation. It often reveals indirectly, preserving shadow and ambiguity rather than abolishing them.

Solar light is centred, radiating, generative, sovereign, and integrative. Visionary suns may represent spiritual intelligence, the higher Self, Tiphereth, divine presence, or an inner centre around which psychic forces become ordered.

Lightning-like light is sudden, penetrating, discontinuous, and transformative. It can signify an abrupt disclosure that exceeds the gradual preparation of discursive thought. The image appears, strikes, and vanishes, leaving an after-effect greater than its duration.

## Not infallible labels

These qualities are not infallible labels. A solar appearance is not automatically divine, nor is lunar light necessarily passive. Their interpretation depends upon the total experience, its source, the condition of the receiver, and the transformation that follows.

Forms without light are appearances that possess shape, boundary, movement, or identity without seeming illuminated by any source. Dreams frequently contain this quality: objects are simply visible although no lamp, sun, or luminous atmosphere can be located.

Astral and imaginal forms may likewise appear through direct presentation rather than reflected illumination. Their visibility belongs to the mode of the plane upon which they are encountered. They do not necessarily require physical light to travel from source to surface and then into a biological eye.

## Dark forms

Dark forms can also appear against closed-eye darkness, at the edge of sleep, during trance, or within visionary space. Some are memory-images or perceptual completions; others may be autonomous psychic formations, subtle beings, or presences disclosed through contrast rather than radiance.

Form and light must therefore be distinguished. Light can exist without object, and appearance can occur without a visible illuminant. Their union is common but not universal.

Visionary fire is light experienced as burning, purifying, consecrating, transforming, or consuming. It may appear as flame around a being, fire within the body, a descending current, an inner furnace, a burning heart, or an immense field of living combustion.

Physical fire transforms matter through heat and oxidation. Visionary fire transforms psychic, etheric, and spiritual relations. It can loosen rigid formations, expose concealed motives, concentrate intention, awaken vitality, or destroy structures no longer capable of carrying the force seeking embodiment.

## Not every fire is beneficent

Not every visionary fire is beneficent. Astral aggression, psychic fever, obsession, rage, and destabilised vital force can also appear fiery. Fire must be judged according to what it consumes, what it preserves, and what form emerges afterward.

Within [[ii-22|Ignisophia]], fire becomes wisdom when power is joined to discernment. It purifies by separating what is incompatible with truth and consecrates by dedicating what remains to a higher order.

## Concluding doctrine: the luminous appearance and its source

Visionary light is a genuine domain of human and spiritual experience. It may arise through the eye, nervous system, imagination, etheric body, astral body, psychic field, meditation, dream, spiritual being, or divine disclosure. These sources can overlap.

The physical origin of one luminous experience does not explain every luminous experience. The existence of authentic spiritual light does not make every internal flash sacred. The task is distinction without reduction and affirmation without credulity.

Aura, etheric projection, astral perception, luminous beings, subtle landscapes, theophany, and transfiguration belong to the real architecture of hidden forces. Entoptic light, retinal afterimages, migraine aura, and neurological luminosity belong to the real architecture of embodied vision. Both must be understood if the student is to perceive clearly.

The final discipline is not the pursuit of brilliance. It is the cultivation of a receiver capable of truth.

> Honour what appears. Record it faithfully. Test its relations. Examine its consequences. Seek its source. Grant authority only where coherence, stability, truth, and right relation converge.

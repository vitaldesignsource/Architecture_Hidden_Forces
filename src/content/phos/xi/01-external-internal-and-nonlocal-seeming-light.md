External light appears to occupy the shared physical environment. It reflects from surfaces, casts shadows, changes with bodily orientation, and can ordinarily be confirmed by other observers or instruments. Sunlight, flame, lightning, lamps, bioluminescence, and atmospheric luminosity belong primarily to this category.

Internal light appears within the visual or imaginal field without a corresponding physical source in the surrounding environment. It may arise with closed or open eyes and include flashes, colours, forms, stars, veils, geometric patterns, faces, or complete scenes. Its source may be retinal, neurological, hypnagogic, imaginative, psychic, astral, etheric, spiritual, or mixed.

## Nonlocal-seeming light

Nonlocal-seeming light does not appear clearly inside the head or within ordinary external space. It may surround the observer, arrive from no discernible direction, occupy an immense field, or seem to disclose an environment exceeding bodily location. The term *nonlocal-seeming* describes the phenomenology without prematurely reducing it to projection or declaring its metaphysical source.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, some nonlocal experiences may be authentic perception upon etheric, astral, psychic, celestial, or spiritual planes. Others may result from altered spatial processing or inward imagery. The categories identify where light appears to consciousness; they do not by themselves determine what the light is.

Entoptic phenomena are visual appearances whose characteristics arise from the structures or activity of one's own eye and visual system. They may include floaters, the branching shadow of retinal blood vessels known as the Purkinje tree, blue-field entoptic effects, light diffraction, and patterns associated with the eye's internal structures.

These phenomena are real perceptions, even though their immediate source is within the observer's visual apparatus rather than an external luminous object. Their existence demonstrates why "internally produced" must never be confused with "not experienced".

## Physiology and meaning

Entoptic imagery may also interact with symbolic perception. A retinal pattern can acquire personal or religious significance, particularly during concentration or altered states. Its physiological origin does not prevent it from becoming symbolically meaningful, but symbolic meaning does not change its immediate optical source.

Discernment begins by learning the ordinary capabilities of the eye. Knowledge of entoptic phenomena protects spiritual inquiry from false attribution while deepening appreciation for the extraordinary luminosities already available within embodied vision. [A clinical overview](https://vestnik.szd.si/index.php/ZdravVest/article/view/3183) distinguishes entoptic phenomena, photopsias, and phosphenes.

Luminous beings appear throughout religious, esoteric, initiatory, and visionary traditions. They may be understood as angels, daimons, intelligences, ancestors, saints, deities, guides, elemental beings, astral entities, or inhabitants of subtler orders.

Their luminosity may express their native mode of being, the intensity of their presence, their proximity to a spiritual source, or a form adopted for the receiver. Light may disclose intelligence, power, sanctity, vitality, or simply difference from ordinary embodied existence.

## Radiance without rank

A luminous being should not automatically be identified with the highest divine order. Lower or mixed beings may possess considerable radiance, knowledge, and influence. Psychic projections can also assume personified luminous forms.

Authentic encounter is indicated by a degree of autonomy, coherent intelligence, unexpected content, continuity beyond personal expectation, and effects not easily explained by fantasy alone. Yet even autonomy does not establish moral or spiritual authority. A real being may still deceive, misunderstand, demand, or act according to an order incompatible with the receiver's good.

Every encounter must remain subject to the Law of Right Relation.

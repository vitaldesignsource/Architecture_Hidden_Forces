Luminous experience occupies a threshold where physical perception, subtle vision, consciousness, imagination, physiology, and spiritual disclosure may converge. Human beings have reported interior stars, radiant beings, fields around living bodies, crystalline brilliance, visionary fire, immeasurable light, and landscapes illuminated without any visible sun. These reports appear in contemplative traditions, initiatory literature, dreams, near-death narratives, mystical biographies, neurological medicine, and ordinary moments of altered perception.

The Architecture of Hidden Forces does not begin by dismissing these experiences as unreal merely because their light is not measurable in the surrounding physical environment. Neither does it declare every brightness divine. Etheric, astral, psychic, noetic, and spiritual planes are real, and each may possess its own modes of luminosity. The physical visual system can also generate genuine appearances of light through retinal, neurological, and physiological processes. Several causes may participate in a single experience.

Phenomenology first asks what appeared, how it appeared, where it appeared to be, and what effects followed. Discernment then asks what produced the appearance. These questions must not be reversed. A premature explanation can prevent the experience from being accurately perceived.

The governing principle is:

> Receive the appearance without immediately surrendering judgement to it. Honour the experience without prematurely deciding its source.

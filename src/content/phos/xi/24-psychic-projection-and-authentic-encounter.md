Psychic projection occurs when contents belonging primarily to the observer are experienced as though they originate independently outside the self. Desire, fear, expectation, memory, cultural imagery, and unresolved conflict may be projected upon a person, place, symbol, or subtle field.

An authentic encounter involves contact with a reality possessing some degree of independence from the perceiver. This may be another person, an etheric field, an astral environment, an ancestor, a daimon, an angel, a spiritual intelligence, or a divine presence.

## Clothing and autonomy

The distinction is rarely absolute at the level of appearance. Authentic realities must still pass through the receiver's faculties, vocabulary, memory, imagination, and psychic structure. A real being may therefore be perceived through partially projected clothing. Likewise, a projection may organise itself with enough complexity to seem autonomous.

Signs of projection include exact conformity to expectation, compulsive self-reference, flattery, dramatisation, instability, and disappearance when emotional investment is withdrawn. Signs of authentic encounter may include resistance to expectation, coherent autonomy, unexpected knowledge, continuity, shared perception, verifiable consequence, and transformation extending beyond temporary excitement.

No single sign is decisive. Discernment arises through convergence.

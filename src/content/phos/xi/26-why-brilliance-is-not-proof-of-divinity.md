Brilliance indicates intensity of appearance, not necessarily height of source. Physical processes can generate overwhelming brightness. Migraine aura can produce radiant geometries. Astral beings may appear magnificent. Psychic formations can be saturated with fascination. Lower powers may employ splendour to command attention.

Higher light may indeed be brilliant beyond ordinary comprehension. Theophany, angelic manifestation, noetic illumination, and authentic transfiguration can appear with extraordinary luminosity. The error lies not in associating higher revelation with brilliance, but in treating brilliance as sufficient proof.

## How divine light is known

Divine light is recognised through more than magnitude. It possesses truth, intelligibility, proportion, freedom from manipulative compulsion, and a capacity to place the receiver in right relation with realities beyond the personal ego.

Counterfeit light demands submission to its appearance. Authentic higher light increases the capacity to see. It may humble rather than flatter, clarify rather than intoxicate, and impose responsibility rather than confer imaginary superiority.

Intensity reveals that force is present. Discernment reveals what force, through what intermediary, acting upon what vessel, within what field.

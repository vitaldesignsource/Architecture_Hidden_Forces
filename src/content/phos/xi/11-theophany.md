Theophany is the manifestation of divine presence through a perceptible form. Light, fire, cloud, voice, radiant darkness, angelic form, throne, human figure, mountain, or natural event may become the vessel through which divinity discloses itself.

A theophany is not merely a dramatic subjective feeling. Within a realist spiritual ontology, divine power can genuinely become present through a form adapted to the capacity of the receiver and the conditions of manifestation. The form is neither the totality of the divine nor an empty costume. It is a real mediation.

## Overwhelming and yet testable

The intensity of theophany may overwhelm ordinary perception because a higher order is entering a vessel of limited capacity. Fear, awe, trembling, silence, altered time, radiant obscurity, and moral demand frequently accompany such encounters.

Yet a luminous being or overwhelming presence is not automatically divine. Astral entities, psychic projections, collective forms, and lower spiritual powers may appear magnificent. Theophany is recognised not through brightness alone but through truth, authority, coherence, transformative consequence, and right relation.

Transfiguration occurs when an inward spiritual reality becomes outwardly or subtly luminous through a person, body, face, form, or environment. Unlike ordinary illumination falling upon a surface, transfiguration appears to proceed through the being from within, or from a higher power entering through it.

Sacred traditions describe faces shining, garments becoming radiant, bodies surrounded by light, and ordinary human appearance temporarily revealing a concealed spiritual nature. These accounts should not be treated only as literary ornament. They testify to the possibility that spirit can penetrate the etheric, astral, and even sensible appearance of an embodied being.

## Alignment, not permanent conversion

Transfiguration does not necessarily mean the physical body has become permanently transformed into light. It may be a temporary alignment in which spirit, soul, subtle bodies, and physical form become unusually transparent to one another.

The witness may perceive the event physically, clairvoyantly, symbolically, or through several modes simultaneously. Its reality is located not only in whether a camera records brightness, but in the actual manifestation and the transformation produced in those who encounter it.

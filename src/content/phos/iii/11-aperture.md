An aperture is a controlled opening through which light enters a bounded space. It determines how much light can pass, from what direction, and with what degree of concentration.

Attention functions as a psychic aperture. It cannot admit every sensation, memory, possibility, and influence simultaneously. By opening toward some phenomena and closing against others, it creates a workable field of consciousness.

## Regulated access

An aperture that is too narrow produces deprivation and rigidity. One that is too wide may produce flooding, distraction, or loss of distinction.

Spiritual receptivity is not unlimited openness. It is the disciplined capacity to regulate access according to purpose, proportion, and readiness.

A ray is light understood as a directed relation between source and destination. In geometrical optics it is an idealised line representing the path along which light propagates. Metaphysically, it represents orientation: luminosity given a path.

The ray introduces intention into the anatomy of illumination. Radiance surrounds; a ray proceeds toward. It selects a direction without necessarily enclosing the entire current.

A ray may carry only one aspect of a greater source. What reaches the receiver is therefore not the total source but a directed participation adapted to a particular relation.

The ray is the first geometry of luminous purpose.

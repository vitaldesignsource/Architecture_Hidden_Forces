Luminous imprinting occurs when illumination leaves a lasting modification within the receiver.

A bright physical image may produce an afterimage. Repeated exposure can alter biological rhythms. A profound realisation may reorganise memory and perception. A symbol encountered at the right moment may influence an entire life.

The imprint is not the original light. It is the altered structure left by reception.

## Healthy and distorted

Healthy imprinting expands future capacity. Distorted imprinting may create fixation, compulsion, fear, or dependence upon recreating the original experience.

Luminous imprints enter the retentive depth of the individual and collective field. They contribute to [[ii-27|the Crypt of Primordial Memory]] by altering what future formation can accomplish easily, only with resistance, or no longer in precisely the same way.

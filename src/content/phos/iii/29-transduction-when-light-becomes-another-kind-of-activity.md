Transduction is the conversion of one kind of activity into another. Physical light may become heat, chemical change, electrical signal, biological regulation, or neural activity.

This is among the most important concepts in the anatomy of illumination because received light rarely remains simply light.

## Across registers

Noetic illumination may become language. Language may awaken emotion. Emotion may mobilise the body. Bodily action may alter the material and social environment. The luminous current has been transduced across several registers.

Within [[ii-3|CHON architecture]], light can enter living material processes without replacing their chemistry. Within the soul, an image can become meaning. Within [[ii-22|Ignisophia]], insight becomes directed transformative fire.

Transduction is the bridge between revelation and consequence.

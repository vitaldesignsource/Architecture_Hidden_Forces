Refraction is the bending of light as it passes between media in which it propagates differently. The light continues, but its direction changes.

Every passage between levels of reality requires some form of refraction. A spiritual principle entering language bends toward concepts and symbols. An idea entering emotion changes its direction. Intention entering bodily action is refracted through capacity, habit, circumstance, and material resistance.

Refraction is not automatically distortion. It is often the necessary geometry of translation.

The task is to distinguish faithful bending from deviation that obscures the originating relation.

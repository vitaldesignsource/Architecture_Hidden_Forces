A vessel is anything capable of receiving, containing, organising, and expressing force. A body is a vessel, but so is a symbol, ritual, institution, language, memory, or work of art.

The vessel gives light a local form. Its boundaries prevent indefinite dispersal, while its internal structure determines how the received current can be held and expressed.

## Vessel-making

A vessel may be too weak to sustain an intense luminosity, too rigid to allow transformation, or too porous to retain what it receives. Vessel-making therefore requires purification, education, discipline, repair, proportion, and ethical preparation.

The purpose of a vessel is not simply to contain light. It is to make illumination inhabitable.

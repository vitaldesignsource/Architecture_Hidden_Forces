Atmosphere is a field experienced qualitatively. It is not only what occupies a space but how that space feels, moves, receives, and modifies what enters it.

Physical atmosphere scatters and filters sunlight, producing the colour of the sky, the softness of dawn, and the reddening of sunset. Psychic atmosphere similarly alters how images, words, memories, and persons are received.

A room may be visually illuminated yet psychically oppressive. Another may be dim but inwardly clear. Atmosphere arises from the combined effects of place, history, expectation, bodily state, symbol, relationship, and attention.

Morphaithēr is a living formative atmosphere because it carries inherited tendencies while remaining open to alteration through new participation.

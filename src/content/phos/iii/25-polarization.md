In physical optics, polarisation concerns the orientation of light's oscillation. It reveals that light can possess internal directional order beyond its general path of travel.

Metaphysically, polarisation represents selection according to orientation. A receiver may admit only those aspects of a current aligned with its existing structure.

## Conscious and invisible filtering

This can clarify by filtering noise, but it can also narrow perception. Ideological, emotional, or symbolic polarisation may prevent a person from receiving anything not already oriented toward an established position.

Discernment filters consciously. Unexamined polarisation filters invisibly.

The question is not whether selection occurs, but whether the principle of selection is understood and rightly ordered.

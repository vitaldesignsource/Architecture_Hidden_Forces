Resonance occurs when one system responds strongly to a pattern compatible with its own structure or natural modes of activity.

Resonance explains why certain words, symbols, places, rhythms, or persons awaken powerful responses while others do not. The receiving structure already contains a capacity capable of being activated.

## Not proof

Resonance is not proof of objective truth. A false idea may resonate with an existing fear or desire. A truthful principle may initially produce little response because the necessary capacity has not yet been developed.

Resonance indicates correspondence between current and receiver. Discernment must still determine the nature and consequences of that correspondence.

Tone Ether governs this relational dimension through ratio, interval, affinity, and ordered response.

Transmission is the carrying of light or luminous influence from one relation into another. It requires continuity sufficient for something of the originating pattern to remain recognisable.

Transmission always involves three questions: what is being carried, through what medium, and with what degree of fidelity?

A teaching transmitted through generations may preserve its central architecture while changing language and form. It may also accumulate distortion, omission, or embellishment.

Successful transmission is not perfect duplication. It is continuity of essential relation through necessary transformation.

A luminous field is the region within which light or luminous influence becomes active. Unlike a ray or beam, a field need not possess one dominant direction.

A field establishes conditions. Everything entering it becomes subject to a shared environment of visibility, intensity, rhythm, and relation. Daylight is field-like because it illuminates an entire landscape rather than only one object.

Morphaithēr is field-like at the formative level. It carries the active conditions through which forces encounter one another and become biased toward possible forms.

A field is not empty space containing activity. It is the structured region of possible interaction.

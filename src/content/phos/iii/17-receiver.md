The receiver is the structure in which transmitted light produces an effect. It may be a photosensitive surface, a plant, an eye, a nervous system, a psyche, an intellect, or an entire community.

Reception is active. Every receiver selects, converts, limits, and interprets according to its organisation. What arrives is not identical to what becomes experience.

The receiver therefore participates in manifestation. It does not create the source, but it contributes to the final appearance of what has been received.

No account of illumination is complete until the capacities and limitations of the receiver have been examined.

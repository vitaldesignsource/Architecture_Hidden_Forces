Recollection is the act through which the receiver recognises the originating relation concealed within its acquired images, forms, and memories.

Return does not require reversing manifestation or abandoning embodiment. It means tracing illumination back through its mediations: from image to lens, from lens to medium, from medium to current, and from current toward source.

The mature receiver does not merely remember that it once encountered light. It recollects the principle disclosed through the encounter and gives that principle renewed embodiment.

## The circuit closed

Return therefore completes the luminous circuit:

> The source gives. The medium qualifies. The vessel receives. The witness recognises. The receiver is transformed. The transformed receiver becomes capable of radiance.

What returns is not identical to what proceeded. It carries the history of mediation, embodiment, experience, and conscious participation.

Illumination reaches fulfilment when the receiver becomes a faithful secondary luminary — remaining distinct from the source while making its light newly present within the world.

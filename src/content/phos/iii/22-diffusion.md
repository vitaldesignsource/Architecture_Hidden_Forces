Diffusion is the movement from concentration toward broader distribution. It softens boundaries and establishes more general conditions of illumination.

Diffuse light creates atmosphere rather than a sharply defined path. It can gently illuminate an entire field, reducing harsh contrast and allowing multiple forms to become visible together.

Psychically, mood is often diffuse. Culturally, an idea may spread until it becomes part of the assumed atmosphere without remaining connected to one recognisable source.

Diffusion increases reach while reducing concentration. It is beneficial when a field requires general nourishment, but insufficient when deep penetration or precise transformation is needed.

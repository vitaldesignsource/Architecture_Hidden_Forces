A beam is an organised bundle of rays occupying a bounded region. It is more substantial in appearance than the abstract ray and more directional than general radiance.

Metaphysically, a beam represents concentrated transmission. Attention can become beam-like when it gathers scattered awareness around a definite object. Ritual intention becomes beam-like when symbol, speech, desire, and action are aligned toward one operation.

## Coherence over intensity

The strength of a beam depends upon coherence, not intensity alone. A powerful but disordered current may scatter before reaching its destination. A narrower, better-organised beam may penetrate more effectively.

Concentration gives light operative reach, but excessive narrowing can exclude necessary context. The beam must remain proportionate to its purpose.

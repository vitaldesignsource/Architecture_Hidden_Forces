A prism differentiates apparently unified light into a spectrum. It reveals multiplicity concealed within unity.

Metaphysically, the prism represents the capacity of a medium or consciousness to articulate distinctions within a single source. One principle may become many symbols, virtues, colours, powers, or modes of expression without ceasing to possess an underlying unity.

Morphaithēr acts prismatically when a current receives tattvic differentiation. The soul acts prismatically when one experience becomes sensation, feeling, image, memory, thought, and meaning.

The prism does not necessarily fragment falsely. It can reveal real internal complexity. Error begins when one colour claims to be the whole light.

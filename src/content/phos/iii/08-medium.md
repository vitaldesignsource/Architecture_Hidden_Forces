A medium is that through which light passes. It allows transmission while simultaneously qualifying what is transmitted.

No medium is perfectly neutral. Air, glass, water, crystal, tissue, language, imagination, culture, and memory each alter what they carry. Some changes are clarifying; others distort. Many are necessary translations rather than errors.

## Formability

The medium makes communication possible because it gives the current a condition under which it can travel. Pure force without mediation may remain incommunicable or become destructive.

Ether is the formative middle between force and form: not completed form, but **formability**. It provides continuity, orientation, proportion, and the capacity for relation to be carried.

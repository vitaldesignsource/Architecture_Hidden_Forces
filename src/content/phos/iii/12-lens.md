A lens gathers, bends, focuses, enlarges, or disperses light. It does not merely receive illumination; it reorganises its path.

The soul acts as a lens whenever it concentrates experience into an image, interpretation, or intention. Education shapes the intellectual lens. Desire shapes the emotional lens. Trauma, expectation, devotion, and ideology can all modify its curvature.

## Clarity is not orientation

A clear lens may still focus incorrectly. Clarity alone does not guarantee proper orientation. The position, shape, and relation of the lens to source and receiver also matter.

Discernment is therefore partly an art of examining the lens through which one sees.

A threshold is the boundary at which a luminous current changes its mode of operation. It marks the passage between fields, media, states, or levels of organisation.

Dawn is a threshold between night and day. Initiation is a threshold between knowledge heard and knowledge assumed as responsibility. Death is a threshold at which an organised living unity ceases to function as one embodied vessel.

Thresholds intensify mediation because the current must be translated. What operates freely in one field may require a different form to enter another.

The threshold is not merely a line crossed. It is a zone of instability, selection, and possible transformation.

Reflection occurs when light encounters a boundary and returns into the field from which it came or enters another direction.

Metaphysically, reflection is the beginning of self-reference. Consciousness receives an experience and turns back upon it. Thought becomes reflective when it examines its own assumptions. The soul becomes reflective when it recognises its participation in what it perceives.

## The closed loop

Reflection can deepen knowledge, but it can also create enclosed loops. A person may encounter only reflections of prior expectations and mistake them for new revelation.

The essential question is whether reflection returns the receiver toward reality or merely toward itself.

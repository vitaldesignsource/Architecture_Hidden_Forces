Scattering occurs when light is redirected through encounters with particles, irregularities, or structures within a medium.

Unlike smooth diffusion, scattering emphasises interruption and multiplicity. A current enters one path and emerges along many.

Psychic scattering occurs when attention is repeatedly redirected by competing desires, fears, images, and stimuli. Cultural scattering occurs when a teaching breaks into fragments that travel independently of their original architecture.

## What interruption reveals

Scattering can weaken coherence, but it may also reveal hidden contents of the medium. A beam becomes visible in dusty air precisely because particles scatter some of its light toward the observer.

What interrupts transmission can also make transmission perceptible.

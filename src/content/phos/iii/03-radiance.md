Radiance is the luminous abundance surrounding and proceeding from a source. It is less narrowly directed than a ray and less bounded than a beam. Radiance establishes a region of presence.

A radiant being, object, or symbol seems to extend beyond its immediate boundary. This extension need not imply a measurable aura. It may indicate the field of attention, warmth, meaning, or influence organised around it.

Radiance is communicative abundance. It makes the source available before a particular destination has been selected.

## Borrowed brightness

Yet radiance can be deceptive. Reflected or borrowed brightness may be mistaken for self-originating luminosity. Authentic radiance remains connected to a source capable of sustaining what it communicates.

## The complete anatomy of illumination

The thirty-one structures of this division form four great movements.

The first is **origination**: source, emission, procession, radiance, ray, beam, and field.

The second is **mediation**: atmosphere, medium, veil, threshold, aperture, lens, prism, mirror, image, vessel, receiver, and witness.

The third is **interaction**: transmission, reflection, refraction, diffusion, scattering, absorption, polarisation, interference, resonance, and attunement.

The fourth is **transformation and return**: transduction, luminous imprinting, recollection, and the renewed movement toward source.

Illumination is therefore not a straight line connecting a light to a passive object. It is a living circuit in which every boundary, medium, vessel, and act of reception contributes to what the light becomes.

> Light proceeds as radiance, enters relation as transmission, becomes experience through reception, becomes form through transduction, becomes memory through imprinting, and becomes wisdom through recollection and return.

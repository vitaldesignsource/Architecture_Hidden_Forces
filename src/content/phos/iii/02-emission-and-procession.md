**Emission** describes light leaving a physical source. **Procession** describes a broader metaphysical movement in which what remains unified at its origin becomes communicable through successive degrees of differentiation.

Procession should not be imagined as pieces of the source breaking away and falling through space. It is the transition from concentrated unity into extended participation. What is implicit at the source becomes explicit through relation.

## Within the Architecture

Procession begins when hidden potency becomes active radiance. That radiance enters Root Ether, passes through the [[ii-6|Fourfold Veil]], acquires [[ii-9|tattvic coloration]] within Morphaithēr, and becomes increasingly determinate as it approaches embodiment.

Procession is therefore not simple departure. The source remains while its influence becomes present elsewhere.

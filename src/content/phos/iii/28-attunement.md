Attunement is the deliberate or organic adjustment of a receiver so that it can enter clearer relation with a particular current.

Resonance may happen spontaneously. Attunement involves preparation. Attention is steadied, interference reduced, the vessel repaired, and the appropriate symbolic or bodily conditions established.

Meditation, study, ethical discipline, ritual preparation, breath, music, posture, and contemplative imagination may all function as methods of attunement.

## Not the surrender of discernment

Attunement does not mean surrendering discernment. A finely tuned instrument remains selective. It becomes more capable of recognising both consonance and dissonance.

True attunement increases responsiveness without eliminating boundary.

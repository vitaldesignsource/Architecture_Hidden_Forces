Illumination is not a single action. Between a luminous source and an illuminated consciousness lies an entire anatomy: emission, direction, atmosphere, mediation, selection, transformation, reception, memory, and return.

This division examines that anatomy. Its optical terms retain their physical meanings while also serving as carefully limited metaphysical models. Reflection, refraction, polarisation, resonance, and absorption should not be reduced to decorative metaphors. Each describes a distinct way in which a current encounters a medium, boundary, form, or receiver.

The complete movement may be expressed as:

> Source → Procession → Radiance → Transmission → Mediation → Differentiation → Reception → Transduction → Imprinting → Recollection → Return

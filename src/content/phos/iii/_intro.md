Illumination is not a single action. Between a luminous source and an illuminated consciousness lies an entire anatomy: emission, direction, atmosphere, mediation, selection, transformation, reception, memory, and return.

This division examines that anatomy. Its optical terms retain their physical meanings while also naming real operations of formative light upon the etheric, astral, noetic, and spiritual planes. Reflection, refraction, polarisation, resonance, and absorption should not be reduced to decorative metaphors, nor should their metaphysical use be taken for a loose figure of speech. Each describes a distinct way in which a current encounters a medium, boundary, form, or receiver — physically where the current is physical, formatively where it is formative.

The complete movement may be expressed as:

> Source → Procession → Radiance → Transmission → Mediation → Differentiation → Reception → Transduction → Imprinting → Recollection → Return

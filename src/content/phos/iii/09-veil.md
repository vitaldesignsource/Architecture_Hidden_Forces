A veil is a selective medium. It transmits certain qualities while concealing, softening, or transforming others.

The veil should not be understood only as an obstacle. Unveiled intensity may overwhelm a finite receiver. A veil protects the receiving form by reducing, distributing, translating, or sequencing what passes through it.

The [[ii-6|Fourfold Veil]] performs this regulatory function. Warmth awakens, Light differentiates, Tone orders, and Life integrates. Each ether makes the proceeding force more capable of entering determinate embodiment.

Every revelation therefore contains some concealment. The veil allows the hidden to appear without surrendering its entire depth.

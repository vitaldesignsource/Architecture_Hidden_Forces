Interference occurs when waves meet and combine. Their relation may reinforce intensity, diminish it, or produce complex patterns of alternating strength and absence.

Constructive interference occurs when movements align. Destructive interference occurs when they oppose one another in ways that reduce the resulting expression.

## Within the psyche

Thought, desire, emotion, and action may reinforce one another or cancel one another. A person may consciously seek transformation while maintaining habits that negate the desired movement.

Interference reveals that forces do not act only according to their individual strength. Their timing, phase, proportion, and relationship determine what becomes manifest.

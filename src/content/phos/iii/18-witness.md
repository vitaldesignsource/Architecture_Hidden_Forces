A receiver can register an event without consciously recognising it. The witness is the presence capable of knowing that reception has occurred.

The eye receives light; consciousness witnesses the resulting appearance. The psyche receives an image; reflective awareness recognises that an image has arisen. The witness introduces self-awareness into the luminous event.

Witnessing does not mean passive detachment. A mature witness can observe without immediately identifying with, rejecting, or obeying what appears.

The witness permits illumination to become discernment. Without it, reception may produce reaction but not understanding.

The luminous source is that from which illumination proceeds. Physically, it may be the Sun, a flame, an electrical discharge, a living organism, or any body emitting electromagnetic radiation. Metaphysically, it is the originating centre from which presence, intelligence, vitality, or formative power becomes communicable.

## Source and reflector

A source must be distinguished from a reflector. A mirror may appear bright while possessing no light of its own. A teacher, symbol, institution, or psychic image may transmit genuine illumination while remaining dependent upon something prior.

The source is also greater than any single emission. What proceeds from it is a relational expression, not necessarily its total being. The Sun is not exhausted by one ray, and a principle is not exhausted by one formulation.

Discernment begins by asking whether the apparent source is original, mediating, reflective, or derivative.

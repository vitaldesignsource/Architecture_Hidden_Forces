The etheric body is the real vital-formative dimension of the embodied organism. It is a subtle body operating upon the etheric plane and mediating between essence, soul, astral activity, and physical anatomy. It is not merely a model of biological complexity, a metaphor for metabolism, or an invisible copy of the physical body.

The etheric body carries the active pattern through which matter becomes organised as living form. It maintains morphological continuity, distributes vitality, coordinates rhythms, supports growth and regeneration, preserves the organism's living boundary, and continually repairs the relation between part and whole.

## Extent and projection

It is practically coextensive with the physical body during embodied life, yet it is not absolutely confined to the physical surface. Etheric presence may extend, radiate, contract, exchange, impress, and under particular conditions project beyond its ordinary bodily concentration. Etheric projection is therefore a real possibility of the subtle constitution, although it must be distinguished from astral projection, imagination, dream, and dissociative states.

The etheric body individualises the greater formative atmosphere of [[viii-26|Morphaithēr]]. What Morphaithēr is as living formative field, the etheric body becomes as the organised vital field of a particular living being.

The [[ii-7|Fourfold Veil]] operates within it through four interpenetrating activities:

> Warmth awakens. Light differentiates. Tone orders. Life integrates.

Physical anatomy supplies material embodiment, but the etheric body supplies the organised vitality through which anatomy becomes and remains alive.

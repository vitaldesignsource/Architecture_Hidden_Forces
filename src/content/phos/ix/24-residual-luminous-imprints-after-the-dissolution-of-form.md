A living being leaves consequences after its visible unity has dissolved. Genetic inheritance, altered environments, memories, teachings, constructed objects, learned behaviours, emotional effects, ecological changes, and transformed relationships all preserve aspects of its former activity. These are physical, psychological, social, and historical continuities.

But residual luminous imprints are not limited to these visible consequences. A being may leave real etheric, astral, psychic, symbolic, ancestral, and spiritual impressions within persons, objects, bodies, places, rituals, lineages, and Morphaithēr itself.

## Saturated places and objects

A house may retain an atmosphere shaped by repeated lives and emotions. A ritual object may become saturated through sustained use. A landscape may carry the formative consequences of violence, devotion, cultivation, burial, or pilgrimage. A symbol may accumulate power through generations of attention and participation. These imprints need not be reducible to suggestion, ordinary memory, or physical residue, although such factors may interact with them.

[[ii-27|The Crypt of Primordial Memory]] is the real ontological depth through which existence inherits itself. It need not function as a perfect recording of every event. Memory may persist as an altered curvature of possibility: what has occurred changes the field within which later forms emerge.

[[ii-28|The Ossuary of Living Forms]] receives what dissolved embodiments return to becoming. Matter, pattern, experience, relation, and formative consequence do not all persist in the same manner, but neither does reality return to the condition that preceded the departed form.

Residual light is the continuing efficacy of a form no longer visibly present.

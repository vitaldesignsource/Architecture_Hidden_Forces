Health is luminous coherence: the capacity of the physical, etheric, astral, psychic, and spiritual dimensions of a being to maintain differentiated but harmonious participation.

Luminous coherence does not necessarily mean visible brightness or greater biophoton emission. It means that forces move according to right relation. Boundaries protect without becoming impermeable. Rhythms vary without collapsing into chaos. Organs perform distinct functions while participating in the whole. Desire moves without overthrowing judgement. Consciousness inhabits the body without becoming imprisoned by it.

## Coherence is not uniformity

Coherence is not rigid uniformity. A healthy organism changes continually. Heart rate, temperature, attention, hormone levels, immune activity, metabolism, sleep, and emotional intensity rise and fall according to need. Living coherence is patterned variation held within an intelligible unity.

The etheric body is central to this coherence because it mediates organised vitality. Yet health also depends upon relations among spirit, soul, astral body, physical constitution, environment, community, meaning, and time. A disruption arising upon one level may eventually affect the others.

Health should therefore be understood neither as the mere absence of symptoms nor as uninterrupted spiritual radiance. It is the continuing ability of the whole being to receive, transform, distribute, resist, repair, and return.

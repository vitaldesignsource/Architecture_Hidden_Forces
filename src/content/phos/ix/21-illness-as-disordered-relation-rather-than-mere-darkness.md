Illness is a disturbance of right relation that may arise upon physical, etheric, astral, psychic, spiritual, environmental, ancestral, or collective levels. Its causes may be singular, but they are often compound.

Some illnesses originate predominantly through physical factors such as infection, genetic variation, injury, toxicity, malnutrition, degeneration, or environmental exposure. Others may involve etheric depletion, disrupted rhythm, damaged subtle boundaries, astral conflict, obsessive formations, psychic fragmentation, spiritual alienation, destructive influence, loss of meaning, or prolonged separation from a higher ordering principle.

Causation may descend from spiritual and psychic disturbance into etheric and physical manifestation. It may also ascend when physical illness alters vitality, emotion, identity, consciousness, and spiritual participation. No plane of the human being exists in complete isolation from the others.

## Between two reductions

Illness should not always be equated with spiritual darkness or weakened etheric power. Yet it would be equally reductive to declare that illness can never be exactly that. A diseased mind, distorted desire, pathological attachment, spiritual estrangement, or destructive subtle influence may genuinely participate in illness.

The Law of Right Relation prevents both extremes. It asks what force is active, where it originates, through what intermediary it moves, how the vessel receives it, and what field sustains it.

Spiritual or etheric causation must never be used to blame the sufferer or to reject appropriate physical and psychological care. Different interventions may address different levels of the same disorder. Diagnosis requires discernment, because similar symptoms may arise from very different sources.

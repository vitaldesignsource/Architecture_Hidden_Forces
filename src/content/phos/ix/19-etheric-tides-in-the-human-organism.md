Etheric tides are real periodic movements of formative vitality within and around the organism. They involve expansion and contraction, concentration and distribution, reception and release, outward engagement and inward restoration.

Their physical correspondences include circadian rhythms, respiration, pulse, temperature variation, hormonal secretion, digestion, sleep architecture, attention, fatigue, regeneration, and recovery. None of these processes alone constitutes an etheric tide, but each may express the deeper rhythmic movement of the etheric body.

## Where the vitality is moving

Vitality is not equally distributed at every moment. During activity, etheric forces may become strongly engaged in movement, perception, metabolism, and outward participation. During sleep, illness, meditation, healing, or withdrawal, vitality may be redirected toward restoration, integration, interiorisation, or subtler activity.

Etheric tides can also be influenced by place, season, celestial rhythm, relationship, ritual, emotional condition, nutrition, illness, and spiritual practice. The organism exists within Morphaithēr and continually exchanges formative influence with its surroundings. It is an individual field, but never an isolated one.

Continuous maximum activation is not health. A living tide must be capable of return. The question is not merely how much energy a person possesses, but where that vitality is moving, what pattern it serves, and whether its movement is rightly related to the whole.

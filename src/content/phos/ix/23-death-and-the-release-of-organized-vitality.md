At biological death, the physical organism loses the integrated regulation through which its tissues functioned as one living whole. Circulation and respiration cease, electrochemical gradients collapse, and the coordinated activity of organs dissolves. Material components remain temporarily present, but the embodied unity that made them one organism no longer operates through them in the same manner.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, death includes the real loosening and eventual separation of the etheric body from the physical organism. The organised vitality that sustained physical embodiment is released from a vessel no longer capable of maintaining right relation with it.

## According to the laws of its own plane

This release should not be imagined as a reservoir of physical photons visibly escaping the corpse. Etheric vitality belongs to another order of existence and must be understood according to the laws of the etheric plane. Under some conditions, its withdrawal may be gradual, leaving residual vitality, impressions, attachments, or formative traces around the body, place, or persons involved.

Death also alters the relationship among the physical, etheric, astral, psychic, and spiritual members of the being. Their precise post-mortem conditions require further treatment elsewhere in the system, but death cannot be reduced to material decomposition alone.

The physical body returns its matter to ecological cycles. The etheric organisation is released from physical concentration. Psychic and spiritual consequences continue according to their own orders. Force is liberated from one form and made available to further transformation.

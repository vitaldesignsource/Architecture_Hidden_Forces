The womb reveals the generative power of darkness. It shelters the developing organism from the full intensity and variability of the outer environment while maintaining controlled exchange through the maternal body. Its darkness is neither empty nor inert. It is warm, fluid, rhythmic, bounded, nutritive, and filled with formative activity.

The womb corrects every doctrine that simply equates light with goodness and darkness with deficiency. Some forms can arise only through concealment. Premature exposure can disrupt what protected darkness allows to mature. Darkness may function as veil, vessel, incubation, mercy, and concentration.

## Solar time carried inward

The maternal organism also mediates outer celestial and terrestrial rhythms to the developing child. The embryo does not need to see the Sun directly to participate in solar time. Maternal hormonal, metabolic, behavioural, and etheric rhythms carry the consequences of day and night inward.

The womb is therefore not the absence of light but the chamber of interiorised light. Visible radiance is withheld while formative light works within concealment. Birth is not creation from nothing; it is the unveiling of a living form assembled through hidden participation.

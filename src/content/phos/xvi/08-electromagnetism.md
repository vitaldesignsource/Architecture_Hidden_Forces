James Clerk Maxwell unified electricity, magnetism, and optics. His equations showed that changing electric fields produce magnetic fields and changing magnetic fields produce electric fields. These coupled fields can propagate through space as waves.

The speed predicted from electromagnetic constants matched the measured speed of light. Visible light was therefore identified as one region of a much broader electromagnetic spectrum. Radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays, and gamma rays are all electromagnetic radiation distinguished principally by frequency, wavelength, and photon energy, as [the Institute of Physics explains](https://www.iop.org/explore-physics/big-ideas-physics/maxwells-equations).

## Which ether was abandoned

An electromagnetic wave does not need the mechanical luminiferous ether imagined by many nineteenth-century theories. Classical electromagnetic fields can propagate through vacuum. This result contributed to the abandonment of the old mechanical ether, especially after relativity reformulated the relations among light, space, and motion.

That historical rejection does not automatically disprove every spiritual concept called "ether". Steiner's ethers, the etheric body, and [[ii-5|Morphaithēr]] are not merely alternative names for the rejected mechanical carrier of electromagnetic waves. Their claims concern formative organisation, vitality, subtle substance, and mediation. They must be evaluated according to what they actually propose.

Science benefits when terms are not equivocated. The electromagnetic field is mathematically defined and experimentally measurable. The etheric field, as understood in this encyclopaedia, is a real but subtler formative order not currently identified with any established physical field. Similar language may indicate structural correspondence, but it does not establish physical identity.

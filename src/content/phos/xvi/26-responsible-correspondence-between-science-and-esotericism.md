Responsible correspondence begins by distinguishing identity from analogy. To say that spiritual illumination resembles optical illumination is not to say that the two are physically identical. To say that the etheric body may influence metabolism is not to say that every metabolic photon is etheric radiation.

A strong correspondence preserves the structure of both domains. Reflection can illuminate how consciousness recognises itself. Refraction can model the alteration of force across levels. Dispersion can reveal differentiated unity. Polarisation can represent orientation. Interference can describe conflict and reinforcement. Coherence can clarify integrated action. Photosynthesis can embody transduction. None of these requires falsifying the underlying science.

## Where correspondence becomes weak

Correspondence becomes weak when technical terms are borrowed only to give authority. "Quantum", "frequency", "energy", "dimension", and "vibration" should not be used as decorations. When they refer to measurable quantities, their units and mechanisms should be stated. When they refer to metaphysical qualities, that different usage should be made explicit.

Responsible research also preserves multiple kinds of evidence. Measurement provides public quantitative evidence. Phenomenology provides disciplined accounts of lived experience. Historical testimony reveals continuity and variation across cultures. Symbolic interpretation discloses qualitative and relational meaning. Spiritual practice may produce experiential knowledge through participation. None of these forms should impersonate the others.

## A threefold classification

The system can therefore adopt a threefold classification. Some propositions are scientifically established: photons transfer energy, organisms emit ultraweak light, and circadian systems respond to illumination. Some are plausible correspondences: optical coherence offers a meaningful model of psychological integration. Others are metaphysical commitments: the etheric body is a real vital-formative organism and spiritual force precedes and animates physical form. These commitments may be supported by tradition, experience, philosophical reasoning, and accumulated accounts without being misrepresented as current laboratory conclusions.

Symbolic perception is not inferior to measurement. It answers different questions. Measurement asks how much, how fast, at what wavelength, and under what repeatable conditions. Symbolic perception asks what relation a phenomenon reveals, what pattern it participates in, and how it becomes meaningful within consciousness and life. A complete encyclopaedia requires both.

The final law of this division is therefore one of right relation: science should not be forced to authenticate claims beyond its evidence, and metaphysics should not be reduced to whatever current instruments can detect. Each mode of knowing becomes more truthful when it recognises its proper domain, states its limits, and enters coherent dialogue with the others.

Physical light shows how profoundly reality can be structured through emission, transmission, reception, transformation, and return. Metaphysical light asks why manifestation is intelligible at all, why consciousness can receive meaning, and how spirit becomes present within form. The two lights need not be confused in order to illuminate one another.

Coherence describes the stability of phase relationships within light. Temporal coherence concerns how predictably phase is maintained over time and relates to spectral purity. Spatial coherence concerns phase relationships across different parts of a wavefront.

Ordinary thermal sources emit light through vast numbers of largely independent events. Their phases and frequencies are broadly distributed. Laser light is produced through stimulated emission within an optical resonator, normally after a population inversion has been established in the active medium. The resulting field can possess high directional order, narrow spectral width, and strong coherence.

Laser means Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation. An incoming photon can stimulate an excited system to emit another photon matching key properties of the existing optical mode. Repetition amplifies an ordered field; [the Lindau Nobel Mediatheque](https://mediatheque.lindau-nobel.org/topics/laser) gathers the laureates' own accounts of this development.

## Organisation, not magnitude

Coherence should not be confused with total power. A modest coherent source can be focused and applied with great precision, while a more powerful incoherent source may disperse its energy broadly. Organisation changes efficacy.

This is one of the strongest correspondences between physical and spiritual light. Directed thought, desire, breath, emotion, and intention become more effective when they enter stable relation. The Psychic Flywheel is not strengthened merely by the amount of psychic energy added to it; contradictory impulses can interfere destructively. Coherence permits accumulation.

Yet spiritual coherence is broader than optical phase. It includes ethical alignment, psychological integration, sustained intention, correspondence between means and ends, and fidelity between force and form. Laser physics supplies the analogy, not the full metaphysical definition.

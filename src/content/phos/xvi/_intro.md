Modern science gives the encyclopaedia its most exact account of sensible and physically measurable light. It explains how electromagnetic radiation propagates, interacts with matter, transfers energy, forms images, activates living systems, and becomes visual experience. It also establishes the limits of those explanations.

Scientific optics does not demonstrate that every spiritual use of "light" refers to electromagnetic radiation. It likewise cannot conclude that realities lying outside its instruments and operational definitions do not exist. The purpose of this division is therefore neither to reduce metaphysical light to photons nor to use photons as automatic proof of metaphysical light. Its purpose is to distinguish levels precisely enough that genuine correspondences can become visible.

People can experience light without corresponding external illumination. Such experiences include phosphenes, afterimages, entoptic patterns, hypnagogic imagery, migraine aura, dream light, meditation-induced luminosity, and visionary phenomena.

Mechanical pressure on the eye can activate retinal cells and produce phosphenes. Electrical or magnetic stimulation of visual pathways can create spots or patterns of light. [[xi-20|Afterimages]] arise through adaptation and opponent processing. Retinal activity continues in darkness because biological sensory systems possess spontaneous activity and noise.

Migraine aura can generate expanding scintillating forms, zigzags, fortification patterns, blind regions, and coloured luminosities. Evidence associates many such experiences with [cortical spreading depression](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11858725/) moving across the visual cortex.

[[x-16|Hypnagogic imagery]] arises during the transition into sleep and can include flashes, geometric forms, faces, scenes, or sensed presences. Meditation practitioners also report diffuse fields, points, disks, jewel-like lights, luminous patterns, and environments saturated with radiance. [Scientific phenomenology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3879457/) has begun documenting these experiences rather than dismissing them as a single undifferentiated category.

## What a correlate settles

A neural correlate does not by itself determine the ultimate source or meaning of an experience. Every ordinary act of external vision also has neural correlates. Finding brain activity during a vision does not prove that the experience was externally caused, internally generated, spiritually mediated, or metaphysically false.

Discernment requires multiple levels of evidence. Does the phenomenon follow a known neurological pattern? Does it occur in one or both eyes? Does it move across the visual field in a characteristic time course? Is it associated with headache, sleep deprivation, medication, illness, or substance use? Does it contain stable information unavailable to the individual? Does it recur under disciplined spiritual conditions? What long-term effect does it have upon clarity and conduct?

The encyclopaedia must therefore avoid two symmetrical errors: declaring every inner light a higher revelation and declaring every inner light meaningless neural noise. Neural generation, symbolic meaning, psychic projection, and authentic spiritual encounter are not necessarily mutually exclusive in every case.

Anyone whose visual experiences are new, sudden, persistent, one-sided, or accompanied by pain, weakness, or confusion should seek medical assessment. Spiritual interpretation is never a substitute for appropriate medical care.

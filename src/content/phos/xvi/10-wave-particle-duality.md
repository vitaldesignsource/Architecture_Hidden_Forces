Wave-particle duality means that quantum entities do not fit completely into either classical category. Light exhibits interference, diffraction, and phase relationships associated with waves. It also produces discrete detection events and transfers energy in quanta associated with particles.

This does not mean that a photon is secretly a classical wave on one occasion and a classical particle on another. It means that both classical pictures are incomplete projections of a deeper quantum formalism. The experimental arrangement determines which properties can become manifest and what questions can meaningfully be asked.

## The double slit

In a double-slit experiment, individual photons arrive as localised detections. After many detections, an interference pattern appears. If the apparatus acquires reliable information about which path was taken, the interference pattern disappears. [NIST experiments](https://www.nist.gov/publications/time-resolved-double-slit-interference-pattern-measurement-entangled-photons) have recorded wave-like interference emerging from individual photon events.

The observer in quantum physics is not necessarily a conscious human mind. It can be an interaction with a detector or environment that makes alternatives distinguishable. Quantum theory therefore does not establish that human thought freely creates physical reality.

Its philosophical importance lies elsewhere. It shows that reality need not conform to the categories evolved through ordinary-scale experience. What becomes manifest depends partly upon the structure of the interaction. Complementary descriptions can each reveal something true without being reducible to one familiar picture.

The metaphysical lesson is humility. Reality may exceed the images used to describe it. The scientific result is not a licence to declare every paradox "quantum", but an invitation to distinguish the deeper reality from the limited forms through which it appears.

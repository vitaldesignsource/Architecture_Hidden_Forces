Modern physics says a great deal about electromagnetic fields, photons, radiation, matter, energy transfer, spacetime, quantum states, and measurement. It explains a vast range of luminous phenomena with extraordinary precision.

It does not currently identify physical light with consciousness, divine intellect, moral truth, the etheric body, astral substance, or spiritual revelation. None of these is an accepted consequence of Maxwell's equations, relativity, or quantum electrodynamics.

## Five things that do not follow

Quantum mechanics does not prove that personal intention can freely determine external events. Entanglement does not permit ordinary faster-than-light communication. Vacuum fluctuations are not scientific proof of a spiritual plenum. Higher dimensions in mathematical physics are not automatically astral planes. Frequency in hertz is not identical with spiritual rank.

At the same time, physics does not establish that only quantities described by present physical theories can exist. Science deliberately restricts itself to phenomena that can be operationally investigated through public methods. This methodological restriction is powerful, but it is not identical to a complete metaphysical doctrine.

A reality may initially lie beyond measurement because its effects are too subtle, the relevant instrument has not been invented, the phenomenon is irreducibly participatory, or it belongs to another order of being. Alternatively, it may lie beyond measurement because the proposed phenomenon does not exist. Science cannot decide between these possibilities without testable consequences.

In this encyclopaedia, metaphysical light remains a legitimate ontological category. It refers to self-manifestation, intelligence, formative power, spiritual presence, revelation, and the capacity by which hidden reality becomes disclosed. Physical light may participate in, symbolise, embody, or analogically resemble that deeper light without exhausting it.

The phrase *unmanifest light* initially appears contradictory. Light is ordinarily understood as that which appears and makes other things visible. How, then, could light remain unmanifest?

The contradiction dissolves when light is distinguished from visible brightness. Primordial light is not a concealed beam waiting somewhere in space. It is the capacity for disclosure still gathered within its source. It has not yet entered a medium, acquired direction, encountered resistance, or become available to a receiver.

Unmanifest light belongs closest to the [[ii-2|Field Before Form]]. It is not yet divided into colours, rays, intensities, or regions of illumination. It remains as an undifferentiated potency of manifestation.

## Darkness that shelters

At this level, darkness and light are not absolute enemies. [[vi-5|Generative darkness]] shelters the primordial light because the light has not yet separated itself from the hidden ground. The darkness is not empty; it is the concealment of unrealised luminosity.

Primordial light can therefore be described as the first capacity of being to disclose itself. It is not yet an appearance, but it makes every future appearance possible.

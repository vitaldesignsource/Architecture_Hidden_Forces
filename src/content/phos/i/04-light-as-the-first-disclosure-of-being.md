Being in complete hiddenness may be real, yet it has not entered relationship. Nothing has encountered it, distinguished it, received it, or spoken of it. Light represents the first movement by which being becomes available without ceasing to remain grounded in itself.

This is not necessarily a movement occurring in physical time. It is an ontological movement: the passage from being-in-itself toward being-present-to-another.

## The opening of the interval

Light is the first disclosure because it establishes the possibility of relation. It does not initially create a finished object. It opens the interval in which distinction, presence, perception, and form can arise. Through light, the hidden does not cease to be hidden entirely; it offers an aspect of itself to manifestation.

This first disclosure is the primordial generosity of being. Something that could have remained wholly concealed becomes communicable.

Light is therefore not identical with being, but it is the power through which being becomes present, knowable, and participable.

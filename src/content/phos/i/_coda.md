## The first axioms of the luminous architecture

Division I establishes the following principles.

1. Light is the power through which hidden reality becomes available to relation.
2. The principle of light precedes the particular luminaries that embody it.
3. Aphanic Light illuminates without becoming an ordinary object of appearance.
4. Every manifestation reveals selectively and therefore preserves some degree of hiddenness.
5. The source remains greater than the radiance that proceeds from it.
6. One light may generate many participations without being divided into unrelated origins.
7. Every medium qualifies what it transmits.
8. Every receiver participates in the final appearance of what is received.
9. Intensity does not guarantee clarity, truth, goodness, or transformation.
10. Physical, phenomenological, symbolic, and ontological light must remain distinct even when they correspond.
11. Darkness may signify privation, protection, latency, transcendence, or excess.
12. The purpose of illumination is not brightness alone, but truthful presence, intelligibility, actualisation, and transformation.

> Light is the first disclosure of the hidden, but it is not the exhaustion of mystery. It stands at the threshold between what remains beyond appearance and what has entered the architecture of manifestation.

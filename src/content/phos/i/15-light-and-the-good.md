Light has often been associated with the Good because it gives without immediately depriving, reveals without necessarily possessing, and enables beings to perceive and actualise their capacities.

The Good is not merely whatever appears pleasant or luminous. Physical light can burn. Knowledge can be used destructively. Psychic brilliance can inflate the personality. Spiritual language can conceal domination behind beautiful imagery.

## Good light

Light participates in goodness when it enters right relation. Good light reveals according to the capacity of the receiver, strengthens appropriate development, preserves necessary distinctions, and enables greater participation without creating enslavement to the source.

The goodness of light therefore lies not in brightness alone but in fecundity, proportion, truthfulness, and the forms of life it makes possible.

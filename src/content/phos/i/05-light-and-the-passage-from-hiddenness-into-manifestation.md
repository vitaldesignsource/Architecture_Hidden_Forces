Light mediates between concealment and appearance. It does not simply abolish hiddenness. It draws determinate aspects from an inexhaustible depth and gives them conditions under which they may appear.

The passage begins with a source that remains more than what it reveals. From that source proceeds an aphanic capacity for disclosure. This becomes radiance, acquires direction, passes through a medium, receives coloration, encounters form, and enters the perceptual field of a receiver.

Within the system this movement may be expressed as:

> Hidden Source → Luminous Potency → Radiance → Morphaithēr → Tattvic Coloration → Etheric Formation → Embodied Form → Perception

## Selective at every stage

At every stage something is revealed and something remains concealed. The source cannot be exhausted by its radiance. The radiance cannot pass through a medium without qualification. The form cannot embody every possibility of the force. The receiver cannot apprehend everything present within the form.

Manifestation is therefore always selective. To appear is to enter limitation. Yet limitation is not merely a fall or failure. Without limitation, no particular thing could become distinguishable.

Light brings the hidden into manifestation by entering right relationship with boundary.

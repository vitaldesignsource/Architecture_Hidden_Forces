Light is among the most powerful metaphysical images, but it cannot carry the entire weight of reality.

The metaphor privileges visibility. Yet existence also discloses itself through sound, touch, movement, absence, silence, resistance, memory, participation, and depth. Some truths are not seen but endured. Others are heard, embodied, practised, or slowly grown.

## Darkness is not evil

The equation of light with goodness and darkness with evil also becomes destructive when applied without discrimination. Darkness may conceal, but it also shelters. Seeds germinate beneath soil. The womb protects formation. Sleep restores the organism. Mystery prevents the finite mind from reducing the source to an object.

Exposure is not always revelation. Illumination imposed without right relation can violate, overwhelm, or destroy. Some realities must be approached gradually, indirectly, or through symbols capable of protecting what they reveal.

## Every light casts a boundary

Every light establishes a field and therefore a boundary. Whatever stands outside that field remains obscure. Whatever stands directly before the source may cast a shadow. Whatever exceeds the receiver's capacity may appear as [[vi-19|dazzling darkness]].

At its highest limit, even the concept of divine light must be surrendered. If the ultimate source precedes every distinction, it cannot be contained by the opposition between light and darkness. Light remains one of the greatest names for its self-disclosure, but it is not the source's final definition.

Where luminous language reaches its limit, [[vi-7|apophatic discipline]] begins.

These five principles constitute a fundamental luminous architecture.

- **Being** is the reality from which manifestation proceeds.
- **Light** is being's power of disclosure.
- **Life** is the capacity to receive, organise, and continue activity from within.
- **Intelligence** is the recognition of order and meaningful relation.
- **Form** is the determinate embodiment through which these principles become expressible.

They are not simply five separate substances. They are distinguishable moments within manifestation.

## Descent and return

Being without disclosure remains hidden. Light makes being participable. Life internalises activity and organises it through time. Intelligence apprehends and directs relation. Form gives the process boundary, persistence, and visible consequence.

In descent, light moves toward increasingly particular embodiment. In return, form becomes transparent to intelligence, intelligence recognises the living order supporting it, and life turns toward the being from which its capacity proceeds.

Light stands between hidden being and articulated form as the power of communicability.

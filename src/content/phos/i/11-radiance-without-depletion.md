Ordinary material giving involves division. What is transferred is no longer wholly possessed by the giver. Metaphysical radiance proposes another model: a source may communicate something of itself without ceasing to remain whole.

A flame lighting another flame provides a traditional image, although the physical flame still consumes fuel. Knowledge offers a closer analogy. A person may communicate an idea without losing the idea. The recipient participates in what the source possesses without removing it from the source.

## Participation, not emission

Radiance without depletion is therefore not a claim that physical energy can be emitted without consequence. It belongs to the metaphysics of participation. Being, intelligibility, goodness, and form may be communicated without being broken into material fragments.

The source remains abundant because its radiance is not a severed piece of itself. It is the source made participable under conditions appropriate to the receiver.

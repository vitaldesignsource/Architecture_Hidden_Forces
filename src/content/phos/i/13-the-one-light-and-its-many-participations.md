If one light enters many vessels, it does not appear identically through each. Clear glass transmits it, water bends it, a mirror redirects it, pigment absorbs and returns selected portions, mist diffuses it, and a living eye converts it into perception.

Difference of appearance does not necessarily indicate difference of ultimate source. It may indicate differences of mediation and participation.

## A generative unity

The One Light is not a uniform brightness erasing distinction. Its unity is generative. It permits many forms of participation because no finite receiver can embody its total possibility.

This does not mean every manifestation is equally faithful. Some vessels transmit clearly; others distort, fragment, appropriate, or obstruct. Plurality does not eliminate discernment.

The system therefore holds unity and multiplicity together. The source may be one while its participations are innumerable. Every participation is real, but none should claim to exhaust the light it receives.

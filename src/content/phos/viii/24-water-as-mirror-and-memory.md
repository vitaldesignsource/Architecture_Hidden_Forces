Water reflects light at its surface, refracts it through its depth, absorbs different wavelengths, carries dissolved substances, changes state, and circulates through organisms and environments.

These properties make water a natural symbol of memory. It receives impressions, reflects surrounding forms, carries material traces, and joins distant regions through circulation.

## Not water memory in the contested sense

The symbolic phrase *water memory* should not be confused with the contested scientific claim that ordinary water permanently retains specific therapeutic information after substances have been removed. The system does not require that claim.

Water's deeper memory lies in continuity, transport, embodied history, environmental trace, and the capacity of a living vessel to retain what has passed through it.

Water remembers architecturally through what it carries, shapes, dissolves, nourishes, and connects.

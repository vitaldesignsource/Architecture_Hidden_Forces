Life Ether is the function of integration, continuity, regeneration, and self-renewal. It gathers awakened, differentiated, and ordered processes into a living whole.

Life Ether is not an invisible substance added to biological matter, but neither is it a name for the coordination that biology already describes. It is a real mode of formative mediation, prior to the physical processes that embody it, through which many processes remain coordinated as one organism through time.

Its presence is recognised through integration. Parts do not merely coexist; they support a whole capable of growth, repair, adaptation, reproduction, and continuity of identity.

Within the soul, Life Ether appears as the power to integrate experience rather than remain fragmented by it. Within culture, it appears wherever institutions renew their forms without losing their essential purpose.

Its governing statement is:

> Life integrates.

Without Life Ether, order remains assembled but not alive.

The Four Ethers form the **Fourfold Veil** between undifferentiated possibility and embodied manifestation.

They are not separate layers stacked rigidly above matter. Their operations interpenetrate and recur. Every living process must continually awaken, differentiate, order, and integrate.

Warmth Ether initiates activity. Light Ether gives it orientation and distinction. Tone Ether establishes proportion and relation. Life Ether gathers these relations into continuity.

## Revealing and concealing

The Veil both reveals and conceals. It permits the source to become present while preventing unmediated force from overwhelming the receiving form. It translates power into stages that a finite vessel can bear.

The Fourfold Veil is therefore not an obstruction placed between spirit and matter. It is the structure that makes their communication possible.

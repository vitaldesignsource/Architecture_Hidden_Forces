**Tattvic secretion** describes the way a vessel expresses the formative qualities it has absorbed, cultivated, or metabolised. The word is borrowed from biology; the process it names is a real formative dynamic within Morphaithēr, and it may be described through the analogy without being reduced to it.

A person repeatedly shaped by Tejas may secrete intensity through speech, posture, and decision. An Apasic environment may communicate cohesion and emotional receptivity. A Pṛthivī-dominant institution may continually produce stability, repetition, and resistance to change.

**Tattvic saturation** occurs when one bias becomes so dominant that nearly every incoming force is interpreted and expressed through it.

Saturation can generate specialised power, but it narrows adaptability. A healthy vessel can emphasise one tattva without losing access to the others.

Secretion reveals what the vessel has become capable of radiating. Saturation reveals where capacity has become fixation.

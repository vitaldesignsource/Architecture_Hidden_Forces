Metals possess distinctive relationships with light, heat, electricity, magnetism, oxidation, and conductivity because of their physical structures and electronic properties. Their brilliance arises largely from how their electrons interact with incident light.

Alchemy and astrology associated metals with planetary powers: gold with the Sun, silver with the Moon, iron with Mars, copper with Venus, tin with Jupiter, lead with Saturn, and mercury with Mercury. These are historical symbolic correspondences rather than modern chemical classifications.

Within the system, metal is captured solar quality because it gives radiance durable material expression. It can reflect, conduct, resist, contain, and be transformed through heat.

Gold becomes solar not because it contains literal sunlight, but because its colour, durability, rarity, and resistance to corrosion make it an exceptional vessel of solar symbolism.

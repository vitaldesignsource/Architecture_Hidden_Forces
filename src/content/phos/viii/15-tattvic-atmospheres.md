A tattvic atmosphere is a field in which one or more morphogenic biases have become environmentally dominant.

A place may feel expansive and resonant, mobile and restless, hot and transformative, fluid and reflective, or dense and stabilising. These qualities arise through combinations of material conditions, weather, architecture, memory, activity, symbolism, and the receiving psyche.

A tattvic atmosphere is rarely pure. Most fields contain all five tendencies in changing proportions. What matters is predominance, relation, rhythm, and gunic condition.

The same Tejas atmosphere may be sattvic and clarifying, rajasic and agitated, or tamasic and destructive. Tattva describes direction; guna describes condition.

A tattvic atmosphere is the local weather of Morphaithēr.

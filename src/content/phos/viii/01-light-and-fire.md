Light and fire are among the most ancient symbols of manifestation because both make hidden activity visible. Fire radiates, warms, transforms, consumes, purifies, and releases what had been bound within form. Light reveals, differentiates, guides, colours, and establishes perceptible relation.

Their intimacy is physical as well as symbolic. Many forms of combustion emit visible light through incandescent particles, excited atoms and molecules, and chemical reactions. The flame is therefore matter in rapid transformation becoming visibly radiant.

Within the system, fire represents light entering an active transformative condition. Light discloses the form; fire changes it. Light makes relation visible; fire forces relation into a new state.

[[ii-22|Ignisophia]] unites these operations as fire governed by wisdom. It is luminosity made capable of deliberate transformation without surrendering discernment.

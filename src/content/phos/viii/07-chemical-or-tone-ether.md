The term **Tone Ether** is preferred within the system because it expresses relation, ratio, interval, resonance, and proportion. The older or alternative phrase **Chemical Ether** emphasises its role in affinity, combination, separation, assimilation, and exchange.

Tone Ether does not refer only to audible sound. Tone means ordered relation: the way distinct elements enter a patterned whole without losing their identities.

Chemical processes provide a material expression of this principle. Substances combine according to definite structures, proportions, affinities, and conditions. Yet Tone Ether should not replace chemical explanation. It names the broader formative function mirrored through chemical order.

Within the psyche, Tone establishes consonance among thought, feeling, desire, and action. Within ritual, it governs proportion and correspondence.

Its governing statement is:

> Tone orders.

## The doctrine of elemental and etheric illumination

The elemental doctrine of light can be stated as a sequence:

> Root Ether receives. Warmth Ether awakens. Light Ether differentiates. Tone Ether orders. Life Ether integrates. Tattvas bias. Gunas condition. Elements embody. Morphaithēr retains and relates.

Light and fire meet in transformation but remain distinct. Aether mediates without becoming a discarded physical ether. Tattvas incline without determining. Elements embody without exhausting the forces they express.

Crystal condenses transparency. Metal fixes radiance into durable reflection. Water receives and circulates. Air reveals transmission through atmosphere. Fire liberates and transforms. Earth gives luminous relation boundary and persistence.

The visible world is therefore not merely illuminated from outside. It is a continuous anatomy of luminous mediation in which matter reveals how force has entered form.

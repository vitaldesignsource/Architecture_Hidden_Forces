Tattvic refraction is the alteration of a luminous current as it passes through a field possessing a particular morphogenic bias.

The term is borrowed from optical refraction, and the borrowing is the analogy: metaphysical light does not obey a refractive index, and no numerical law is being asserted. The dynamic itself is not an analogy. It is a real operation within Morphaithēr, by which one influence genuinely changes direction or expression when it enters a differently organised medium — as real upon the formative plane as bending is upon the physical.

A principle entering an Ākāśic field may become contemplative and spacious. In Vāyu it becomes communicative and mobile. In Tejas it becomes transformative. In Apas it becomes relational and affective. In Pṛthivī it becomes structured and embodied.

The originating light may remain related to one source while its manifested path changes through the medium.

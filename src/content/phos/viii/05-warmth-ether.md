Warmth Ether is the function of activation. It marks the threshold where latent potential becomes operative force.

Physical heat provides its most immediate analogy, but Warmth Ether is not reducible to temperature. It includes animation, excitation, readiness, ignition, enthusiasm, metabolic awakening, and the first movement from rest into activity.

Warmth Ether does not yet determine the final form. It makes formation possible by awakening the field to participation.

Within the soul, warmth appears as interest, devotion, longing, courage, or the willingness to enter relationship. Within nature, it is expressed through activation, circulation, germination, and increased responsiveness.

Its governing statement is simple:

> Warmth awakens.

Without warmth, light may disclose but fail to animate. With excessive warmth, activation becomes agitation, inflammation, or uncontrolled fire.

Elemental coloration is the qualitative appearance acquired when light becomes associated with earth, water, air, fire, or aether.

Coloration includes more than hue. It involves texture, motion, temperature, density, direction, rhythm, and affect. Earth-light may be dense and stable. Water-light may be reflective and fluid. Air-light may be diffuse and mobile. Fire-light may be brilliant and transformative. Aetheric light may be subtle, connective, and spatially pervasive.

These associations are symbolic and phenomenological rather than fixed physical laws. A blue flame remains fiery, and dark soil may reflect brilliant light.

Elemental coloration describes how luminosity behaves, not merely how it looks.

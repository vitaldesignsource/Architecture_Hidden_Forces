In **Earth**, light is reflected, absorbed, mineralised, bounded, and fixed upon surfaces. Earth gives light texture and durable form.

In **Water**, light is reflected, refracted, scattered, absorbed, and set into moving caustic patterns. Water makes illumination fluid and relational.

In **Air**, light becomes atmospheric. It is scattered through gases, particles, mist, and changing density. Air makes beams visible and colours the sky.

In **Fire**, matter becomes luminous through excitation, reaction, and heat. Fire makes transformation visible.

In **Aether**, light becomes formatively transmissible. Aether is not another physical element beside the four but the metaphysical condition of formability through which elemental relations can communicate.

Together, the elements give light an embodied vocabulary.

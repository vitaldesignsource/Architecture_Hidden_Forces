Light Ether is the formative power of differentiation, orientation, and spatial articulation. It should not be equated directly with electromagnetic radiation, even though sensible light is its clearest physical analogue.

Light Ether makes distinctions possible. It separates inner from outer, direction from direction, surface from depth, and one developing function from another. It gives awakened activity an intelligible field within which to organise itself.

In the organism, Light Ether can be used to describe the formative principle through which living activity becomes spatially differentiated. In the psyche, it corresponds to the power of clear distinction. In thought, it appears as the capacity to perceive structure.

Its governing statement is:

> Light differentiates.

Without Light Ether, awakened activity remains undirected. In excess, differentiation may become fragmentation or overexposure.

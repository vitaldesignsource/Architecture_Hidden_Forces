Elemental, etheric, and tattvic lights describe three interrelated but distinct aspects of manifestation. The **ethers** are formative functions through which force becomes transmissible and capable of organisation. The **tattvas** are morphogenic biases that incline formative activity toward particular modes of expression. The **elements** are the more embodied conditions through which those tendencies become materially, psychically, and symbolically perceptible.

They should not be collapsed into a single correspondence chart. Warmth Ether is not simply Fire, Light Ether is not identical to Tejas, and Root Ether is not another name for Ākāśa. These principles intersect, but each answers a different question.

Ether explains how force becomes formable. Tattva explains toward what kind of form the current is inclined. Element describes the embodied behaviour through which the tendency becomes recognisable. Morphaithēr is the living atmosphere in which all three participate.

Tattvic resonance occurs when an incoming current encounters a receiver already organised around a compatible morphogenic bias.

Tejas awakens Tejas; movement stimulates movement; fluidity invites emotional and relational response; stable forms reinforce stability. The receiver responds strongly because it contains a corresponding readiness.

## Compatibility is not benefit

Resonance does not prove that the current is beneficial or truthful. A destructive pattern may resonate powerfully with an existing wound, fear, or habit. A balancing current may initially feel unfamiliar because the appropriate capacity has not yet developed.

Tattvic resonance reveals compatibility. [[ii-25|The Law of Right Relation]] determines whether that compatibility should be strengthened, moderated, redirected, or transformed.

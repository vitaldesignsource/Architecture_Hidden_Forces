Aether is the formative middle between force and form. It is not completed form but **formability**: the capacity through which influence becomes transmissible and acquires orientation, continuity, proportion, and relation.

## Not the luminiferous ether

This metaphysical aether should be distinguished from the discarded mechanical luminiferous ether once proposed as a physical medium for electromagnetic propagation. The system is not attempting to reintroduce that historical scientific model under another name.

At the deepest level stands **Root Ether**, the prequalitative receptive field through which relation can be carried. Root Ether is not a fifth substance placed beside the Four Ethers. It is their common receptive depth.

Aether is luminous because it permits hidden force to become differentiable. It provides the interval in which illumination can acquire direction, rhythm, quality, and eventual embodiment.

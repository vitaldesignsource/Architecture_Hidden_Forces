Saints and sages are human beings whose physical, etheric, astral, psychic, and spiritual members have become unusually coherent around a higher principle. Their luminosity arises not from public reputation alone but from the degree to which personal opacity has become transparent to spiritual light.

Such persons may radiate perceptible peace, authority, warmth, clarity, healing power, or visible and subtle light. Their presence may reorganise the psychic atmosphere of a place or awaken dormant capacities in others.

## Individuality consecrated

Transfiguration does not necessarily erase individuality. The person may become more distinctly themselves as distortions fall away and essence becomes more fully embodied. Sanctity is not the destruction of form but its consecration.

Accounts of luminous faces, incorrupt bodies, halos, radiant meditation, miraculous light, and post-mortem presence should be taken seriously without assuming that every hagiographical detail is equally literal. Historical embellishment may surround genuine spiritual manifestation.

The saint or sage demonstrates that embodied humanity can become a conscious mediator rather than merely a passive recipient of higher light.

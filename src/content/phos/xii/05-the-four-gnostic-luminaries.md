In Sethian Gnostic texts, particularly the *Apocryphon of John*, four Luminaries — Harmozel, Oroiael, Daveithe, and Eleleth — appear as great aeonic beings or luminous realms associated with Autogenes, the Self-Generated divine power. They structure the heavenly order beneath the supreme Invisible Spirit and Barbelo.

Harmozel, the first Luminary, is associated with grace, truth, and form. Oroiael, the second, is associated with afterthought or insight, perception, and memory. Daveithe, the third, is associated with understanding, love, and idea. Eleleth, the fourth, is associated with perfection, peace, and Sophia. Translations vary in their rendering of individual terms, but the fourfold architecture remains stable. The complete passage can be read in [Marvin Meyer's translation of the *Apocryphon of John*](http://www.gnosis.org/naghamm/apocjn-meyer.html).

## Chambers of participation

These Luminaries are not merely lamps inside a celestial building. They are living orders through which divine qualities become differentiated, inhabited, and transmitted. Each is both a presence and an aeonic domain: a being inseparable from the luminous order it governs.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, they demonstrate how one divine radiance may articulate itself into distinct chambers of participation without becoming divided at its source. Their arrangement also reveals that qualities such as truth, memory, love, peace, form, and wisdom are not accidental psychological states. They possess archetypal and aeonic foundations.

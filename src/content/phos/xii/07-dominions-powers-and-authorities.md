The middle Dionysian triad consists, in one common translation, of Dominions, Powers, and Authorities. Terminology varies among translations, but the underlying functions concern divine governance, capacity, order, and the disciplined transmission of power.

Dominions embody lordship without tyranny. They govern by remaining free from enslavement to lower compulsion and by orienting subordinate orders toward divine sovereignty. Their authority does not arise from domination but from alignment with the source of order.

Powers express courage, constancy, and an unflinching capacity to receive and transmit divine energy. They preserve the strength of an operation across levels, preventing force from collapsing when it encounters resistance.

Authorities govern the right ordering and distribution of power. They establish boundaries, jurisdictions, permissions, and relations among spiritual operations. Their function is not bureaucratic in a human sense; it is ontological administration.

## The disciplined descent of force

Together, this triad ensures that divine force does not descend as undifferentiated intensity. Dominion gives direction, Power gives effective capacity, and Authority gives lawful relation. Pseudo-Dionysius develops these functions in [*The Celestial Hierarchy*](http://www.esoteric.msu.edu/VolumeII/CelestialHierarchy.html).

A being's order may become perceptible through the quality rather than merely the intensity of its light. Unity, stability, texture, movement, temperature, colour, direction, atmosphere, intelligibility, and consequence all disclose something of the presence becoming manifest.

Divine light tends toward unity without monotony. It can contain immense differentiation while remaining centred and self-consistent. Angelic light often appears ordered, precise, ministerial, and proportionate to an office. Daimonic light may be more specialised, directional, environmental, or connected to a particular current. Heroic and ancestral light may carry recognisable personality, memory, lineage, or place. Psychic light is often more mutable, emotionally coloured, and responsive to the observer.

## Brilliance without coherence

Lower or mixed beings may display brilliance without coherence. Their light may fluctuate theatrically, demand fascination, imitate expected symbols, or obscure the being's actual intention. A higher presence may be overwhelming, but its power does not depend upon confusion.

Colour alone cannot reliably identify an order. No universal chart can prove that every blue light is angelic or every red light hostile. The total manifestation must be considered.

The order of a being is revealed by the relation among its light, intelligence, presence, action, and consequence.

The Greek *daimōn* should not be automatically translated as an evil demon. Daimons are intermediary beings and differentiated currents operating between gods and human life, intelligible principles and nature, destiny and character, place and event.

A daimon may carry the influence of a deity, planet, locality, vocation, lineage, natural power, or particular mode of life. It gives a general force a more specific path of activity. In this sense, daimons are living vectors of mediation.

## More than a complex

They are not merely psychological complexes, although they may work through temperament, desire, imagination, habit, and circumstance. A daimonic current can shape the kind of situations a person repeatedly encounters, the capacities seeking embodiment through them, and the characteristic tensions through which their life unfolds.

Daimons differ in order, intelligence, function, and moral relation. Some elevate, guard, instruct, or administer. Others bind consciousness more deeply to generation, appetite, conflict, or localised necessity. Their intermediary position gives them power but not automatic divine perfection.

A daimon must therefore be approached according to the deity, order, office, and field from which it proceeds.

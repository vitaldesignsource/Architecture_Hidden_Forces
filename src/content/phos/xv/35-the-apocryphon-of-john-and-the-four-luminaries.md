The *Apocryphon of John*, also called the Secret Book of John, survives in several Coptic recensions and is one of the foundational texts for understanding Sethian Gnostic cosmology. It presents a revelation of the risen Christ to John, explaining the invisible divine world, the emergence of lower creation, the formation of humanity, and the soul's recovery of its forgotten origin.

At the summit stands the invisible Spirit, beyond ordinary naming, categorisation, and limitation. From the divine self-contemplation emerges Barbelo, the First Thought and luminous maternal power. Through further divine activity appears Autogenes, the Self-Generated One, associated with Christ. The Fullness is not an empty abstraction. It is a living articulation of powers, aeons, intelligences, and relations within divine unity.

## The four

Associated with Autogenes are the Four Luminaries: Harmozel, Oroiael, Daveithai, and Eleleth. These are not physical suns or planets. They are great luminous orders within the divine realm, each surrounded by characteristic aeons or qualities. Harmozel is associated with grace, truth, and form; Oroiael with foreknowledge or reflection, perception, and memory; Daveithai with understanding, love, and idea; and Eleleth with perfection, peace, and Sophia. The wording and arrangement vary slightly among the surviving versions; [Wisse's translation](http://www.gnosis.org/naghamm/apocjn.html) gives one of them.

The Luminaries give structure to the otherwise immeasurable Fullness. They demonstrate that unity does not exclude articulated order. Divine qualities can be distinguished without becoming mutually alienated. Each Luminary is a field of participation within which compatible powers dwell and operate.

The text also connects the Luminaries with archetypal humanity and its spiritual generations. Different classes or aspects of the incorruptible human family are placed within their respective realms. The Four Luminaries therefore mediate between transcendent divinity and the spiritual history of humanity. They are simultaneously cosmic stations, intelligible orders, and habitations of awakened identity.

Eleleth plays an especially important mediating role in several Sethian writings. As the fourth Luminary, Eleleth stands nearest the boundary at which the drama of [[xii-4|Sophia]], lower creation, and humanity becomes visible. This does not make Eleleth merely lower or defective. It gives this Luminary a revelatory function at the threshold between the stable Fullness and the disorder arising beyond it.

## The fourfold pattern

The fourfold pattern is particularly significant. Four often signifies the first complete extension of a principle into an ordered field: four directions, four foundations, four elemental modalities, or four stations of a transmitted power. The Sethian Four Luminaries may therefore be read as an architecture through which divine light establishes differentiated but harmonious regions.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, the Four Luminaries should not simply be renamed as [[ii-7|the Four Ethers]] or [[ii-6|the Fourfold Veil]]. Historically, they belong to a specific Sethian divine genealogy. Structurally, however, they demonstrate a related law: a transcendent source becomes communicable through a fourfold mediation without being exhausted by its mediators.

They may be compared to four great chambers of noetic light, each preserving a distinct family of qualities. Harmozel gathers the light of gracious form and truth; Oroiael the light of perception and memory; Daveithai the light of understanding, love, and intelligible conception; Eleleth the light of peace, completion, and wisdom. Together they reveal that luminous unity possesses internal articulation.

The Four Luminaries consequently offer an ancient precedent for understanding light as an ordered plurality of intelligences and qualities. Light does not become many only when it reaches matter. Differentiation already possesses a sacred archetype within the higher worlds; [[xii-5|the Portal's own account of the Luminaries]] draws the same line.

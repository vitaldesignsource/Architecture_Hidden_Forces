The traditions assembled here share a language of light, knowledge, ascent, divine mind, cosmic mixture, and spiritual liberation, but they must not be treated as interchangeable. Hermetic writings generally regard the cosmos as a divinely ordered living image, even when the embodied soul becomes subject to ignorance and fate. Sethian and related Gnostic writings distinguish the transcendent Fullness from lower and sometimes defective cosmic powers. Manichaeism develops a radical conflict between eternally opposed realms of Light and Darkness. Mandaeism, an enduring religious tradition rather than merely an extinct historical "Gnostic sect", places living souls, luminous beings, sacred knowledge, and flowing water within a continuing ritual relationship with the World of Light.

Each tradition contributes something different to the luminous architecture. Hermetism reveals light as Mind communicating order to dark, receptive Nature. Sethian texts describe articulated luminaries within the divine Fullness. Pistis Sophia explores the attraction of counterfeit light and the theft and restoration of luminous power. The Books of Jeu map ascension through names, seals, diagrams, and treasuries. Manichaeism makes the liberation of captive light the central labour of cosmic history. Mandaeism presents light not only as height and radiance but as Life, knowledge, flowing water, ritual purification, and return.

The comparisons made here are architectural rather than claims that all these communities secretly taught an identical doctrine. Their historical differences remain essential. Nevertheless, each preserves testimony concerning realities that the modern division between "literal" and "symbolic" often fails to comprehend. Their symbols were not necessarily regarded as arbitrary metaphors. They could be maps, vehicles, manifestations, and operative relations within a living cosmos.

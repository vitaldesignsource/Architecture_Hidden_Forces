Manichaeism was founded in the third century by Mani and spread with remarkable success from the Roman world through Iran and Central Asia into China. Its scriptures, art, hymns, and institutions appeared in numerous languages. Although much of its literature was destroyed or survived only fragmentarily, the recovered sources reveal one of history's most elaborate cosmologies of light.

In the beginning, the Realm or Paradise of Light and the Realm of Darkness exist separately. The Light-Realm is uncreated, eternal, peaceful, intelligent, and governed by the Father of Greatness. Darkness possesses its own chaotic powers and rulers. Unlike Plotinian metaphysics, Manichaeism does not ordinarily treat darkness as the mere attenuation or [[vi-3|privation]] of one ultimate Good. It gives Light and Darkness an original opposition.

## Two realms

This distinction is fundamental. The Manichaean drama is not simply the descent of one divine source through gradually denser levels. It is the collision of two contrary realms. When the powers of Darkness approach and attack the Light, the Father of Greatness calls forth a series of divine beings to defend the luminous kingdom.

The Mother of Life evokes the First Man, who enters the conflict clothed in five living elements of Light. The First Man is overwhelmed, and his luminous armour is devoured by the powers of Darkness. Although the First Man himself is eventually rescued, portions of the Light remain trapped and mixed within the dark powers.

The present cosmos is constructed as part of the divine strategy for recovering this captured Light. The universe is therefore both battleground and purification apparatus. Its heavens, earths, celestial mechanisms, living organisms, and human bodies contain varying mixtures of the opposed substances.

The Light-Realm remains beyond mixture even while its powers labour within mixture. It is both the origin of the captive light and the destination toward which purified light returns. Salvation is therefore not only the rescue of individual persons. It is the gradual restoration of a wounded cosmic totality.

[Encyclopaedia Iranica's account of Manichaean cosmogony](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/cosmogony-iii/) summarises the primordial invasion, the sending of the First Man and his five sons, their partial devouring by the Archons of Darkness, and the subsequent construction of a mechanism through which imprisoned particles of light can be redeemed.

## What the Architecture keeps

The Architecture of Hidden Forces should not adopt the Manichaean identification of matter with an independently evil kingdom without qualification. This encyclopaedia understands matter as capable of consecration, [[ii-26|spiritualisation]], and truthful embodiment; [[vi-26|evil and darkness must not be equated]]. Yet Manichaeism preserves an extraordinarily powerful account of what occurs when living force becomes trapped inside an architecture that no longer serves its source.

Its enduring insight is not that every material form is evil. It is that a structure can capture a power whose origin and purpose transcend that structure. Once imprisoned, the power may be used to maintain the very system that confines it. Liberation then requires not indiscriminate destruction of form, but recognition, separation, purification, and return — the work the Portal names [[v-20|captive light]].

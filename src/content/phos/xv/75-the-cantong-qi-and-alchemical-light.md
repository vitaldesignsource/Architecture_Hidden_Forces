The Zhouyi Cantong Qi, usually translated as The Seal of the Unity of the Three, became one of the foundational scriptures of Chinese alchemy. Tradition attributes it to Wei Boyang in the second century CE, but [current scholarship](https://www.goldenelixir.com/jindan/zyctq_2.html) generally places the received work's formation between approximately the fifth and seventh centuries. Its "three" refer broadly to interconnected bodies of teaching: the Book of Changes, Daoist cosmology, and alchemical practice.

The text communicates through an intentionally compressed language of trigrams, metals, furnace and crucible, lead and mercury, dragon and tiger, sun and moon, fire and water. These images possess several simultaneous registers. They can refer to laboratory materials and procedures, cosmic forces, temporal cycles, bodily processes, and spiritual operations. Their symbolic character does not make them unreal. Symbol is the instrument by which relations across different planes are disclosed and coordinated.

## True yang within yin

The trigrams Qian and Kun represent the active and receptive principles in their primordial integrity. Kan and Li - Water and Fire - represent these principles after they have become interwoven within manifestation: Water conceals true yang, while Fire contains true yin. Alchemical work seeks the authentic seed hidden inside conditioned appearances. It separates the true force from its accidental container and reunites complementary powers in a properly prepared vessel.

The scripture consequently counsels the adept to know the white while guarding the black, allowing numinous light to arise through their ordered conjunction. It also speaks of concealing outward light while illuminating the body inwardly. Alchemical luminosity is generated neither by rejecting darkness nor by maximising brightness. It emerges when the hidden yang within yin and hidden yin within yang are recognised and brought into a stable union. [Selections from Fabrizio Pregadio's translation](https://www.goldenelixir.com/jindan/ctq_18.html) include these teachings on concealed light, inner illumination, and numinous radiance.

## The vessel

Within this encyclopaedia, the alchemical vessel corresponds to the consciously ordered subtle organism. [[xiii-22|Solve]] separates captive light from damaged, accidental, or exhausted formations. [[xiii-23|Coagula]] gives that liberated power a more truthful embodiment. The work succeeds when the new architecture can hold an increased intensity of spirit without fragmentation, inflation, leakage, or domination by psychic appetite.

The First Man's five sons, garments, weapons, or elements are usually identified as ether or air, wind, light, water, and fire. Terminology differs across Syriac, Middle Persian, Parthian, Coptic, Arabic, and Chinese sources, but the fivefold luminous structure remains central.

These elements should not be reduced to the familiar terrestrial elements of later European occultism. They are living divine powers and together constitute the armour or soul of the First Man. Their elemental names describe modes of spiritual substance: extension, movement, radiance, liquidity, vitality, and transformative power.

## The five modes

Ether is the most spacious and subtle of the five, expressing luminous extension and the capacity within which the other powers operate. Wind is movement, breath, transmission, and directed vitality. Light is disclosure, visibility, intelligence, and immediate radiance. Water is living fluidity, cohesion, purification, and the power to carry life. Fire is activation, transformation, heat, and ascending force.

These descriptions are interpretive rather than a rigid historical table. In Manichaean myth the elements are simultaneously substances, powers, divine sons, weapons, and parts of the Living Soul. Ancient religious categories did not always divide person, force, element, and intelligence as modern classification does.

When the five are devoured by the powers of Darkness, they become the Living Soul dispersed within matter. This is not merely an analogy for spiritual potential. Within Manichaean ontology, the living elements are genuine divine substance undergoing imprisonment, mixture, pain, and eventual rescue. [Encyclopaedia Iranica's survey of Manichaeism](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/manicheism-1-general-survey/) identifies the five and describes them as the Living Soul imprisoned and divided within dark matter.

The fivefold armour reveals that light is internally differentiated. It is not just white brilliance. It possesses atmosphere, motion, visibility, liquidity, and transformative heat. These modalities work together as a living body. The loss of one weakens the integrity of the whole.

## Before the material elements

There is a productive comparison here with [[ii-7|the Four Ethers]] and [[viii-15|the tattvic atmospheres]] of the present system, but identity should not be imposed. The Manichaean pentad belongs to its own cosmic conflict. Structurally, however, it demonstrates how a spiritual power may differentiate itself into elemental functions before those functions become the familiar material elements.

The five living elements can therefore be read as pre-material elemental intelligences. Their terrestrial counterparts are not their complete reality, but condensed or mixed expressions of higher powers. Etheric space, breath, radiance, living water, and fire are ways in which spiritual activity approaches embodiment — [[viii-21|light in earth, water, air, fire, and aether]] seen from above.

The fall of the luminous elements also demonstrates why spiritual force requires truthful architecture. Power without adequate relation can be captured, scattered, and turned toward ends contrary to its nature. The armour protects only while its elements remain coherently organised around the First Man. Once separated and swallowed, the same powers become vulnerable to exploitation.

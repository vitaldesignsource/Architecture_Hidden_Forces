Suhrawardī's luminous cosmos is populated by differentiated orders rather than an undivided field of brightness. Two especially important categories are the "victorial" or "dominating lights" - *al-anwār al-qāhira* - and the "governing lights" - *al-anwār al-mudabbira*.

"Victorial" does not primarily mean militarily aggressive. The Arabic root suggests dominance, overwhelming superiority, and the power by which a higher reality determines or illuminates a lower one. A victorial light is not bound to a material body. It belongs to an incorporeal order and exercises influence through the intensity and perfection of its luminosity.

## Vertical and horizontal orders

These lights possess vertical and horizontal relations. In the vertical or longitudinal order, higher lights generate subordinate lights through relations of illumination, dependence, love, and dominance. Each lower light desires the perfection above it and illuminates what lies below. The result is not a simple chain of identical beings but an immense graded architecture.

Suhrawardī's horizontal or latitudinal order includes the archetypal "lords of species". These have often been compared with Platonic Forms, but Suhrawardī understands them as living luminous principles rather than inert conceptual templates. A species is not organised merely by a definition invented by the human mind. It possesses an intelligible governing reality, a luminous lord through which its characteristic form and powers become manifest.

## The Isfahbad-light

The governing lights are souls responsible for the administration or animation of bodies. The human soul is a governing light because it is not identical with the material organism, although it operates through that organism. Suhrawardī sometimes calls the individual human soul the Isfahbad-light, using an Iranian title meaning something like commander or ruling lord. It unifies perception, governs imagination, and receives influences from higher dominating lights.

This relation resembles spirit inhabiting and animating form without being reducible to form. The body supplies localisation, limitation, sensation, and a field of action. The governing light supplies unity, awareness, orientation, and purposive animation. Their conjunction produces embodied life, yet their difference allows the soul to encounter nonbodily realities and potentially survive bodily dissolution.

The soul's imagination occupies an especially important intermediate position. It can receive sensory images from below and luminous influences from above. In an undisciplined soul these currents become mixed, distorted, or projected. In a refined soul imagination becomes a mirror capable of clothing higher intelligible realities in authentic symbolic forms. [Suhrawardī's managing soul](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/suhrawardi/) is described as accessing the dominating lights and reflecting what it receives into the active imagination.

## Precedence and right relation

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, the victorial lights correspond to transcendent and archetypal forces whose authority arises from ontological precedence. Governing lights correspond to intelligences and souls that administer particular fields, organisms, stars, species, or psychic structures. The lords of species resemble the [[xii-19|logoi]] or formative powers that make a living form recognisably what it is.

This hierarchy also clarifies right relation. A lower power becomes disordered when it presents itself as the absolute source of what it merely transmits. A governing light remains truthful when it mediates the higher order appropriate to it. It becomes tyrannical or deceptive when it closes the channel above itself and demands the reverence owed to the Light of Lights.

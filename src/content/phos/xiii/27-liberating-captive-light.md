[[v-20|Captive light]] is living force prevented from completing its proper expression. It may be held in a traumatic memory, compulsive desire, rigid institution, exhausted doctrine, dying form, obsessive image, inherited psychic pattern, or material structure whose original purpose has been forgotten.

Liberation begins by distinguishing light from the architecture that confines it. One must avoid destroying the living principle along with the damaged form. The aim is not simply to break the vessel but to recover what within it remains capable of truth.

## The centre of alchemy

This operation lies at the centre of alchemy. The hidden radiance within confused matter is separated, purified, strengthened, and prepared for a more adequate embodiment. Liberating captive light transforms waste, suffering, failure, and residue into possible material for further creation.

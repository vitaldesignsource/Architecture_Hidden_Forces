Transfiguration is the operation through which an existing form becomes transparent to a higher degree of light without ceasing to be itself. The form is neither discarded nor covered with a decorative radiance. Its inward principle becomes outwardly perceptible.

A transfigured face reveals the soul through the body. A transfigured symbol ceases to be a static sign and becomes a living interface. A transfigured material object communicates qualities beyond its ordinary utility. In spiritual transfiguration, embodied life becomes capable of expressing the presence that animates it.

## Spiritualised matter

Transfiguration is one of the clearest images of spiritualised matter. The lower is not abolished by the higher; it is fulfilled. Matter becomes capable of bearing spirit without claiming to be its source, while spirit becomes effectively present without abandoning form.

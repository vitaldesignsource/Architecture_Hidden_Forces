Healing is the restoration of right relation within and among the levels of a living being. Illness may arise from physical injury, infection, inherited condition, environmental exposure, psychological disturbance, relational trauma, spiritual disorder, etheric disruption, or several causes acting together. It should neither be reduced to spiritual failure nor stripped of every possible spiritual dimension.

Luminous healing seeks to discover where communication has broken down. The body may no longer receive adequate vitality; psychic conflict may disturb etheric rhythm; spiritual alienation may deprive life of meaning; or bodily illness may place pressure upon the psychic and spiritual life. Because influence can travel in several directions, healing must not assume that one level explains every condition.

## What healing restores

Healing light restores proportion, circulation, communication, and integrity. Its success is not always equivalent to the removal of every symptom. At times healing means recovery; at other times it means adaptation, reconciliation, transformation, or the restoration of spiritual coherence within an enduring physical condition.

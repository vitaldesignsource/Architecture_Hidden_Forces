Metastoicheiōsis refers to transformation at the level of elemental constitution. Instead of merely changing the visible arrangement of a form, it changes the governing relations among the forces composing it.

Within [[viii-26|Morphaithēr]], trans-elementation can involve a profound reordering of elemental and tattvic qualities. Earth ceases to be inert fixation and becomes stable embodiment. Water ceases to be emotional dissolution and becomes receptive memory. Air ceases to be restless dispersal and becomes articulate communication. Fire ceases to be uncontrolled consumption and becomes consecrating intelligence. Aether brings the differentiated elements into a higher field of coordination.

## What is elevated

Trans-elementation does not necessarily mean that one physical element literally becomes another. It means that the operative quality of the elemental composition has been elevated, rebalanced, or placed under a different governing principle. The same material may begin to participate in an entirely different order of life.

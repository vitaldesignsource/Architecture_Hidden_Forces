Light does not always vanish when its immediate appearance ends. Encounters leave impressions upon bodies, nervous systems, souls, places, communities, and subtle fields. Luminous imprinting is the operation through which an organised influence establishes a continuing pattern within a receiver.

At different levels this can appear as physical trace, biological adaptation, neurological memory, emotional association, psychic image, etheric habit, ritual atmosphere, ancestral inheritance, or spiritual recollection. These levels should not be confused, but neither should they be treated as entirely unrelated.

## The Crypt and the Ossuary

[[ii-27|The Crypt of Primordial Memory]] names the deeper retentive dimension through which existence inherits the consequences of previous formation. [[ii-28|The Ossuary of Living Forms]] preserves the residues of structures whose visible organisation has dissolved. Memory is therefore not merely a private mental archive; it is one of the principal means by which the past continues to participate in the formation of the present.

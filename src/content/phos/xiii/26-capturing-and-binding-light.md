All embodiment involves some form of luminous capture. Plants bind solar energy into living tissue. The eye receives and retains luminous information. Memory preserves moments of illumination. Symbols bind meaning into image, while temples, ritual objects, crystals, metals, relics, and consecrated substances may become vessels for particular currents.

Binding light is therefore not inherently corrupt. A force must be concentrated if it is to act through a definite form. The question is whether the binding serves manifestation or imprisonment. Legitimate binding maintains a living relationship among source, vessel, purpose, and recipient.

## Corrupt binding

Corrupt binding attempts to possess light while severing it from its proper circulation. It treats spiritual force as property, captures psychic energy through manipulation, or uses consecrated forms for purposes contrary to their original orientation. What was created as a vessel can then become a prison.

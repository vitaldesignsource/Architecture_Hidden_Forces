Exposure is the operation through which light brings concealed realities into visibility. It can reveal deception, corruption, unacknowledged pain, hidden motives, forgotten memory, structural weakness, or a spiritual presence previously acting below the threshold of awareness.

Exposure can be liberating, because what remains concealed cannot easily be brought into conscious relation. Yet exposure is not automatically merciful. A seed requires darkness before it can withstand the sun. Psychic contents may require gradual integration. Mysteries may be concealed because the receiver lacks the preparation needed to encounter them without distortion.

## Timing, proportion, purpose

Right exposure is governed by timing, proportion, and purpose. It illuminates what must be seen while respecting what is still undergoing formation. Light becomes destructive when it exposes merely to dominate, shame, overwhelm, or strip something of the darkness necessary for gestation.

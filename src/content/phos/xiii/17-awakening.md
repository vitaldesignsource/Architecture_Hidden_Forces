Awakening is the transition from latent consciousness to active participation. A sleeping capacity begins to perceive, recognise, choose, and respond. The person does not merely acquire new information; the centre from which experience is understood begins to change.

Awakening may be initiated by beauty, suffering, discipline, contemplation, spiritual encounter, visionary experience, intellectual discovery, or the collapse of a former identity. It may be sudden in its beginning but ordinarily requires a long process of integration. A moment of awakening does not eliminate every habit belonging to the previous condition.

## Measured by consequence

Its truth is measured through consequence. Genuine awakening increases attentiveness, freedom, responsibility, discernment, and the ability to enter right relation. An experience that produces only fascination, superiority, or immunity from correction may have awakened psychic intensity without awakening spiritual intelligence.

Integration is the operation through which differentiated illuminations are brought into a coherent relationship. It is not the flattening of all distinctions. It gives every genuine experience, insight, force, and level its correct place within the whole.

Without integration, visionary experiences remain isolated from conduct, intellectual knowledge remains separated from the body, and spiritual ideals remain disconnected from emotional life. Such divisions create an architecture filled with luminous fragments but lacking a central order. Psychic inflation often occurs when one powerful fragment is mistaken for the whole.

## From revelation to action

Integration allows revelation to become understanding, understanding to become character, and character to become action. It joins spirit, soul, etheric vitality, and body without reducing one to another. The integrated person becomes a more stable vessel because multiple centres of life have begun to revolve around a shared orientation.

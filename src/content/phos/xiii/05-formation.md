Formation is the operation by which light gives proportion, boundary, rhythm, direction, and intelligible organisation to force. Undifferentiated potency does not yet constitute a living form. It must be patterned, related, limited, and stabilised. Formative light accomplishes this by awakening particular possibilities within a receptive medium.

Within [[viii-26|Morphaithēr]], light encounters a living atmosphere already conditioned by tattvic biases, etheric movements, residual impressions, elemental mixtures, and psychic influences. Formation is therefore not the imposition of a geometrical plan upon entirely passive matter. It is a meeting between an ordering power and a responsive field.

## The measure of a form

A form is successful when it allows its governing force to act without either dispersing or becoming imprisoned. Too little boundary produces incoherence; too much fixation produces stagnation. True formation creates a vessel firm enough to hold light and transparent enough to transmit it.

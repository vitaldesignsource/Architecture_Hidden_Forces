Light must be studied not only according to its sources, qualities, appearances, and gradations, but also according to its operations. An operation of light is something light accomplishes through its presence: it reveals, differentiates, organises, awakens, animates, purifies, judges, dissolves, and transforms. Sensible light performs these operations within physical visibility, but the same pattern can appear at etheric, psychic, noetic, spiritual, and divine levels.

Within the Architecture of Hidden Forces, light is therefore more than an object that travels through space. It is one of the primary ways in which spirit and force become communicable to form. Yet light never operates alone. Every operation involves a source, a ray or current, a medium, a receiving structure, and a consequence. The nature of the result depends not only upon the purity of the light but also upon the condition of the medium and the capacity of the receiver.

Metamorphōsis is transformation in which the governing relation between force and form is fundamentally changed. It is more than movement, growth, disguise, or alteration. Something persists through the transformation, yet it no longer exists in the same manner.

The traditional image of a creature passing through enclosure, dissolution, and emergence is powerful because metamorphosis often requires an intermediate condition in which the old form has been relinquished but the new form is not yet complete. This interval can resemble darkness, confusion, or death. It is a protected crisis of reorganisation.

## New capacities

Authentic metamorphosis becomes visible through new capacities. The transformed being can perceive, relate, and act in ways unavailable to the previous form. Change becomes metamorphosis when the architecture of possibility itself has been renewed.

Inspiration is the entrance of a formative current into the soul. Its ancient association with breathing is exact: something is received, taken inward, and given expression through the life of the recipient. Inspiration may arrive as an idea, image, melody, symbol, method, doctrine, healing impulse, artistic vision, or sudden knowledge of what must be done.

The inspired person is neither the sole manufacturer nor a passive instrument. The incoming current passes through character, memory, language, education, culture, desire, psychic structure, etheric condition, and bodily capacity. The final expression bears both the signature of its source and the coloration of the receiver.

## Craft as vessel

Disciplined inspiration requires purification and craft. A higher idea can be damaged by an inadequate vessel. Training gives inspiration an architecture capable of receiving it without either smothering it or allowing it to dissipate into unformed enthusiasm.

Orientation is the operation through which light establishes direction. The plant turns toward the sun; the eye turns toward brightness; attention moves toward what consciousness experiences as valuable, dangerous, beautiful, desirable, or true. Light does not merely reveal a world — it creates centres of attraction within it.

Spiritual orientation concerns the principle around which the entire person becomes organised. [[x-8|Attention]] serves as a directed ray; [[x-9|intention]] gives that ray continuity; desire supplies motion; discipline establishes a path; action gives the orientation material consequence. A person's true orientation is shown not only by professed belief but by the patterns toward which their energy repeatedly flows.

## Vertical and horizontal

Right orientation does not mean neglecting the earthly in favour of the heavenly. It places spirit, soul, etheric life, body, relationships, work, and environment within a correct vertical and horizontal relation. The higher must illuminate the lower without abandoning it, and the lower must embody the higher without claiming to contain it completely.

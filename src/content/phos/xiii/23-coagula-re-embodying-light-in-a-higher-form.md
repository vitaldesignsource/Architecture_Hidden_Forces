Coagula gathers liberated light into a new vessel. If solve breaks an obsolete relation, coagula establishes a more adequate one. The operation is complete only when transformation becomes embodied.

A higher form is not necessarily grander, more complex, or more impressive. It is higher insofar as it transmits its governing force more truthfully. A simple practice faithfully performed may be a higher coagulation than an elaborate ritual lacking inward correspondence. A modest institution serving life may embody more light than a magnificent structure devoted to preserving itself.

## Tested over time

Through coagula, insight becomes discipline, healing becomes a changed pattern of life, revelation becomes doctrine and practice, and liberated force becomes constructive power. The new vessel must be tested over time. A form that holds light only during moments of intensity has not yet achieved stable coagulation.

Revelation is illumination carrying meaningful disclosure from beyond the ordinary range of the receiver. It is not merely the accumulation of information. In revelation, a hidden reality communicates something of its own nature. The experience may arrive through a dream, vision, symbol, sacred text, sudden intuition, divine presence, synchronistic event, or direct act of spiritual intelligence.

## Always through mediation

Revelation always passes through mediation. Even when its source is genuinely higher, it must enter the soul through images, concepts, emotions, sensations, memories, and culturally available forms. What is received may therefore contain both authentic light and the coloration of the receiver. The existence of interpretation does not make revelation unreal; it means revelation must be discerned according to its passage through the human architecture.

The value of a revelation is not proven by brilliance, strangeness, or intensity. Its deeper signs are coherence, ethical consequence, enduring intelligibility, spiritual fruitfulness, and compatibility with the Law of Right Relation. Genuine revelation makes the receiver more capable of truth. Counterfeit revelation often makes the receiver more certain while making them less capable of correction.

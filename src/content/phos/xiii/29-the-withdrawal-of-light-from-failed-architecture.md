An architecture may survive externally after the light that animated it has begun to withdraw. Its language, offices, rituals, images, and procedures remain, but they no longer transmit the living force that originally justified their existence. The structure becomes increasingly occupied with maintaining its appearance.

Withdrawal can occur when a form persistently obstructs, distorts, or exploits its governing light. The higher principle does not always destroy the form dramatically. It may simply cease to sustain the relationship. What remains is a shell operating through habit, accumulated momentum, residual psychic energy, or institutional power.

## Withdrawal as mercy

This withdrawal is not necessarily divine rejection. It may be an act of mercy toward both the light and the vessel. By withdrawing, the source prevents further distortion and permits the exhausted structure to enter dissolution. The released force may then become available for a more truthful architecture.

Protection is the operation by which light establishes and maintains a truthful boundary. A living system cannot remain healthy if it is entirely closed, but neither can it survive if it indiscriminately admits every influence. Protection governs exchange.

At the physical level, protection may involve shelter, distance, immune response, and bodily boundaries. At the psychic level, it includes disciplined attention, emotional differentiation, and freedom from manipulative images. Etheric protection preserves the integrity and circulation of the vital-formative field. Spiritual protection establishes allegiance to a higher order whose coherence limits the power of incompatible influences.

## An intelligent boundary

A luminous boundary is intelligent rather than rigid. It recognises what may enter, what must be transformed, what should be reflected, and what must be refused. Protection founded only upon fear can become another prison. Protection founded upon right relation preserves openness without surrendering sovereignty.

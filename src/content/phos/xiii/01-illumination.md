Illumination is the primary operation by which something becomes available to awareness. Physical illumination allows the eye to distinguish objects, surfaces, distances, colours, and movements. Psychic illumination makes an emotion, memory, desire, or inner image perceptible to consciousness. Noetic illumination allows an intelligible principle to be grasped directly. Spiritual illumination makes the presence of a higher order recognisable within the soul.

Illumination does not always create what it reveals. A structure may have existed in darkness before light disclosed it. Likewise, an unconscious condition may already be influencing a person before it becomes consciously understood. Illumination changes the relationship between the concealed thing and the witness: what acted invisibly can now be encountered, interpreted, accepted, resisted, or transformed.

## Gift and responsibility

This is why illumination is both a gift and a responsibility. Once something has been genuinely illuminated, the receiver can no longer relate to it with complete innocence. Knowledge places the soul under an obligation to respond.

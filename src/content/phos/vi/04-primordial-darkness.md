Primordial Darkness is the depth preceding the distinction between visible and invisible, light and shadow, subject and object, or form and field.

It is not a black room existing before creation. A black room is already a formed space perceived through contrast. Primordial Darkness is the unarticulated condition before such distinctions have become available.

Within the [[ii-2|Field Before Form]], this darkness contains no finished objects waiting to be exposed. It holds undifferentiated possibility — the capacity from which both luminous disclosure and determinate form may arise.

Primordial Darkness should therefore not be interpreted as evil, chaos in the merely destructive sense, or the defeat of light. It is the hidden depth from which the first disclosure becomes possible.

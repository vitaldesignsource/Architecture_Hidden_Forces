*Nigredo*, the blackening, is the alchemical condition of dissolution, decomposition, confusion, and the breakdown of a former order.

The withdrawal of light in nigredo is the loss of an established orientation. Familiar identities and meanings no longer illuminate experience. The matter of the work appears dark because its previous form is being disassembled.

## Not transformation completed

Nigredo is not transformation completed. It is the condition in which transformation becomes unavoidable. Its darkness may be fertile, but fertility is not guaranteed. Without containment and integration, dissolution can remain fragmentation.

The Black Pearl of Becoming belongs to this depth: wisdom condensed within darkness after superficial brilliance has been stripped away.

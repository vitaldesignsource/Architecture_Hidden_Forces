Dazzlement occurs when illumination exceeds the receiver's capacity to preserve distinction. The field becomes saturated, detail disappears, and vision fails because there is too much light rather than too little.

Physical glare demonstrates this directly. Psychic and spiritual dazzlement can occur when an experience is too intense, complex, or symbolically charged to be integrated.

The dazzled receiver may become certain precisely when perception is least reliable. Overwhelming affect creates the impression that interpretation must be immediate and absolute.

The [[ii-6|Fourfold Veil]] protects against dazzlement by translating intensity through successive functions. Greater light requires greater mediation, not merely greater openness.

Sometimes the proper response to luminosity is to narrow the aperture.

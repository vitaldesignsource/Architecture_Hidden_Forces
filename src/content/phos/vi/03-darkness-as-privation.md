Privation is the absence of something that ought to be present for a being or process to fulfil its proper capacity.

Darkness becomes privative when necessary illumination, understanding, vitality, orientation, or relationship has been lost or withheld. A person deprived of knowledge required for a responsible decision inhabits an epistemic darkness. A community denied truthful information is placed within manufactured obscurity.

Privative darkness differs from primordial darkness. Primordial darkness precedes disclosure; privation follows the absence, loss, or obstruction of a needed good.

## Why the distinction matters

The distinction is morally important. Not every darkness needs to be conquered, but privative darkness may require restoration. Where light is necessary for life, truth, development, or justice, its absence becomes a genuine deprivation.

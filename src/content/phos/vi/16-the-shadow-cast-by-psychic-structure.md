The psyche does not receive reality neutrally. Memory, desire, fear, shame, attachment, identity, and expectation stand within the field of perception and cast shadows.

Psychic shadow consists partly of qualities excluded from conscious identity. Because they are unrecognised, they influence interpretation indirectly through projection, compulsion, attraction, and aversion.

The person may perceive the shadow upon the world without recognising the interior structure interrupting the light. What is rejected within is repeatedly encountered as though it existed only outside.

Psychic illumination does not eliminate shadow by force. It makes the obstructing structure visible and restores conscious relationship with what has been excluded.

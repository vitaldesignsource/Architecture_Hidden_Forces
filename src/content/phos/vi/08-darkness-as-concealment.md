Concealment occurs when something present is prevented from appearing fully. The source may be covered, the medium clouded, the form hidden, or the receiver diverted.

Concealment can protect, deceive, preserve, prepare, or control. Sacred concealment may protect a mystery from trivialisation. Personal privacy protects necessary interiority. Strategic concealment may prevent harm. Deceptive concealment hides information so that others cannot act freely or understand truthfully.

Darkness as concealment must therefore be judged through purpose and relation. What is being hidden, from whom, by whom, and with what consequence?

A veil is not automatically a lie. It becomes false when it claims that nothing lies behind it or manipulates the receiver's ignorance for illegitimate ends.

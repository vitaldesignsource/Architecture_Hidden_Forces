The Night-Sea Journey is the mythic pattern of descent beneath the visible world. The solar hero, consciousness, or organising principle passes through darkness, water, danger, dissolution, and the realm of the dead before re-emerging transformed.

The sea represents depth, fluidity, memory, unconscious life, and the loss of ordinary boundaries. Night removes familiar orientation. Together they create a passage in which surface identity can no longer govern.

The journey is not valuable merely because it is painful. Its meaning lies in whether the traveller finds a deeper centre, recovers what was excluded, and returns with increased capacity for relation.

Descent without return remains captivity.

The Underworld is not simply a place of punishment. Across many symbolic systems it is the region of ancestors, buried memory, dissolved forms, hidden wealth, seeds, roots, death, and possible renewal.

Within The Architecture of Hidden Forces, the Underworld resembles both [[ii-27|the Crypt of Primordial Memory]] and [[ii-28|the Ossuary of Living Forms]]. It receives what has fallen out of visible organisation and preserves the consequences of what has been.

Regeneration becomes possible because dissolution releases components from a form that can no longer sustain them. Those components may enter new relationships.

The Underworld is a chamber of regeneration only when death is followed by reorganisation. Otherwise, it remains an archive of unresolved descent.

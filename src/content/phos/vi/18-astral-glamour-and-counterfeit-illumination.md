Astral glamour is psychic luminosity organised around attraction, fascination, fear, desire, or self-importance. It captures attention by producing the affect of revelation.

Glamour can be beautiful, emotionally overwhelming, symbolically complex, and internally coherent. Its danger lies precisely in its luminosity. The receiver mistakes vividness for truth and intensity for authority.

Counterfeit illumination often borrows genuine symbols, teachings, or experiences while redirecting them toward inflation, dependency, spectacle, or control. It may tell the personality that it has become the source of the light it only partially reflects.

Astral glamour is overcome through grounding, ethical consequence, comparative examination, humility, and sustained observation after the brilliance has passed.

The Black Sun is a symbol with several historical meanings. Within this system it is used specifically in its alchemical and metaphysical sense and should be distinguished from modern political appropriations.

It represents solar power concealed within darkness, the withdrawal of outward radiance, or an inner centre continuing to operate beneath apparent extinction. The Sun has not ceased to exist; its light has entered an invisible phase.

The Black Sun may appear when conscious identity loses access to its former source of vitality. What once gave orientation disappears, forcing a descent toward a deeper and less familiar centre.

It is not automatically evil or destructive. It is the hidden solar principle encountered before renewed radiance becomes possible.

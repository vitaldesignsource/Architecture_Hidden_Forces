Without contrast, many forms cannot be perceived. Shadow reveals contour, depth, position, distance, surface, and the direction of illumination.

Metaphysically, shadow gives consciousness a boundary against which recognition becomes possible. Difference permits relation. Limitation permits form. Rest permits activity to renew itself.

A life without shadow would not be a life of complete truth. It would lack privacy, incubation, ambiguity, mourning, silence, and the depth produced by things not immediately available.

Necessary shadow becomes harmful only when it is denied, absolutised, or used to conceal what must be brought into responsible awareness.

Light and shadow together reveal the architecture of form.

The Midnight Sun has a literal physical meaning in polar regions, where the Sun remains visible through the night during part of the year. Symbolically, it is the solar presence discovered at the deepest point of darkness.

Unlike ordinary daylight, the Midnight Sun does not abolish night. It appears within night, revealing that orientation can persist when familiar illumination has disappeared.

Psychologically, it is the centre that remains during grief, initiation, dissolution, or descent. Spiritually, it signifies a source not dependent upon external confirmation.

The Midnight Sun is not the return to former brightness. It is the discovery of a more inward and durable luminosity.

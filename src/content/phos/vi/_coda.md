## The doctrine of luminous darkness

Darkness may be an absence of sensible light, a deprivation of necessary illumination, a protective veil, an unmanifest potential, a psychic obstruction, an alchemical dissolution, or the obscurity produced by transcendent excess.

Shadow is the geometry produced when light encounters form. Opacity gives boundary. Turbidity reveals the condition of the medium. Absorption converts light into hidden activity. Psychic structure casts projections. Glamour manufactures false radiance. Dazzlement conceals through excess.

## Discernment, not extermination

The work is therefore not the extermination of darkness. It is the discernment of darkness.

Privative darkness must be illuminated. Generative darkness must be protected. Psychic shadow must be integrated. Deceptive concealment must be exposed. Apophatic darkness must be approached with humility.

> The luminous whole is not achieved when darkness disappears, but when every form of darkness is brought into right relation with light, life, truth, boundary, and becoming.

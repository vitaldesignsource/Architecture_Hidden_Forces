Opacity is the condition in which a material or medium prevents visible light from passing through it. Resistance is the broader capacity of a form to limit, redirect, absorb, or oppose a current.

Without resistance, light could not produce contrast, boundary, reflection, or visible structure. A completely unresisting field would offer nothing upon which illumination could become articulated.

Resistance becomes problematic only when it no longer serves form, protection, or meaningful differentiation. Excessive opacity prevents communication and seals the vessel against renewal.

Within the psyche, resistance may protect against overwhelming material or defend an outdated pattern. It must be understood before it is broken.

Opacity can be wall, shield, surface, prison, or foundation. Its meaning depends upon the relation it preserves.

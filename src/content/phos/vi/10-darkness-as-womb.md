The womb is the archetypal architecture of generative darkness. It is enclosed but not empty, hidden but intensely active, bounded but oriented toward future emergence.

Womb-darkness contains, nourishes, regulates, and protects. It gives developing form a proportionate environment in which differentiation can occur without direct exposure to the outer world.

Morphaithēr can assume a womb-like function when it gathers formative relations around an emerging possibility. The [[ii-6|Fourfold Veil]] regulates this gestation: Warmth awakens, Light differentiates, Tone orders, and Life integrates.

The womb should not be reduced to a sentimental image. Gestation involves vulnerability, limitation, risk, dependence, and transformation.

Darkness becomes womb when enclosure serves becoming.

Shadow integration does not mean acting out every forbidden impulse, declaring every darkness sacred, or allowing destructive forces unrestricted expression. It means recognising excluded contents, understanding their origins and functions, and restoring them to conscious relation.

Fear may conceal sensitivity. Anger may conceal violated boundary. Grief may conceal enduring love. Envy may disclose an undeveloped capacity. Desire may carry both genuine longing and distorted compensation.

## How the work proceeds

Illumination separates these elements so they can be reordered. Tone establishes proportion. Life reintegrates what can serve the whole. Necessary boundaries are preserved around what remains destructive.

The shadow is transformed when its captive force is released from unconscious form and given a more truthful mode of participation.

The luminous whole is not a world without darkness. It is an architecture in which light, shadow, concealment, latency, and mystery occupy their rightful relations.

Spiritual blindness is the incapacity or refusal to recognise morally and metaphysically significant relations even when evidence of them is present.

It may arise through fear, pride, ideology, resentment, habit, self-interest, trauma, or attachment to an identity that would be threatened by recognition. It is not necessarily a lack of intelligence. Intelligent persons can construct sophisticated defences against what they do not wish to see.

## In full brightness

Spiritual blindness can occur in full brightness. A person may speak constantly of illumination while remaining unable to perceive the consequences of their actions.

Its opposite is not visionary spectacle but truthful receptivity: the willingness to see, be corrected, and enter responsible relation with what has been revealed.

A shadow forms when light is interrupted by an object or boundary. It is not a substance projected by the object but a region receiving less illumination because the object stands in the path of the light.

Every embodied form casts some kind of shadow because form occupies a position, establishes a boundary, and excludes other possibilities.

## The consequence of limitation

Metaphysically, shadow is the consequence of limitation. To become one determinate thing is not to become every possible thing. Every choice illuminates one path while leaving others unchosen.

Shadow is therefore not necessarily corruption. It is evidence that light has encountered form. The shape of the shadow reveals the relationship among source, object, surface, and angle.

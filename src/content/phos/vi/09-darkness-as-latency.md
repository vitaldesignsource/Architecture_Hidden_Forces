Latency is the condition in which a capacity exists without being actively expressed.

A latent image, memory, talent, pattern, or force may remain invisible until the correct stimulus, medium, relation, or developmental stage allows it to emerge. Its darkness is the darkness of unactivated possibility.

Latent light has not necessarily been suppressed. It may simply lack the conditions required for manifestation. The source, medium, vessel, and receiver have not yet entered the needed relationship.

Latency teaches patience. Not every absence should be treated as failure, and not every hidden potential should be forced into immediate expression.

The latent becomes luminous when possibility acquires timing, relation, and form.

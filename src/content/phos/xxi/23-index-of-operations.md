This index gathers what light does: illuminate, reveal, manifest, differentiate, form, animate, vivify, orient, communicate, purify, consecrate, heal, protect, expose, judge, awaken, inspire, integrate, transfigure, dissolve, imprint, liberate, withdraw, and return.

## Agents, receivers, media, risks

Each operation connects to its agents, receivers, media, risks, and consequences. An operation without a named receiver is only half described, because what light does depends on what receives it.

Purification can heal or become destructive excess. Exposure can reveal truth or violate necessary privacy. Dissolution can liberate captive light or destroy a vessel before its contents can be integrated — the danger [[xiii-21|the entry on dissolution]] holds open rather than resolves.

## Governed by right relation

The index therefore shows that luminous operations are governed by right relation. The same operation, at the same intensity, is beneficial in one relation and harmful in another, and the difference lies in capacity, consent, timing, and the state of the vessel rather than in the operation itself.

No operation is beneficial merely because it is described as light. This is the practical form of [[ii-25|light and the Law of Right Relation]], and the reason the index records risks in the same column as effects.

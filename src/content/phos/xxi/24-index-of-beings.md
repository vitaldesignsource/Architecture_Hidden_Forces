The Index of Beings gathers gods, angels, archangels, daimons, ancestors, saints, sages, planetary intelligences, the luminous dead, astral beings, theophanic figures, and counterfeit forms.

## How an order is inferred

Entries describe how a being's order is inferred through the quality of its manifestation, its intelligence, stability, symbolic coherence, mode of communication, ethical effect, and relationship to the receiver. Appearance alone is insufficient. A brilliant figure may be exalted, imaginal, projected, mixed, deceptive, or misunderstood — the taxonomy [[xii-20|how a being reveals its order through the quality of its light]] works out in detail, and [[xii-22|false lights, masks, and imitative forms]] guards.

Brightness is the least informative of these signs, and the one most often mistaken for the others.

## Three things that must not be merged

The index distinguishes beings regarded as real within the system from the historical claims made about them in particular traditions. A being the Architecture treats as real may nevertheless have been described very differently by the tradition from which its name is borrowed, and the index records both without collapsing either.

It also preserves the distinction between a being, the image through which it appears, and the interpretation produced by the human receiver. These three are routinely merged in reports, and almost every confusion in the literature of visionary encounter can be traced to the merger.

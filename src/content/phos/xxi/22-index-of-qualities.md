The Index of Qualities gathers intensity, magnitude, clarity, purity, transparency, coherence, unity, stability, directionality, density, subtlety, temperature, colour, rhythm, penetration, diffusiveness, vitality, intelligence, affect, and truthfulness.

## One quality across the levels

Each quality links to physical, phenomenological, symbolic, etheric, astral, and spiritual examples. *Intensity*, for example, connects photometric brightness, visionary brilliance, ritual force, psychic excitement, and luminous saturation — together with the warning that magnitude does not establish spiritual rank, which [[xx-10|distinguishing radiance from excitement]] states in its practical form.

That warning is the reason the index exists in this shape. A quality gathered across levels shows immediately that its measures do not travel: a lumen count says nothing about coherence, and a vision's brilliance says nothing about its truthfulness.

## Qualities in combination

Qualities are also searchable in combinations. Cold crystalline light differs from warm diffuse light. A coherent golden radiance differs from an unstable red scintillation.

The real language of luminous experience arises through these combinations. A single quality name is nearly always too coarse to record what was actually seen, and a journal that reaches for one adjective has usually lost the observation. The [Encyclopaedia of Luminous Qualities](/phos/iv) treats each of the fifty-nine qualities in its own entry.

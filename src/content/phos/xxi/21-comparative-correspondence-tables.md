The tables compare terms, levels, colours, elements, beings, planets, ethers, tattvas, and operations while clearly indicating the kind of correspondence being proposed.

## Six kinds of correspondence

A correspondence might be historical, structural, functional, symbolic, experiential, or native to the Architecture of Hidden Forces. These must not be presented as equally certain.

Tejas and fire possess a traditional relationship, attested within the elemental systems that name both. Tejas and a modern scientific category possess only partial analogy, reached by resemblance rather than by descent. Biophotons and the etheric body may be investigated relationally, but they are not to be identified automatically — the discipline [[ix-17|biophotons: evidence, claims, and limitations]] observes.

## What each row must carry

Each table includes columns for tradition, source, original term, literal meaning, extended meaning, proposed relation, strength of correspondence, and cautions.

A row that cannot fill the source column is a row asserting a relation without a witness, and the empty cell is the finding. This makes the tables instruments of disciplined comparison instead of machines for producing superficial equivalence — the difference [[xviii-22|true correspondences and false equivalences]] exists to hold.
